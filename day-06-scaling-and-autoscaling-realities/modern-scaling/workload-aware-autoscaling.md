# Workload-Aware Autoscaling

Modern autoscaling increasingly focuses on:
- workload behavior
instead of:
- infrastructure utilization alone.

This improves scaling accuracy significantly.

---

# Why Workload Awareness Matters

CPU and memory frequently fail to represent:
- real operational pressure.

Applications may experience:
- queue buildup
- concurrency saturation
- dependency latency
- or request overload

while infrastructure metrics still appear healthy.

---

# Better Scaling Signals

Workload-aware scaling may use:
- request concurrency
- queue depth
- latency
- requests per second
- or business metrics

to scale systems more accurately.

---

# Operational Benefits

Workload-aware autoscaling improves:
- scaling responsiveness
- user-facing reliability
- and operational stability.

---

# Key Insight

Modern autoscaling works best when scaling decisions reflect real workload pressure instead of raw infrastructure usage alone.
