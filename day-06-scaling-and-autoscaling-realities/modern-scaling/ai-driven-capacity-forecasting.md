# AI-Driven Capacity Forecasting

Large-scale platforms increasingly use:
- forecasting systems
- and machine learning models

to predict:
- infrastructure demand
- traffic spikes
- and operational saturation.

---

# Why Forecasting Matters

Distributed systems scale slower than:
- traffic changes occur.

Forecasting attempts to prepare:
- infrastructure
- workloads
- and operational capacity

before overload begins.

---

# Forecasting Inputs

Capacity forecasting may analyze:
- historical traffic
- workload trends
- seasonal patterns
- user behavior
- deployment schedules
- and business events.

---

# Operational Benefits

Forecasting improves:
- infrastructure planning
- scaling responsiveness
- and operational reliability

while reducing:
- emergency scaling events.

---

# Operational Tradeoffs

Forecasting systems still require:
- accurate telemetry
- continuous tuning
- and operational validation.

Incorrect predictions may create:
- waste
- or underprovisioning.

---

# Key Insight

Modern platforms increasingly predict operational demand instead of reacting only after saturation appears.
