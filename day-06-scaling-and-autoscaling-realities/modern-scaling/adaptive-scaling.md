# Adaptive Scaling

Adaptive scaling continuously adjusts:
- scaling behavior
- thresholds
- and operational policies

based on:
- workload characteristics
- traffic patterns
- and system behavior.

---

# Why Adaptive Scaling Matters

Static scaling thresholds frequently fail under:
- unpredictable workloads
- burst traffic
- or changing operational conditions.

Adaptive systems attempt to respond more intelligently.

---

# Adaptive Behavior

Adaptive scaling may dynamically modify:
- scaling aggressiveness
- cooldown periods
- stabilization windows
- or scaling thresholds

based on:
- live operational feedback.

---

# Operational Benefits

Adaptive systems improve:
- scaling stability
- responsiveness
- and infrastructure efficiency

during variable production traffic.

---

# Tradeoffs

Adaptive systems introduce:
- operational complexity
- tuning challenges
- and observability requirements.

---

# Key Insight

Adaptive scaling continuously adjusts operational behavior instead of relying on fixed autoscaling rules.
