# GitOps Is Operational Consistency

GitOps is often incorrectly understood as:
- deployment automation
- or synchronization tooling

The real value of GitOps comes from continuously maintaining operational consistency across distributed systems.

---

Distributed systems continuously drift through:
- manual mutations
- failed updates
- infrastructure instability
- and operational divergence

GitOps continuously attempts restoration toward intended operational behavior.

---

Git becomes:
- the operational contract
- desired-state reference
- and centralized source of truth

for:
- infrastructure
- applications
- and platform configuration

inside Kubernetes environments.

---

Continuous reconciliation improves:
- predictability
- auditability
- rollback safety
- and operational convergence

inside distributed systems.

---

# Key Insight

GitOps is fundamentally a reconciliation-driven operational consistency model for distributed systems.
