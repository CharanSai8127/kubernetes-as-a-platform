# Reconciliation Over Deployment

Modern Kubernetes platforms operate through:
- controllers
- reconciliation loops
- and convergence behavior

This makes reconciliation significantly more important than one-time deployment execution.

---

Deployment is:
- an event

Reconciliation is:
- continuous operational correction

inside distributed systems.

---

Distributed environments continuously experience:
- failures
- scaling changes
- infrastructure mutations
- and operational drift

Without reconciliation:
- consistency degrades rapidly
- and operational recovery becomes unpredictable.

---

GitOps extends Kubernetes reconciliation principles toward:
- infrastructure
- applications
- operational coordination
- and platform management

through continuous convergence.

---

# Key Insight

Reliable distributed systems depend more heavily on continuous reconciliation than deployment execution itself.
