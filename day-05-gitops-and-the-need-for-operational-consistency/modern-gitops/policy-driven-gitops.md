# Policy-Driven GitOps

Modern platforms frequently require:
- security enforcement
- operational governance
- and standardized deployment controls

Policy-driven GitOps helps integrate these requirements directly into reconciliation workflows.

---

# Why Policies Matter

Without operational policies:
- inconsistent deployments
- security drift
- and invalid operational behavior

become increasingly common inside distributed systems.

Policies improve:
- predictability
- standardization
- and operational safety.

---

# GitOps Perspective

GitOps centralizes:
- infrastructure definitions
- operational workflows
- and deployment state

Policies can continuously validate:
- operational correctness
- security requirements
- and deployment standards

during reconciliation.

---

# Kubernetes Relationship

Kubernetes already supports:
- admission control
- validation
- and policy enforcement

GitOps extends these operational controls toward:
- continuous reconciliation
- and declarative governance.

---

# Reliability Engineering

Policy-driven convergence improves:
- operational consistency
- security reliability
- and recovery predictability

inside distributed environments.

---

# Key Insight

Policy-driven GitOps improves operational reliability by continuously validating convergence against standardized operational rules.
