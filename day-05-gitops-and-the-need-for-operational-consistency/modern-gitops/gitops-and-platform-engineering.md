# GitOps and Platform Engineering

Platform engineering focuses heavily on:
- operational consistency
- standardized workflows
- and scalable infrastructure management

GitOps naturally aligns with these operational goals.

---

# Why GitOps Fits Platform Engineering

Distributed systems continuously evolve through:
- deployments
- scaling
- infrastructure changes
- and operational updates

GitOps improves platform management through:
- centralized desired state
- reconciliation-driven convergence
- and declarative operational control.

---

# Operational Consistency

Platform teams require:
- repeatable environments
- predictable recovery
- operational visibility
- and scalable management

GitOps improves these capabilities significantly through:
- continuous reconciliation
- drift correction
- and centralized operational workflows.
