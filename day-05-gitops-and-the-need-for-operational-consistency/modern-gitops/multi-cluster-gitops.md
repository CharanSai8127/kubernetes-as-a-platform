# Multi-Cluster GitOps

Modern platforms frequently operate across:
- multiple Kubernetes clusters
- regions
- environments
- and operational domains

Managing these environments consistently becomes increasingly difficult at scale.

---

# Why Multi-Cluster Management Matters

Without centralized reconciliation:
- environments diverge
- operational visibility decreases
- and recovery coordination becomes difficult

Multi-cluster GitOps improves:
- operational consistency
- centralized visibility
- and convergence management

across distributed Kubernetes environments.

---

# GitOps Perspective

GitOps helps coordinate:
- cluster configuration
- platform services
- infrastructure layers
- and application deployments

through:
- centralized desired state
- and continuous reconciliation.

---

# Operational Reliability

Multi-cluster reconciliation improves:
- disaster recovery coordination
- deployment predictability
- and operational consistency

inside large distributed platforms.

---

# Platform Engineering

Platform teams can standardize:
- networking
- security
- observability
- and operational policies

across multiple clusters declaratively.

---

# Key Insight

GitOps improves operational consistency across multiple Kubernetes clusters through centralized reconciliation and convergence management.
