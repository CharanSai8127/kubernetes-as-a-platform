# Progressive Delivery

Distributed systems frequently require controlled rollout strategies to reduce operational risk during deployments.

Progressive delivery helps systems converge gradually instead of shifting operational state abruptly.

---

# Why Progressive Rollouts Matter

Large production systems may experience:
- hidden failures
- dependency instability
- latency propagation
- and operational regressions

during deployments.

Controlled rollout behavior improves:
- stability
- observability
- and recovery predictability.

---

# GitOps Relationship

GitOps continuously manages:
- desired state
- reconciliation behavior
- and deployment convergence

Progressive delivery extends this model toward:
- controlled rollout orchestration
- and operational safety.

---

# Operational Reliability

Gradual convergence improves:
- rollback coordination
- failure isolation
- and deployment confidence

inside distributed systems.

---

# Platform Engineering

Progressive delivery supports:
- safer production updates
- operational visibility
- and dependency-aware rollout management

inside Kubernetes environments.

---

# Key Insight

Progressive delivery improves reliability by reducing operational risk during distributed-system convergence.
