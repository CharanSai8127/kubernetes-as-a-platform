# ApplicationSets

Managing multiple environments manually becomes increasingly difficult as distributed systems scale.

ApplicationSets help automate:
- application generation
- environment discovery
- and operational consistency

across Kubernetes platforms.

---

# Why ApplicationSets Matter

Large environments often contain:
- multiple overlays
- clusters
- namespaces
- and deployment variations

Managing these independently creates:
- duplication
- configuration drift
- and operational inconsistency

ApplicationSets improve scalability through declarative generation models.

---

# Declarative Scaling

ApplicationSets dynamically generate Argo CD Applications using:
- generators
- templates
- and repository-driven discovery

This allows GitOps systems to scale operational management predictably.

---
