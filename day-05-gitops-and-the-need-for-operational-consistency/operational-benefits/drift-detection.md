# Drift Detection

Distributed systems continuously drift away from intended operational state.

Drift may occur through:
- manual changes
- failed updates
- runtime mutations
- or inconsistent operational workflows

Drift detection helps identify these inconsistencies continuously.

---

# Why Drift Matters

Operational drift creates:
- unpredictable behavior
- inconsistent recovery
- debugging difficulty
- and production instability

inside distributed environments.

Examples include:
- manual kubectl edits
- stale configurations
- or undeclared infrastructure changes.

---

# GitOps Perspective

GitOps continuously compares:
- desired Git state
against:
- actual cluster state

Whenever divergence occurs:
systems become:
- OutOfSync

This exposes operational inconsistency clearly.

---

# Continuous Correction

GitOps controllers continuously attempt:
- reconciliation
- convergence
- and restoration

toward intended operational state.

This transforms environments into:
- continuously self-correcting systems.

---

# Reliability Engineering

Drift detection improves:
- operational visibility
- recovery coordination
- and deployment predictability

during infrastructure changes.

---

# Key Insight

Distributed systems naturally drift.

GitOps continuously detects and corrects operational divergence through reconciliation.
