# Rollback and Auditability

Reliable distributed systems require:
- predictable recovery
- operational traceability
- and controlled change management

GitOps improves these capabilities significantly through version-controlled operational state.

---

# Rollback Safety

Operational recovery becomes significantly safer when:
- infrastructure
- applications
- and platform configuration

are managed declaratively through Git.

Previous operational states remain:
- versioned
- reproducible
- and recoverable.

---

# Why Rollback Matters

Distributed systems frequently experience:
- failed deployments
- configuration drift
- dependency issues
- and operational instability

Rollback mechanisms help restore:
- operational correctness
- consistency
- and stability

during failures.

---

# Auditability

Git history provides visibility into:
- operational changes
- ownership
- deployment history
- and configuration evolution

This improves:
- accountability
- debugging
- and operational transparency

inside distributed systems.

---

# Reliability Engineering

Auditability improves:
- recovery coordination
- incident analysis
- and operational trust

during production incidents.

---

# Key Insight

GitOps improves operational reliability by making system state versioned, traceable, recoverable, and continuously auditable.
