# Single Source of Truth

Distributed systems continuously evolve through:
- deployments
- infrastructure changes
- scaling events
- and operational updates

Without centralized state management:
- environments diverge
- recovery becomes inconsistent
- and operational visibility decreases significantly.

---

# Git as Operational Truth

GitOps uses Git as:
- the desired-state reference
- operational contract
- and centralized configuration source

for infrastructure and applications.

This creates a unified operational model across environments.

---

# Why Centralization Matters

Without centralized operational state:
- undocumented changes accumulate
- environments drift independently
- and debugging becomes significantly harder

A single source of truth improves:
- consistency
- traceability
- and operational predictability

inside distributed systems.

---

# Operational Visibility

Centralized state management improves:
- auditability
- ownership clarity
- rollback visibility
- and reconciliation tracking

during operational changes.

---

# Kubernetes Perspective

Kubernetes already relies on:
- declarative state
- controllers
- and convergence

GitOps extends this philosophy toward:
- infrastructure
- platform services
- and operational management.

---

# Reliability Engineering

Reliable systems depend heavily on:
- centralized operational state
- predictable convergence
- and consistent recovery workflows

during production instability.

---

# Key Insight

A centralized source of truth improves operational consistency by continuously aligning distributed systems toward declared intended state.
