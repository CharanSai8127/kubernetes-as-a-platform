# Consistency at Scale

Operational complexity increases rapidly as distributed systems scale.

Large environments frequently contain:
- multiple clusters
- overlays
- applications
- infrastructure layers
- and operational dependencies

Maintaining consistency manually becomes operationally unsustainable.

---

# Why Scale Introduces Complexity

As systems grow:
- environments diverge
- operational drift increases
- and recovery becomes unpredictable

Manual operational management introduces:
- inconsistency
- configuration duplication
- and deployment instability

inside Kubernetes platforms.

---

# GitOps Perspective

GitOps improves scalability through:
- declarative management
- centralized desired state
- and continuous reconciliation

Controllers continuously attempt convergence toward intended operational behavior.

---

# ApplicationSets and Scaling

ApplicationSets help scale:
- environment generation
- reconciliation management
- and operational consistency

across overlays and clusters.

This reduces:
- duplicated operational logic
- and environment inconsistency.

---

# Reliability Engineering

Consistency at scale improves:
- deployment predictability
- operational visibility
- and recovery coordination

inside distributed systems.

---

# Key Insight

GitOps enables distributed systems to scale operationally while preserving consistency through centralized reconciliation and convergence.
