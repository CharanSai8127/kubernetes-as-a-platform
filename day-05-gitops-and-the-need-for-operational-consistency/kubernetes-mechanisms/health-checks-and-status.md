# Health Checks and Status

Distributed systems require continuous visibility into:
- workload behavior
- operational readiness
- and convergence state

Health checks and reconciliation status help identify whether systems remain operationally correct.

---

# Kubernetes Perspective

Kubernetes exposes:
- readiness
- liveness
- and startup validation

to help determine workload operational state.

These mechanisms improve:
- recovery behavior
- workload stability
- and orchestration predictability.

---

# GitOps Relationship

GitOps systems expose:
- synchronization state
- health visibility
- and reconciliation status

to help engineers understand:
- operational convergence
- drift conditions
- and dependency readiness

inside distributed systems.

---

# Operational Correctness

A system may:
- synchronize successfully
while still:
- remaining operationally invalid

if:
- dependencies fail
- migrations remain incomplete
- or APIs become inconsistent.

Operational correctness depends on:
- dependency-safe convergence
- and valid system behavior.

---

# Reliability Engineering

Health visibility improves:
- debugging
- incident analysis
- and recovery coordination

during operational instability.

---

# Key Insight

Operational reliability depends on continuously understanding both reconciliation state and workload correctness across distributed systems.
