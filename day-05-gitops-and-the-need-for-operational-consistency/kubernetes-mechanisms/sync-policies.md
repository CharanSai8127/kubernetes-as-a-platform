# Sync Policies

GitOps systems continuously reconcile:
- desired state
- and actual cluster behavior

Sync policies help define:
- how reconciliation should occur
- and how operational convergence should be managed

inside distributed systems.

---

# Why Sync Policies Matter

Distributed environments frequently require:
- automated convergence
- controlled operational behavior
- and predictable recovery workflows

Without controlled synchronization:
- drift persists
- operational inconsistency increases
- and convergence becomes unpredictable.

---

# Automated Synchronization

Automated sync policies allow GitOps controllers to:
- continuously reconcile drift
- restore deleted resources
- and converge toward intended state

without requiring manual operational intervention.

---

# Self-Healing Behavior

GitOps systems can continuously:
- detect divergence
- identify drift
- and attempt restoration

toward operational consistency.

This creates:
- self-correcting operational environments.

---

# Controlled Reconciliation

Operational teams may still define:
- synchronization boundaries
- reconciliation timing
- and deployment safety controls

to preserve:
- stability
- predictability
- and operational coordination

during production workflows.

---

# Reliability Engineering

Controlled synchronization improves:
- operational consistency
- rollback predictability
- and convergence reliability

inside Kubernetes platforms.

---

# Key Insight

Sync policies help transform distributed systems into continuously converging operational environments.
