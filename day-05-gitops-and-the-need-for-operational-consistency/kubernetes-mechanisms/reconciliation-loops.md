# Reconciliation Loops

Kubernetes fundamentally operates through reconciliation loops.

Controllers continuously:
- observe system state
- compare desired behavior
- and attempt convergence

toward intended operational state.

---

# Continuous Convergence

Reconciliation is not:
- one-time execution
- or deployment automation

It is:
- continuous operational correction
- and convergence-driven management

inside distributed systems.

---

# Why Reconciliation Matters

Distributed systems continuously experience:
- workload failures
- scaling changes
- infrastructure instability
- and operational drift

Without reconciliation:
- systems diverge rapidly
- and operational consistency degrades.

---

# Kubernetes Perspective

Every major Kubernetes object depends on:
- controllers
- control loops
- and convergence behavior

Examples include:
- Deployments
- ReplicaSets
- StatefulSets
- Jobs
- and custom resources

inside the Kubernetes control plane.

---

# GitOps Relationship

GitOps extends Kubernetes reconciliation toward:
- infrastructure management
- application delivery
- and operational consistency

through continuous convergence between:
- Git state
- and cluster state.

---

# Reliability Engineering

Reliable systems depend heavily on:
- self-correcting operational behavior
- predictable convergence
- and continuous restoration

during failures and infrastructure mutations.

---

# Key Insight

Kubernetes reliability emerges from continuously running reconciliation loops that restore intended operational state predictably.
