# Sync Windows

Distributed systems frequently require controlled reconciliation timing.

Sync windows help define:
- when synchronization is allowed
- restricted
- or coordinated

inside GitOps platforms.

---

# Why Timing Matters

Continuous reconciliation is powerful,
but uncontrolled synchronization during:
- peak traffic
- maintenance periods
- or operational incidents

may increase instability.

Controlled synchronization improves:
- operational safety
- coordination
- and deployment predictability.

---

# Operational Coordination

Sync windows help teams coordinate:
- production changes
- infrastructure updates
- dependency sequencing
- and recovery workflows

across distributed systems.

---

# GitOps Perspective

GitOps continuously attempts convergence.

Sync windows provide operational boundaries around:
- when reconciliation should occur
- and how convergence should be coordinated safely.

---

# Reliability Engineering

Controlled reconciliation timing improves:
- rollout safety
- operational predictability
- and incident coordination

during production operations.

---

# Platform Engineering

Large platforms frequently require:
- change governance
- deployment coordination
- and operational safety controls

across multiple environments and operational layers.

---

# Key Insight

Continuous reconciliation becomes more operationally predictable when synchronization timing is coordinated safely.
