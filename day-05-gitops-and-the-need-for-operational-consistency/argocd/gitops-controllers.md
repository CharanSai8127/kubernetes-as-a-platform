# GitOps Controllers

GitOps controllers continuously reconcile distributed systems toward declared desired state.

They extend Kubernetes reconciliation principles into:
- infrastructure management
- application delivery
- and operational consistency

inside dynamic environments.

---

# Controller-Driven Operations

GitOps controllers continuously:
- observe Git repositories
- compare cluster state
- detect drift
- and attempt convergence

This creates:
- automated consistency correction
- operational predictability
- and self-healing behavior

inside Kubernetes platforms.

---

# Why Controllers Matter

Distributed systems continuously experience:
- configuration drift
- inconsistent updates
- manual mutations
- and operational divergence

Without reconciliation controllers:
- operational consistency degrades rapidly

Controllers continuously restore systems toward intended operational behavior.
