# Pull-Based Deployments

GitOps systems typically operate using pull-based reconciliation models.

Instead of external systems pushing deployments toward clusters,
controllers continuously pull desired state from Git repositories.

---

# Why Pull-Based Models Matter

Push-based deployment systems often introduce:
- credential sprawl
- inconsistent operational execution
- and reduced visibility into deployment state

Pull-based reconciliation improves:
- security
- operational consistency
- and centralized convergence management

inside distributed systems.

---

# Continuous Reconciliation

GitOps controllers continuously:
- monitor repositories
- detect changes
- compare cluster state
- and reconcile drift

This creates:
- automated convergence
- operational predictability
- and self-correcting environments

through reconciliation loops.

---

# Kubernetes Perspective

Kubernetes already operates through:
- reconciliation
- controllers
- and convergence behavior

Pull-based GitOps models align naturally with Kubernetes operational philosophy.

---

# Operational Visibility

Pull-based reconciliation improves:
- auditability
- deployment visibility
- and operational traceability

because desired state remains centralized inside version-controlled repositories.

---

# Reliability Engineering

Reliable systems prioritize:
- predictable convergence
- centralized operational management
- and controlled reconciliation behavior

during infrastructure and application updates.

---

# Key Insight

Pull-based reconciliation improves operational consistency by continuously converging distributed systems toward centralized desired state.
