# Declarative and Immutable Systems

Modern infrastructure increasingly moves toward:
- declarative management
- immutable operations
- and reconciliation-driven consistency

instead of imperative operational changes.

---

# Declarative Systems

Declarative systems define:
- the intended final state
rather than:
- the exact execution procedure

This improves:
- operational clarity
- reproducibility
- and consistency

inside distributed systems.

---

# Imperative vs Declarative

Imperative systems focus on:
- step-by-step operational execution

Declarative systems focus on:
- continuously converging toward intended state

This reduces:
- operational variability
- human inconsistency
- and undocumented changes

during infrastructure management.

---

# Immutability

Immutable operational models avoid:
- modifying running systems directly

Instead:
- new versions replace old state predictably

This improves:
- rollback safety
- auditability
- recovery consistency
- and operational reliability

---

# Kubernetes Perspective

Kubernetes strongly encourages:
- declarative manifests
- immutable infrastructure patterns
- and controller-driven reconciliation

GitOps extends these operational principles into platform management.

---

# Reliability Engineering

Distributed systems continuously drift through:
- manual changes
- runtime mutations
- and inconsistent operational behavior

Declarative and immutable systems reduce this instability significantly.

---

# Key Insight

Declarative and immutable systems improve operational reliability by reducing variability and continuously restoring intended system state.
