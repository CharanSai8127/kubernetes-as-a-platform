# Operational Consistency

Distributed systems continuously change operational behavior through:
- deployments
- scaling
- infrastructure updates
- and dependency coordination

Maintaining operational consistency becomes increasingly difficult at scale.

---

# Why Consistency Matters

Operational inconsistency creates:
- unpredictable behavior
- debugging complexity
- failed recovery
- and production instability

Examples include:
- inconsistent environments
- stale configurations
- partial rollouts
- and undocumented manual changes

---

# GitOps Perspective

GitOps improves consistency by:
- centralizing desired state
- continuously reconciling drift
- and standardizing operational management

This improves:
- repeatability
- auditability
- and recovery coordination

inside distributed systems.

---

# Kubernetes Relationship

Kubernetes already operates using:
- desired-state convergence
- reconciliation loops
- and controller-driven management

GitOps extends this consistency model toward:
- applications
- infrastructure
- and platform operations

---

# Dependency Coordination

Operational consistency also requires:
- dependency ordering
- migration coordination
- readiness validation
- and operational sequencing

Successful deployment alone does not guarantee operational correctness.

---

# Reliability Engineering

Reliable systems depend heavily on:
- predictable operational behavior
- convergence toward intended state
- and continuous drift correction

during production conditions.

---

# Key Insight

GitOps improves reliability by continuously maintaining operational consistency across distributed systems.
