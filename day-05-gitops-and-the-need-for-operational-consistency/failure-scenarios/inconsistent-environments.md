# Inconsistent Environments

Distributed systems frequently operate across:
- development
- staging
- production
- and regional environments

Without declarative management:
these environments gradually diverge operationally.

---

# Why Environment Drift Happens

Environment inconsistency often occurs through:
- manual configuration changes
- duplicated manifests
- partial updates
- and operational shortcuts

Over time:
- behaviors differ
- debugging becomes unreliable
- and recovery workflows fail unpredictably.

---

# Operational Impact

Inconsistent environments create:
- deployment surprises
- invalid assumptions
- failed rollouts
- and operational instability

inside distributed systems.

Problems that appear only in production become increasingly difficult to reproduce safely.

---

# GitOps Perspective

GitOps improves consistency through:
- declarative overlays
- centralized desired state
- and continuous reconciliation

Environment behavior becomes:
- versioned
- repeatable
- and convergent.

---

# Reliability Engineering

Reliable platforms require:
- predictable operational behavior
- reproducible environments
- and controlled convergence

during deployments and recovery workflows.

---

# Key Insight

Operational reliability decreases rapidly when distributed environments drift away from consistent declarative state.
