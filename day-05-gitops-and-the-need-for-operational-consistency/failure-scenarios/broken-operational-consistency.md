# Broken Operational Consistency

Distributed systems continuously evolve through:
- deployments
- scaling
- infrastructure updates
- and operational mutations

Without continuous reconciliation:
operational consistency gradually degrades.

---

# What Broken Consistency Looks Like

Examples include:
- stale configurations
- inconsistent overlays
- partial rollbacks
- manual infrastructure changes
- and mismatched operational state

These inconsistencies create:
- unpredictable behavior
- debugging difficulty
- and failed recovery workflows.

---

# Why It Matters

Operational inconsistency causes:
- dependency mismatches
- rollout instability
- hidden production drift
- and unreliable system behavior

inside Kubernetes environments.

Distributed systems become increasingly difficult to reason about operationally.

---

# GitOps Perspective

GitOps continuously attempts:
- drift correction
- convergence
- and restoration

toward intended operational behavior.

This improves:
- predictability
- consistency
- and operational reliability

inside distributed systems.

---

# Reliability Engineering

Reliable systems depend heavily on:
- controlled operational state
- centralized reconciliation
- and continuous convergence

during production operations.

---

# Key Insight

Distributed systems become operationally unstable when consistency stops being continuously reconciled.
