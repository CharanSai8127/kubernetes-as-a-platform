# Platform Engineering Is Not Kubernetes

One of the most common misconceptions is:

Platform Engineering = Kubernetes.

This is incorrect.

---

# Why This Misconception Exists

Many organizations build their platforms on top of:

- Kubernetes

which creates the impression that:

- Kubernetes is the platform.

---

# The Reality

Kubernetes is:

- a platform component.

Platform Engineering includes:

- developer experience
- security
- observability
- governance
- automation
- and self-service capabilities.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

A platform may use Kubernetes.

A platform is not Kubernetes.

---

# Key Insight

Kubernetes is a tool. Platform Engineering is a discipline.
