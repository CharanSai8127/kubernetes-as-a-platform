# Platform Engineering Is Not DevOps

Platform Engineering and DevOps are related,
but they are not the same thing.

---

# What DevOps Is

DevOps focuses on:

- collaboration
- automation
- feedback loops
- and shared responsibility.

DevOps is primarily:

- a culture
- and a set of practices.

---

# What Platform Engineering Is

Platform Engineering focuses on:

- building reusable platforms

that apply DevOps principles at scale.

---

# The Relationship

DevOps provides:

- the philosophy.

Platform Engineering provides:

- the implementation model.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Platform Engineering extends DevOps rather than replacing it.

---

# Key Insight

DevOps describes how teams should work together.

Platform Engineering provides the platform that enables it.
