# Platform Engineering Does Not Remove Operations

Platform Engineering does not eliminate:

- operations work.

Instead it changes:

- how operations are delivered.

---

# The Misconception

Some organizations assume platforms eliminate:

- operational responsibility.

In reality,
platforms still require:

- maintenance
- upgrades
- security
- governance
- and reliability management.

---

# The Difference

Traditional Operations:

Many teams manage operations independently.

Platform Engineering:

A dedicated platform team provides operational capabilities as shared services.

---

# Platform Benefits

This approach improves:

- consistency
- efficiency
- and scalability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Operations do not disappear.

Operations become a platform capability.

---

# Key Insight

Platform Engineering changes the operating model, not the need for operations.
