# Self Service Does Not Mean No Governance

Another common misconception is:

Self-service means unrestricted access.

This is incorrect.

---

# Why Governance Still Matters

Shared platforms require:

- security
- compliance
- reliability
- and operational consistency.

Without governance,
self-service can become:

- platform chaos.

---

# Platform Goal

Provide:

Developer Freedom

WITH

Guardrails

Not:

Developer Freedom

WITHOUT

Controls.

---

# Common Guardrails

Examples include:

- RBAC
- quotas
- policy enforcement
- admission controls
- and security requirements.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Self-service should accelerate delivery while maintaining platform safety.

---

# Key Insight

The best platforms combine autonomy with governance.
