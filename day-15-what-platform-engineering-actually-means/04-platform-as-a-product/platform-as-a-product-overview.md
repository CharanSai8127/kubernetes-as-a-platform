# Platform As A Product Overview

Platform Engineering treats the platform as:
- a product

rather than:
- an internal infrastructure project.

The users of the platform become:
- developers
- application teams
- and engineering teams.

---

# Why Platform As A Product Matters

Infrastructure teams traditionally focus on:
- building systems.

Platform teams focus on:
- delivering value to platform users.

This changes the mindset from:

Building Infrastructure

to

Serving Customers.

---

# Product Thinking

Platform teams should understand:

- user needs
- user pain points
- adoption challenges
- and developer workflows.

---

# Platform Outcomes

A successful platform becomes:

- useful
- reliable
- easy to consume
- and widely adopted.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

The platform should solve developer problems rather than create additional complexity.

---

# Key Insight

If nobody wants to use the platform, it is not a successful product.
