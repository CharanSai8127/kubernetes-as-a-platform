# Platform Users As Customers

The primary customers of a platform are:

- developers
- application teams
- and engineering teams.

Their experience determines:
- platform success.

---

# Why Customer Thinking Matters

Developers have choices.

If the platform is:

- difficult
- unreliable
- or restrictive

they may seek alternative solutions.

---

# Platform Responsibilities

Platform teams should optimize for:

- usability
- reliability
- consistency
- and developer productivity.

---

# User Focus Areas

Platform teams should understand:

- developer workflows
- operational challenges
- deployment requirements
- and platform adoption barriers.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Platform adoption is earned through value rather than enforcement.

---

# Key Insight

Developers are platform users, and users determine product success.
