# Platform Feedback Loops

Platforms should continuously evolve based on:
- user feedback.

Feedback helps platform teams understand:

- adoption challenges
- usability issues
- and improvement opportunities.

---

# Why Feedback Matters

Platform teams often build:

- capabilities
- services
- and workflows.

Only users can determine whether those solutions are effective.

---

# Common Feedback Sources

Examples include:

- developer surveys
- platform usage metrics
- incident reviews
- and support requests.

---

# Platform Benefits

Feedback loops improve:

- platform quality
- developer satisfaction
- and adoption.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Successful platforms evolve alongside their users.

---

# Key Insight

A platform should be continuously improved rather than considered complete.
