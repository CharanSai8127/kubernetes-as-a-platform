# Platform Adoption

A platform only creates value when:
- teams actively use it.

Adoption is one of the most important indicators of:
- platform success.

---

# Why Adoption Matters

Organizations often invest heavily in:

- infrastructure
- automation
- and platform tooling.

Without adoption,
those investments provide:
- limited value.

---

# Adoption Drivers

Developers adopt platforms when they provide:

- simplicity
- speed
- reliability
- and operational benefits.

---

# Adoption Challenges

Poor adoption commonly results from:

- excessive complexity
- poor documentation
- unreliable workflows
- or restrictive processes.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Platform teams should continuously improve the user experience.

---

# Key Insight

The most technically advanced platform may still fail if nobody wants to use it.
