# Why Platform Engineering Emerged

Modern systems have become increasingly complex.

Organizations now operate:
- cloud infrastructure
- Kubernetes platforms
- CI/CD systems
- observability stacks
- security controls
- and governance frameworks.

---

# The Challenge

Application teams often become responsible for:
- solving infrastructure problems

instead of:

- delivering business value.

---

# The Result

Teams duplicate:
- tooling
- deployment patterns
- operational practices
- and infrastructure solutions.

This creates:
- inefficiency
- inconsistency
- and operational risk.

---

# Platform Engineering Response

Platform engineering centralizes:
- common capabilities

and provides them through:
- reusable self-service platforms.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Platform teams solve platform problems so application teams can solve business problems.

---

# Key Insight

Platform engineering emerged to reduce cognitive load and operational duplication.
