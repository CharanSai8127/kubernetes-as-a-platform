# Platform Engineering Overview

Platform engineering is the discipline of building:
- reusable internal platforms

that enable developers to safely consume:
- infrastructure
- networking
- security
- observability
- deployment capabilities
- and governance.

---

# Why Platform Engineering Exists

As organizations grow,
application teams spend increasing amounts of time managing:
- infrastructure
- deployment tooling
- security requirements
- operational processes
- and platform integrations.

This creates:
- operational overhead
- duplicated effort
- and inconsistent practices.

---

# The Platform Engineering Goal

Platform engineering aims to provide:

Developer Self-Service

WITH

Operational Safety

Governance

Security

And

Consistency.

---

# Platform As An Enabler

Platform teams do not build:
- business applications.

Platform teams build:
- capabilities
that application teams consume.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Platform engineering focuses on:
- enabling developers
while maintaining:
- reliability
- scalability
- security
- and operational consistency.

---

# Key Insight

Platform engineering is the discipline of turning infrastructure capabilities into reusable developer products.
