# Platform Engineering Principles

Platform engineering is built upon:
- standardization
- automation
- self-service
- and operational excellence.

---

# Core Principles

Successful platforms provide:

- consistency
- repeatability
- reliability
- security
- and developer productivity.

---

# Platform Design Goals

A platform should:

- simplify complexity
- reduce operational burden
- improve developer experience
- and increase delivery speed.

---

# Platform Benefits

Well-designed platforms improve:

- scalability
- governance
- security
- operational efficiency
- and platform adoption.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

A platform should become:
- the preferred way to build and deploy applications.

---

# Key Insight

The easiest path should also be the safest and most reliable path.
