# Platform Capabilities Integration

Successful platforms integrate:

- deployment
- security
- networking
- observability
- and governance.

These capabilities should work together rather than operate independently.

# Why Integration Matters

Disconnected tooling creates:

- operational complexity
- inconsistent workflows
- and poor developer experience.

# Platform Benefits

Integrated platforms improve:

- reliability
- usability
- and operational efficiency.
