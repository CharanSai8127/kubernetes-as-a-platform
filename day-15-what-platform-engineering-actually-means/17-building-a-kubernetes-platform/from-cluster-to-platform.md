# From Cluster To Platform

A cluster provides:

- orchestration.

A platform provides:

- orchestration
- security
- observability
- governance
- automation
- and self-service.

---

# Kubernetes Cluster

Provides:

- scheduling
- scaling
- networking
- storage
- and workload management.

---

# Kubernetes Platform

Adds:

- GitOps
- CI/CD
- observability
- policy enforcement
- security controls
- developer workflows
- and governance.

---

# Platform Outcome

Developers consume:

- capabilities

instead of managing:

- infrastructure.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

The goal is not simply running workloads.

The goal is enabling teams to safely deliver software.

---

# Key Insight

A cluster runs applications. A platform enables organizations.
