# Self Service Infrastructure Overview

Self-service infrastructure allows developers to:

- provision resources
- deploy applications
- access platform capabilities
- and consume services

without requiring manual intervention from platform teams.

---

# Why Self Service Infrastructure Matters

Traditional infrastructure models often rely on:

- tickets
- approvals
- manual provisioning
- and operational dependencies.

These processes slow:
- software delivery.

---

# Platform Goal

Provide:

Infrastructure On Demand

WITH

Security

Governance

And

Operational Controls.

---

# Platform Benefits

Self-service improves:

- delivery speed
- developer autonomy
- operational efficiency
- and platform scalability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Infrastructure should be consumed like a service rather than requested like a task.

---

# Key Insight

The fastest infrastructure request is the one that never requires a ticket.
