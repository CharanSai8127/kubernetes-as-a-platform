# Infrastructure On Demand

Modern platforms should provide:

- compute
- storage
- networking
- and platform services

on demand.

Developers should be able to consume resources when needed.

---

# Why On Demand Matters

Waiting for infrastructure creates:

- bottlenecks
- delays
- and reduced delivery velocity.

Self-service removes:
- operational friction.

---

# Consumption Model

Developer
↓

Platform Interface

↓

Automated Provisioning

↓

Infrastructure

---

# Platform Benefits

On-demand infrastructure improves:

- agility
- scalability
- and developer experience.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Infrastructure provisioning should be automated and repeatable.

---

# Key Insight

Infrastructure should be available when developers need it, not when operations becomes available.
