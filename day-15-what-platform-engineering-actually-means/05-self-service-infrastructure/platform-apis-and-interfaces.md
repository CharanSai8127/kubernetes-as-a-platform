# Platform APIs And Interfaces

Platforms expose capabilities through:

- APIs
- portals
- templates
- and automation workflows.

These become the primary interfaces for consuming platform services.

---

# Why Interfaces Matter

Developers should interact with:

- platform abstractions

rather than:

- low-level infrastructure components.

---

# Common Platform Interfaces

Examples include:

- GitOps repositories
- developer portals
- service catalogs
- infrastructure APIs
- and deployment templates.

---

# Platform Benefits

Standardized interfaces improve:

- consistency
- usability
- automation
- and governance.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

A platform is only as useful as the interfaces it exposes.

---

# Key Insight

Good platforms make complex operations simple to consume.
