# Operational Efficiency Indicators

Platform engineering aims to improve:

- operational efficiency

across the organization.

Efficiency measures how effectively platform capabilities are delivered.

---

# Why Efficiency Matters

Operational inefficiencies create:

- delays
- duplicated work
- manual effort
- and increased costs.

---

# Common Indicators

Examples include:

- automation coverage
- incident reduction
- deployment success rates
- and infrastructure utilization.

---

# Platform Benefits

Operational efficiency improves:

- scalability
- reliability
- and organizational productivity.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Efficient platforms reduce the operational burden placed on engineering teams.

---

# Key Insight

Platform value increases when operational effort decreases.
