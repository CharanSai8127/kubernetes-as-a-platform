# Platform Adoption Metrics

Platform adoption measures:

- how widely the platform is used

across engineering teams.

Adoption is one of the strongest indicators of:
- platform value.

---

# Why Adoption Matters

Even technically impressive platforms fail when:

- developers avoid using them.

Adoption indicates that users find value in:

- platform capabilities
- workflows
- and services.

---

# Common Adoption Indicators

Examples include:

- active platform users
- onboarded teams
- self-service usage
- and service consumption.

---

# Platform Benefits

Strong adoption improves:

- standardization
- governance
- and operational consistency.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Platforms should earn adoption through value rather than mandate.

---

# Key Insight

Useful platforms attract users naturally.
