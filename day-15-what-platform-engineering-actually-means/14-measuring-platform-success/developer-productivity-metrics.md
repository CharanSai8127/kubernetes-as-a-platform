# Developer Productivity Metrics

One of the primary goals of Platform Engineering is:

- improving developer productivity.

Productivity measures how effectively teams can:

- build
- deploy
- and operate applications.

---

# Why Productivity Matters

Developer time is valuable.

Reducing operational overhead allows teams to focus on:

- delivering features
- solving customer problems
- and creating business value.

---

# Common Measurements

Examples include:

- deployment frequency
- onboarding time
- environment creation time
- and developer satisfaction.

---

# Platform Benefits

Improved productivity increases:

- delivery velocity
- platform adoption
- and engineering efficiency.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Platforms should help developers move faster without sacrificing reliability.

---

# Key Insight

The best platform allows developers to spend more time building products.
