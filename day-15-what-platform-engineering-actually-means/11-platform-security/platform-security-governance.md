# Platform Security Governance

Security governance defines:

- security requirements
- operational boundaries
- and compliance standards.

Governance ensures:

- security controls are consistently enforced.

---

# Why Governance Matters

Security without governance often becomes:

- inconsistent
- difficult to audit
- and difficult to scale.

---

# Common Governance Controls

Examples include:

- RBAC
- NetworkPolicies
- admission controls
- policy engines
- and compliance requirements.

---

# Platform Benefits

Governance improves:

- visibility
- accountability
- and security consistency.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Governance transforms security requirements into enforceable controls.

---

# Key Insight

Security policies are only effective when they are consistently enforced.
