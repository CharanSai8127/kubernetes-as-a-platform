# Platforms As Products

The future of platform engineering increasingly treats platforms as:

- products

rather than:

- infrastructure projects.

Platform users become:

- customers.

---

# Why This Shift Matters

Developers expect:

- usability
- reliability
- documentation
- and self-service experiences.

Platform adoption depends on:

- delivered value.

---

# Future Platform Focus

Successful platforms optimize for:

- developer experience
- productivity
- and operational efficiency.

---

# Platform Benefits

Product thinking improves:

- adoption
- usability
- and platform effectiveness.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

The platform should continuously evolve based on user needs.

---

# Key Insight

Platforms succeed when users choose them because they create value.
