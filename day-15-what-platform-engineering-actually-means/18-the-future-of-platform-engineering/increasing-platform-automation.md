# Increasing Platform Automation

Automation will continue to become:

- a foundational platform capability.

Future platforms will automate:

- provisioning
- deployments
- governance
- security
- and operational workflows.

---

# Why Automation Matters

Engineering organizations continue to grow.

Manual operations do not scale effectively.

Automation improves:

- consistency
- speed
- reliability
- and efficiency.

---

# Future Platform Characteristics

Platforms will increasingly provide:

- automated workflows
- automated governance
- automated compliance
- and automated operations.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Automation allows platform teams to support more users with fewer operational bottlenecks.

---

# Key Insight

The future platform automates repetitive operational work.
