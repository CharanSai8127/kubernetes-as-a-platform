# The Platform Engineering End State

The goal of platform engineering is not:

- Kubernetes
- cloud infrastructure
- GitOps
- observability
- or security tooling.

These are components of the solution.

---

# The Real Goal

Enable developers to:

- safely build
- deploy
- operate
- and scale software

through self-service capabilities.

---

# Platform Formula

Infrastructure
+
Automation
+
Security
+
Observability
+
Governance
+
Developer Experience

=

Platform Engineering

---

# What Success Looks Like

Developers focus on:

- applications

while platforms provide:

- capabilities
- reliability
- security
- and operational consistency.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

The platform becomes the primary interface between engineering teams and infrastructure.

---

# Key Insight

Platform Engineering is the discipline of turning operational complexity into reusable capabilities.
