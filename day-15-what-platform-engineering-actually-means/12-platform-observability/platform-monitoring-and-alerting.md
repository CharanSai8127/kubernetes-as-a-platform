# Platform Monitoring And Alerting

Monitoring continuously evaluates:

- platform health
- application health
- and operational performance.

Alerting notifies teams when:

- conditions require attention.

---

# Why Monitoring Matters

Failures often begin as:

- performance degradation
- resource exhaustion
- dependency issues
- or operational anomalies.

Early detection improves:

- recovery speed.

---

# Common Monitoring Areas

Examples include:

- availability
- latency
- error rates
- resource utilization
- and service health.

---

# Platform Benefits

Monitoring and alerting improve:

- reliability
- incident response
- and operational awareness.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Visibility should exist before incidents occur.

---

# Key Insight

The earlier a problem is detected, the easier it is to resolve.
