# Platform Observability Overview

Platform observability provides:

- visibility
- monitoring
- troubleshooting
- and operational awareness

across platform services and applications.

Observability becomes:
- a shared platform capability.

---

# Why Platform Observability Matters

Modern systems are distributed.

Failures can occur across:

- applications
- infrastructure
- networking
- and platform services.

Without observability,
understanding system behavior becomes:
- difficult.

---

# Platform Goal

Provide:

Visibility

Into

Platform Health

Application Health

And

Operational Performance.

---

# Platform Benefits

Observability improves:

- troubleshooting
- incident response
- reliability
- and operational decision making.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

A platform should be observable by default.

---

# Key Insight

You cannot operate what you cannot observe.
