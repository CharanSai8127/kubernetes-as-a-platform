# Observability As A Platform Service

Platform teams provide observability as:

- a reusable capability

rather than requiring every team to build it independently.

---

# Why Shared Observability Matters

Building separate observability systems creates:

- duplication
- inconsistency
- and operational overhead.

Shared services improve:

- standardization
- governance
- and operational visibility.

---

# Common Platform Services

Examples include:

- metrics collection
- log aggregation
- distributed tracing
- dashboards
- and alerting.

---

# Platform Benefits

Shared observability improves:

- consistency
- operational efficiency
- and platform adoption.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Observability should be easy to consume and difficult to bypass.

---

# Key Insight

Observability becomes more valuable when it is standardized across teams.
