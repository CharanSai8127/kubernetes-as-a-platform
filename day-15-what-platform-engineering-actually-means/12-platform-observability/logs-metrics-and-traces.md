# Logs Metrics And Traces

Observability is commonly built around:

- logs
- metrics
- and traces.

Together they provide:

- operational visibility
- system understanding
- and troubleshooting capabilities.

---

# Logs

Logs describe:

- events
- actions
- and runtime behavior.

They answer:

What happened?

---

# Metrics

Metrics describe:

- performance
- utilization
- and health.

They answer:

How much?

How often?

---

# Traces

Traces describe:

- request flow
- dependencies
- and latency.

They answer:

Where did the request go?

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Logs, metrics, and traces provide complementary operational visibility.

---

# Key Insight

Observability requires multiple perspectives into system behavior.
