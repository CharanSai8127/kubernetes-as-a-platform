# Internal Developer Platform Overview

An Internal Developer Platform (IDP) is a collection of:
- tools
- services
- workflows
- and automation

that enable developers to consume platform capabilities through self-service interfaces.

---

# Why Internal Developer Platforms Matter

Modern software delivery requires:

- infrastructure
- deployments
- observability
- security
- networking
- and governance.

Managing these individually increases:
- complexity
- operational overhead
- and delivery time.

---

# The Goal Of An IDP

Provide:

Developer Self-Service

WITHOUT

Exposing Infrastructure Complexity.

---

# Platform Benefits

Internal Developer Platforms improve:

- developer productivity
- platform consistency
- operational reliability
- and governance.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

The platform becomes:
- the primary interface between developers and infrastructure.

---

# Key Insight

An Internal Developer Platform packages operational capabilities into reusable developer services.
