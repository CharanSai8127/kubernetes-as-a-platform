# Platform Capabilities

Internal Developer Platforms provide:

- infrastructure capabilities
- deployment capabilities
- security capabilities
- and operational capabilities.

These services become:
- reusable building blocks.

---

# Common Platform Capabilities

Examples include:

- application deployment
- secret management
- logging
- monitoring
- networking
- storage
- and policy enforcement.

---

# Why Capabilities Matter

Developers should focus on:

- application development

rather than:

- building platform tooling.

---

# Platform Benefits

Shared capabilities improve:

- consistency
- reliability
- and operational efficiency.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Platform capabilities should be standardized and reusable.

---

# Key Insight

Platforms provide services, not just infrastructure.
