# Platform Consumption Model

Platform Engineering changes how infrastructure is consumed.

Instead of managing infrastructure directly,
developers consume:

- platform services
- platform APIs
- templates
- and workflows.

---

# Traditional Model

Developer
↓

Infrastructure

↓

Operations

---

# Platform Model

Developer
↓

Platform

↓

Infrastructure

---

# Benefits Of The Platform Model

Improves:

- consistency
- governance
- security
- and developer experience.

---

# Operational Impact

Platform teams manage:
- complexity.

Application teams consume:
- capabilities.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

The platform becomes the product developers use every day.

---

# Key Insight

Successful platforms abstract operational complexity without removing operational control.
