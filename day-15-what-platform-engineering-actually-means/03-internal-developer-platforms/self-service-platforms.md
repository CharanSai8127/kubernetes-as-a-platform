# Self Service Platforms

Self-service platforms allow developers to:

- deploy applications
- provision resources
- access observability
- and consume platform services

without requiring platform team intervention.

---

# Why Self Service Matters

Platform teams cannot manually handle:

- every deployment
- every request
- every environment
- and every operational task.

Self-service improves:
- scalability.

---

# Platform Objectives

Enable:

- speed
- autonomy
- and productivity.

While maintaining:

- security
- governance
- and reliability.

---

# Operational Benefits

Self-service reduces:

- bottlenecks
- wait times
- and operational dependencies.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Developers should consume platform capabilities on demand.

---

# Key Insight

The best platform is one developers can use without opening a ticket.
