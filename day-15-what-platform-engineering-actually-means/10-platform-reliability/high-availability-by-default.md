# High Availability By Default

Modern platforms should provide:

- highly available services

as a default capability.

Availability should not depend on:

- individual application implementations.

---

# Why High Availability Matters

Failures are inevitable.

Examples include:

- node failures
- infrastructure failures
- deployment failures
- and dependency outages.

---

# Platform Goal

Reduce:

- downtime
- service disruption
- and operational impact.

---

# Common Platform Mechanisms

Examples include:

- workload replication
- autoscaling
- health checks
- and failure recovery.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Availability should be built into the platform.

---

# Key Insight

Platforms should assume failures will occur and prepare for them.
