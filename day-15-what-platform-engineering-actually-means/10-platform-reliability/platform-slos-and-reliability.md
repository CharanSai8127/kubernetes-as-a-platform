# Platform SLOs And Reliability

Reliability should be measurable.

Platforms commonly define:

- Service Level Objectives (SLOs)

to measure operational performance.

---

# Why SLOs Matter

Reliability cannot be improved if it is not measured.

SLOs provide visibility into:

- availability
- performance
- and operational health.

---

# Common Platform Measurements

Examples include:

- uptime
- deployment success rates
- recovery times
- and service availability.

---

# Platform Benefits

Measurement improves:

- decision making
- operational visibility
- and continuous improvement.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Reliable platforms are built through measurement and iteration.

---

# Key Insight

You cannot improve what you do not measure.
