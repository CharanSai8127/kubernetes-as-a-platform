# Shared Reliability Capabilities

Platform engineering provides:

- reliability as a service.

Application teams should not need to independently solve:

- availability
- scalability
- failover
- and resilience.

---

# Common Reliability Capabilities

Examples include:

- autoscaling
- self-healing
- workload scheduling
- high availability
- and traffic management.

---

# Why Shared Capabilities Matter

Building reliability independently creates:

- duplication
- inconsistency
- and operational overhead.

---

# Platform Benefits

Shared reliability capabilities improve:

- consistency
- operational efficiency
- and platform adoption.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Reliability should be delivered as a platform capability.

---

# Key Insight

Reliability becomes more effective when it is standardized.
