# Platform Ownership Model Overview

Platform ownership defines:

- responsibilities
- accountability
- operational boundaries
- and service ownership.

Clear ownership is essential for:
- scalable platform operations.

---

# Why Ownership Matters

Modern platforms contain:

- infrastructure
- security systems
- observability services
- deployment tooling
- and governance controls.

Without ownership,
organizations experience:

- confusion
- duplicated effort
- and operational gaps.

---

# Platform Goal

Create:

Clear Responsibilities

Clear Accountability

And

Clear Operational Boundaries.

---

# Organizational Benefits

Ownership improves:

- supportability
- reliability
- governance
- and operational efficiency.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Every platform capability should have a clearly defined owner.

---

# Key Insight

Undefined ownership creates undefined responsibility.
