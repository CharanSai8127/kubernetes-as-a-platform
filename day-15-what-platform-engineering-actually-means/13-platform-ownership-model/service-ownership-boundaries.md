# Service Ownership Boundaries

Every service should have:

- an owner
- an operational model
- and a support model.

Ownership creates:

- accountability
- reliability
- and governance.

---

# Common Shared Services

Examples include:

- ArgoCD
- Vault
- Prometheus
- Grafana
- Gateway API
- and identity systems.

---

# Why Boundaries Matter

Without ownership boundaries:

- upgrades become unclear
- incidents become confusing
- and governance becomes inconsistent.

---

# Platform Benefits

Clear service ownership improves:

- supportability
- operational efficiency
- and platform reliability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Shared services should be centrally owned and consistently operated.

---

# Key Insight

Every critical service should have a clearly identifiable owner.
