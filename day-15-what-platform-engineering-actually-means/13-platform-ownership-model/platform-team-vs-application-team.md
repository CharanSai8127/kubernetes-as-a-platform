# Platform Team Vs Application Team

Platform Engineering separates:

- platform responsibilities

from:

- application responsibilities.

Both teams solve different problems.

---

# Platform Team Responsibilities

Platform teams commonly own:

- Kubernetes
- infrastructure
- networking
- observability
- security
- governance
- and platform services.

Their mission is:

Platform Reliability.

---

# Application Team Responsibilities

Application teams commonly own:

- application code
- APIs
- business logic
- deployments
- and customer-facing functionality.

Their mission is:

Business Value Delivery.

---

# Organizational Benefits

Separation improves:

- focus
- scalability
- accountability
- and operational consistency.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Platform teams provide capabilities.

Application teams consume capabilities.

---

# Key Insight

Platform teams build the roads. Application teams drive on them.
