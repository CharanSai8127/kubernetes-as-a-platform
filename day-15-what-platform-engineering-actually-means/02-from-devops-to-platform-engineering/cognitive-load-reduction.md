# Cognitive Load Reduction

Developers should focus on:
- building applications

rather than:
- understanding every infrastructure detail.

---

# Why Cognitive Load Matters

Modern platforms contain:

- networking
- storage
- security
- observability
- governance
- and deployment systems.

Understanding everything creates:
- operational burden.

---

# Platform Engineering Goal

Platforms reduce:
- unnecessary complexity

while exposing:
- necessary capabilities.

---

# Benefits Of Reduced Cognitive Load

Teams can focus on:

- product development
- customer value
- and application reliability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Reducing cognitive load improves both developer productivity and platform adoption.

---

# Key Insight

Not every developer needs to become an infrastructure expert.
