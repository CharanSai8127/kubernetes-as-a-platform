# DevOps Vs Platform Engineering

DevOps and Platform Engineering are closely related.

Platform Engineering did not replace:
- DevOps.

Platform Engineering evolved from:
- DevOps principles.

---

# What DevOps Solved

DevOps focused on:

- collaboration
- automation
- shared ownership
- and faster software delivery.

Its goal was to remove barriers between:
- development
and:
- operations.

---

# The Scaling Challenge

As organizations grew,
teams began managing:

- Kubernetes
- CI/CD
- observability
- cloud infrastructure
- security
- and governance.

This increased:
- operational complexity.

---

# Platform Engineering Response

Platform teams build:
- reusable capabilities

that application teams consume through:
- self-service workflows.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

DevOps is a culture.

Platform Engineering is an implementation model.

---

# Key Insight

Platform Engineering applies DevOps principles at organizational scale.
