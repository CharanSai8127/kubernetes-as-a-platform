# The Evolution Of DevOps

DevOps introduced:

- automation
- collaboration
- and continuous delivery.

These principles transformed:
- software delivery.

---

# Early DevOps

Teams commonly managed:

- application deployments
- infrastructure automation
- and operational processes.

The environment was often:
- relatively simple.

---

# Modern Complexity

Today's platforms include:

- cloud infrastructure
- Kubernetes
- service meshes
- observability systems
- security tooling
- and governance controls.

---

# The Next Evolution

Organizations required:

- dedicated platform capabilities

to support:
- multiple development teams.

This led to:
- Platform Engineering.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Platform Engineering extends DevOps by productizing operational capabilities.

---

# Key Insight

Platform Engineering emerged because infrastructure complexity continued to grow.
