# Why Platform Teams Exist

Platform teams exist to solve:
- platform problems.

Application teams exist to solve:
- business problems.

---

# The Challenge

Without platform teams,
every application team may build:

- deployment pipelines
- observability systems
- security integrations
- and infrastructure tooling.

This creates:
- duplication
- inconsistency
- and operational overhead.

---

# Platform Team Role

Platform teams build:

- reusable tooling
- shared services
- governance controls
- and developer platforms.

---

# Organizational Benefits

Platform teams improve:

- consistency
- reliability
- security
- and operational efficiency.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

A platform team enables many application teams to move faster.

---

# Key Insight

Platform teams centralize common capabilities so application teams do not have to reinvent them.
