# Reducing Friction

Engineering organizations move faster when:

- common tasks are simple.

Platform engineering focuses on removing:

- operational bottlenecks
- manual processes
- repetitive tasks
- and unnecessary complexity.

---

# Why Friction Matters

Developers often encounter delays caused by:

- infrastructure requests
- deployment approvals
- environment creation
- and operational dependencies.

These delays reduce:

- productivity
- and delivery speed.

---

# Platform Goal

Reduce:

- waiting
- manual intervention
- and operational overhead.

---

# Platform Benefits

Low-friction platforms improve:

- efficiency
- adoption
- and developer satisfaction.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Every unnecessary step creates delivery friction.

---

# Key Insight

The best platform removes obstacles without removing control.
