# Platform Adoption And Usability

A platform only succeeds when:

- developers choose to use it.

Adoption depends heavily on:

- usability.

---

# Why Usability Matters

Developers prefer platforms that are:

- reliable
- intuitive
- predictable
- and well documented.

Poor usability often results in:

- platform avoidance
- custom tooling
- and shadow infrastructure.

---

# Measuring Usability

Examples include:

- onboarding speed
- deployment success rates
- support requests
- and developer feedback.

---

# Platform Benefits

Usable platforms improve:

- consistency
- productivity
- and platform adoption.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Platform success depends on user success.

---

# Key Insight

Developers adopt platforms that make their jobs easier.
