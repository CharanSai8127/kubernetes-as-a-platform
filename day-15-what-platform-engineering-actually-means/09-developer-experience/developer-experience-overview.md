# Developer Experience Overview

Developer Experience (DevEx) refers to:

- how easy it is for developers
to build
deploy
operate
and maintain applications.

Developer experience is one of the primary goals of:
- Platform Engineering.

---

# Why Developer Experience Matters

Developers interact with:

- infrastructure
- deployment systems
- observability tooling
- security controls
- and platform services.

Poor experiences create:

- frustration
- inefficiency
- and reduced productivity.

---

# Platform Goal

Improve:

- usability
- productivity
- reliability
- and platform adoption.

---

# Organizational Benefits

Good developer experience improves:

- delivery velocity
- platform adoption
- engineering satisfaction
- and operational consistency.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

The platform should make developers more productive rather than creating additional work.

---

# Key Insight

Developer experience is a platform feature, not an afterthought.
