# Self Service Developer Workflows

Developers should be able to:

- deploy applications
- provision environments
- access observability
- and consume platform services

through self-service workflows.

---

# Why Self Service Matters

Platform teams cannot become:

- deployment bottlenecks
- provisioning bottlenecks
- or operational bottlenecks.

Self-service enables:

- scale.

---

# Workflow Examples

Examples include:

- GitOps deployments
- environment provisioning
- secret requests
- and application onboarding.

---

# Platform Benefits

Self-service workflows improve:

- autonomy
- productivity
- and delivery velocity.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Developers should consume platform capabilities on demand.

---

# Key Insight

The fastest workflow is the one that does not require human intervention.
