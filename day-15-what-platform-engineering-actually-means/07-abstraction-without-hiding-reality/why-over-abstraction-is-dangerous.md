# Why Over Abstraction Is Dangerous

Abstractions become dangerous when they hide:

- operational realities
- system limitations
- failure modes
- and platform behavior.

---

# The Problem

Developers may successfully deploy applications while having no understanding of:

- networking
- storage
- security
- observability
- or scalability.

This becomes a problem during:
- incidents.

---

# Runtime Impact

When failures occur,
teams may struggle to understand:

- root causes
- dependencies
- and platform interactions.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Platforms should reduce complexity but not eliminate visibility.

---

# Platform Goal

Provide:

Simple Interfaces

WITH

Operational Transparency.

---

# Key Insight

Hiding complexity is useful. Hiding reality is dangerous.
