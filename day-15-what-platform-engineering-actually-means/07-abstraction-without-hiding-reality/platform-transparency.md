# Platform Transparency

Platform transparency means:

Users can understand:

- what the platform is doing
- how services are provisioned
- and how workloads are operated.

---

# Why Transparency Matters

Engineers should be able to answer:

- where applications run
- how traffic flows
- where logs are stored
- and how failures are handled.

---

# Transparency Benefits

Transparency improves:

- troubleshooting
- observability
- incident response
- and platform trust.

---

# Common Transparency Mechanisms

Examples include:

- dashboards
- documentation
- architecture diagrams
- and operational visibility.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Transparency creates confidence in platform capabilities.

---

# Key Insight

Developers should understand the platform they depend on.
