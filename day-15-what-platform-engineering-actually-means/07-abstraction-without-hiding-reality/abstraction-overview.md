# Abstraction Overview

Abstraction is the process of hiding:
- unnecessary complexity

while exposing:
- useful capabilities.

Platform engineering relies heavily on:
- abstractions.

---

# Why Abstraction Matters

Modern platforms contain:

- Kubernetes
- networking
- storage
- observability
- security
- governance
- and cloud infrastructure.

Application teams should not need to manage:
- every implementation detail.

---

# Platform Goal

Simplify:

- platform consumption

without removing:

- operational understanding.

---

# Platform Benefits

Abstractions improve:

- usability
- adoption
- productivity
- and operational consistency.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

The platform should simplify complexity without creating ignorance.

---

# Key Insight

Good abstractions remove unnecessary complexity while preserving understanding.
