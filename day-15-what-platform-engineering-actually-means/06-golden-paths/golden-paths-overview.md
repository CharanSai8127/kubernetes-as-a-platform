# Golden Paths Overview

Golden paths are:
- approved
- supported
- and recommended ways

to build, deploy, and operate applications on a platform.

They provide developers with:
- a safe default path.

---

# Why Golden Paths Matter

Without guidance,
teams often create:

- inconsistent architectures
- custom tooling
- security gaps
- and operational complexity.

Golden paths reduce:
- variability.

---

# Platform Goal

Provide:

Developer Freedom

WITH

Platform Standards

Security

And

Operational Reliability.

---

# Platform Benefits

Golden paths improve:

- consistency
- security
- maintainability
- and developer productivity.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

The preferred path should also be the most reliable path.

---

# Key Insight

Golden paths make the correct solution the easiest solution.
