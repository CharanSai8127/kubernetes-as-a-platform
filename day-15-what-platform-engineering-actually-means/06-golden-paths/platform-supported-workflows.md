# Platform Supported Workflows

Platforms should provide:
- supported workflows

for common engineering tasks.

These workflows become:
- reusable operational patterns.

---

# Common Platform Workflows

Examples include:

- application deployment
- environment creation
- secret management
- observability onboarding
- and incident response.

---

# Why Supported Workflows Matter

Teams should not need to:

- build custom processes
- create custom tooling
- or solve solved problems.

---

# Platform Benefits

Supported workflows improve:

- reliability
- consistency
- and operational efficiency.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

The platform should encode organizational best practices.

---

# Key Insight

Platform workflows allow teams to focus on outcomes rather than implementation details.
