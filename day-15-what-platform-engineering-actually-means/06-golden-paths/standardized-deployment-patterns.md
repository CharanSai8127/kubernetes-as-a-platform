# Standardized Deployment Patterns

Platforms should provide:
- repeatable deployment approaches.

Developers should not need to reinvent:

- deployment pipelines
- observability integrations
- security controls
- or infrastructure patterns.

---

# Why Standardization Matters

Inconsistent deployments create:

- operational drift
- support challenges
- and governance issues.

Standardization improves:
- reliability.

---

# Common Deployment Standards

Examples include:

- GitOps workflows
- approved container images
- deployment templates
- monitoring integrations
- and security policies.

---

# Platform Benefits

Standardization improves:

- troubleshooting
- supportability
- scalability
- and governance.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Common problems should have common solutions.

---

# Key Insight

Consistency reduces operational complexity.
