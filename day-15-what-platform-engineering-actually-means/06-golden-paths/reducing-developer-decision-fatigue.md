# Reducing Developer Decision Fatigue

Modern platforms contain:

- networking options
- deployment strategies
- observability choices
- security requirements
- and infrastructure decisions.

Too many choices create:
- cognitive load.

---

# Why Decision Fatigue Matters

Developers should spend time:

- building products

rather than:

- evaluating platform implementation details.

---

# Golden Path Approach

Provide:

- proven defaults
- supported architectures
- and recommended workflows.

---

# Platform Benefits

Reducing unnecessary decisions improves:

- productivity
- consistency
- and platform adoption.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Not every decision should be delegated to every team.

---

# Key Insight

Good defaults are often more valuable than unlimited flexibility.
