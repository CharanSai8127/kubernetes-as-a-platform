# Shared Services Management

Many organizations operate:

- shared platform services

consumed by multiple teams.

---

# Common Shared Services

Examples include:

- ArgoCD
- Vault
- Prometheus
- Grafana
- Gateway API
- identity systems
- and developer portals.

---

# Why Shared Services Matter

Centralized services improve:

- consistency
- governance
- security
- and operational efficiency.

---

# Platform Responsibilities

Platform teams manage:

- upgrades
- reliability
- security
- backups
- and operational support.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Shared services should be centrally operated and broadly consumed.

---

# Key Insight

Centralized platform services reduce operational duplication across teams.
