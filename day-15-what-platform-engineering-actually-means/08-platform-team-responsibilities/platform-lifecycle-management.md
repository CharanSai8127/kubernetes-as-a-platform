# Platform Lifecycle Management

Platforms continuously evolve.

Platform teams are responsible for managing:

- upgrades
- maintenance
- security improvements
- governance changes
- and operational enhancements.

---

# Why Lifecycle Management Matters

Technology changes continuously.

Platforms must adapt to:

- new requirements
- security threats
- operational lessons
- and organizational growth.

---

# Lifecycle Activities

Examples include:

- Kubernetes upgrades
- policy updates
- tooling improvements
- and architecture evolution.

---

# Platform Benefits

Lifecycle management improves:

- stability
- security
- reliability
- and long-term sustainability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Platforms are products that require continuous investment.

---

# Key Insight

A platform is never finished; it continuously evolves with its users.
