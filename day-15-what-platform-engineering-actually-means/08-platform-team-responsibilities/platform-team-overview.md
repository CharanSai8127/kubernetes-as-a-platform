# Platform Team Overview

Platform teams are responsible for:

- building
- operating
- securing
- and evolving

the internal platform used by application teams.

---

# Why Platform Teams Exist

Application teams should focus on:

- business logic
- product features
- and customer value.

Platform teams focus on:

- infrastructure
- automation
- governance
- and operational capabilities.

---

# Platform Mission

Provide:

Reliable

Secure

Scalable

Self-Service

Capabilities

To Engineering Teams.

---

# Organizational Impact

Platform teams reduce:

- duplicated effort
- operational complexity
- and inconsistent implementations.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Platform teams enable engineering organizations to scale.

---

# Key Insight

Platform teams solve platform problems so application teams can solve business problems.
