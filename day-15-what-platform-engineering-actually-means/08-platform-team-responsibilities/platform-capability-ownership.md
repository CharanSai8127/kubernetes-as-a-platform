# Platform Capability Ownership

Platform teams own:

- platform capabilities

rather than:

- application functionality.

---

# Common Platform Capabilities

Examples include:

- Kubernetes platforms
- CI/CD systems
- GitOps workflows
- observability stacks
- security tooling
- networking
- and infrastructure automation.

---

# Why Ownership Matters

Clear ownership improves:

- accountability
- reliability
- supportability
- and operational consistency.

---

# Platform Responsibility

Platform teams ensure capabilities are:

- available
- reliable
- secure
- and maintainable.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Shared capabilities require dedicated ownership.

---

# Key Insight

A platform capability should always have a clearly defined owner.
