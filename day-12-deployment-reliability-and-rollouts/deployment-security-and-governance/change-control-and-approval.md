# Change Control And Approval

Runtime deployments continuously introduce:
- operational risk.

Change control ensures:
- deployments remain observable
- reviewable
- and controlled.

---

# Why Change Control Matters

Unreviewed runtime changes may create:
- deployment instability
- dependency failures
- and runtime unpredictability.

---

# Runtime Deployment Governance

Change management commonly includes:
- approval workflows
- deployment reviews
- and rollout validation processes.

---

# Operational Benefits

Controlled change management improves:
- deployment reliability
- rollback safety
- and runtime accountability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Deployment governance strongly impacts:
- runtime survivability engineering.

---

# Key Insight

Most production instability appears during uncontrolled runtime change.
