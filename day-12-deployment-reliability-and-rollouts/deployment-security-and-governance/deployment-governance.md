# Deployment Governance

Distributed systems continuously evolve through:
- runtime deployments
- configuration updates
- and infrastructure changes.

Governance improves:
- deployment safety
- operational consistency
- and runtime control.

---

# Why Governance Matters

Unsafe deployment practices may create:
- unauthorized runtime changes
- unhealthy rollouts
- and operational instability.

---

# Runtime Deployment Control

Governance commonly includes:
- deployment policies
- approval workflows
- and runtime validation standards.

---

# Operational Benefits

Governance improves:
- deployment reliability
- rollback safety
- and operational accountability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Governance becomes:
- runtime deployment survivability control.

---

# Key Insight

Reliable systems require controlled runtime change management.
