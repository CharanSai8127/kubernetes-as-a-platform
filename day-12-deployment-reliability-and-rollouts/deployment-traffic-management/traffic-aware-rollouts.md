# Traffic Aware Rollouts

Deployments should continuously understand:
- runtime traffic behavior.

Traffic should only reach:
- healthy
- synchronized
- and validated workloads.

---

# Why Traffic Awareness Matters

Unsafe traffic exposure may create:
- failed requests
- retry amplification
- and cascading instability.

---

# Runtime Deployment Coordination

Traffic-aware rollouts continuously manage:
- readiness validation
- rollout progression
- and runtime traffic exposure.

---

# Operational Benefits

Traffic awareness improves:
- deployment safety
- runtime survivability
- and user experience.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Traffic-aware deployments become:
- runtime communication survivability engineering.

---

# Key Insight

Traffic should only reach workloads that are truly operational.
