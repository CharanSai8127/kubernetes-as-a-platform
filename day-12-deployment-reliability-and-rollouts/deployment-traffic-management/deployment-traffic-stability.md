# Deployment Traffic Stability

Distributed systems continuously depend on:
- stable runtime traffic behavior.

Deployments should not destabilize:
- healthy traffic communication.

---

# Why Stability Matters

Deployment instability may create:
- failed requests
- unhealthy retries
- and runtime communication degradation.

---

# Runtime Traffic Coordination

Traffic stability depends on:
- readiness validation
- healthy routing
- and gradual rollout orchestration.

---

# Operational Challenges

Traffic instability may spread across:
- dependencies
- communication paths
- and healthy workloads.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Traffic stability strongly impacts:
- deployment survivability engineering.

---

# Key Insight

Reliable deployments preserve healthy runtime traffic behavior.
