# Traffic Shifting

Traffic shifting gradually redirects:
- production requests
toward:
- new deployment versions.

This improves:
- deployment safety
- and runtime validation.

---

# Why Traffic Shifting Matters

Routing all traffic immediately toward:
- new workloads
introduces:
- large operational risk.

Gradual exposure reduces:
- deployment blast radius.

---

# Runtime Deployment Coordination

Traffic commonly shifts through:
- progressive rollout percentages.

Examples include:
- 5%
- 20%
- 50%
- and full production exposure.

---

# Operational Benefits

Traffic shifting improves:
- rollout observability
- rollback flexibility
- and runtime resilience.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Traffic shifting becomes:
- runtime deployment risk management.

---

# Key Insight

Traffic exposure should evolve gradually during deployments.
