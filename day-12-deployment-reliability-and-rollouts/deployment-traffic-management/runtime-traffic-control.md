# Runtime Traffic Control

Distributed systems continuously process:
- live production traffic.

Traffic control ensures:
- deployment changes do not destabilize runtime communication.

---

# Why Traffic Control Matters

Unsafe deployments may expose:
- unhealthy replicas
- unstable runtime behavior
- and failing dependencies.

---

# Runtime Deployment Safety

Traffic routing should remain:
- controlled
- observable
- and reversible during deployments.

---

# Operational Challenges

Improper traffic control may create:
- cascading retries
- latency amplification
- and unhealthy traffic distribution.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Traffic control directly impacts:
- deployment survivability.

---

# Key Insight

Deployment safety strongly depends on controlled traffic behavior.
