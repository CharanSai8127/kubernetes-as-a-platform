# Deployment Runtime Instability

Runtime deployments continuously introduce:
- infrastructure change
- traffic change
- and workload replacement.

These operations may destabilize:
- healthy runtime systems.

---

# Why Runtime Instability Matters

Distributed systems continuously depend on:
- stable communication
- healthy traffic routing
- and synchronized runtime behavior.

---

# Runtime Deployment Risks

Deployment instability may create:
- unhealthy replicas
- synchronization failures
- and cascading communication instability.

---

# Operational Challenges

Runtime instability may amplify:
- retries
- traffic pressure
- and operational complexity.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Deployment reliability strongly depends on:
- runtime coordination safety.

---

# Key Insight

Distributed systems commonly fail during runtime evolution.
