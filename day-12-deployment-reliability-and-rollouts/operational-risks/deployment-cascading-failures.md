# Deployment Cascading Failures

Unsafe deployments may create:
- cascading runtime instability.

Failures may spread across:
- workloads
- dependencies
- and communication paths.

---

# Why Cascading Failures Matter

Distributed systems continuously depend on:
- stable runtime coordination.

Deployment instability may amplify:
- retries
- traffic pressure
- and dependency overload.

---

# Runtime Failure Risks

Cascading deployment behavior may create:
- platform-wide outages
- synchronization failures
- and unhealthy runtime recovery.

---

# Operational Benefits

Failure containment improves:
- deployment survivability
- rollback safety
- and runtime resilience.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Distributed systems require:
- localized failure isolation during deployments.

---

# Key Insight

Deployment instability should never spread across healthy systems.
