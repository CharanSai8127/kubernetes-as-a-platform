# Runtime Traffic Failures

Deployments continuously affect:
- runtime traffic behavior.

Unsafe deployments may create:
- failed requests
- traffic amplification
- and unhealthy routing.

---

# Why Traffic Failures Matter

Distributed systems continuously depend on:
- healthy communication paths.

Traffic instability may rapidly spread across:
- workloads
- dependencies
- and runtime services.

---

# Runtime Deployment Risks

Traffic failures commonly emerge from:
- unhealthy replicas
- failed readiness validation
- and unsafe rollout exposure.

---

# Operational Challenges

Traffic instability may create:
- cascading retries
- degraded user experience
- and runtime outages.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Traffic stability strongly impacts:
- deployment survivability engineering.

---

# Key Insight

Healthy traffic routing is critical during runtime deployments.
