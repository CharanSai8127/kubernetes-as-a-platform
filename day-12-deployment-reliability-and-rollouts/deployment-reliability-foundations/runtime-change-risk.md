# Runtime Change Risk

Distributed systems continuously evolve through:
- deployments
- configuration updates
- and runtime changes.

Every change introduces:
- operational risk.

---

# Why Change Is Dangerous

Even small updates may introduce:
- missing dependencies
- startup instability
- runtime communication failures
- and unhealthy rollout behavior.

---

# Runtime Deployment Risks

Changes may impact:
- application behavior
- traffic flow
- dependency stability
- and synchronization consistency.

---

# Operational Challenges

Unsafe change introduction may create:
- outages
- cascading failures
- and runtime unpredictability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Reliable platforms continuously manage:
- runtime change safely.

---

# Key Insight

Most distributed-system instability appears during runtime change introduction.
