# Deployment Survivability

Reliable systems must survive:
- runtime deployment changes safely.

Deployments continuously introduce:
- new runtime behavior
- traffic changes
- and synchronization events.

---

# Why Survivability Matters

Deployment failures may create:
- unhealthy workloads
- traffic instability
- and dependency failures.

---

# Runtime Deployment Safety

Safe deployment behavior depends on:
- health validation
- traffic-aware rollouts
- and failure containment.

---

# Operational Challenges

Deployment instability may spread across:
- workloads
- dependencies
- and communication paths.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Deployment survivability directly impacts:
- distributed-system reliability.

---

# Key Insight

Reliable platforms survive runtime change safely.
