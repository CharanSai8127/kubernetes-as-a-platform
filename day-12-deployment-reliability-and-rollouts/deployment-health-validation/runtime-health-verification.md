# Runtime Health Verification

Distributed systems continuously depend on:
- healthy runtime behavior.

Verification ensures:
- workloads become operational before receiving traffic.

---

# Why Verification Matters

Applications may appear operational while:
- internal failures still exist.

Examples include:
- database connectivity failures
- synchronization delays
- and dependency instability.

---

# Runtime Validation Operations

Health verification commonly checks:
- application responsiveness
- runtime initialization
- and dependency communication.

---

# Operational Challenges

Improper validation may create:
- unhealthy traffic routing
- cascading failures
- and deployment instability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Runtime verification strongly impacts:
- deployment survivability.

---

# Key Insight

Healthy traffic depends on verified runtime readiness.
