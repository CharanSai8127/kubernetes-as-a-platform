# Runtime Consistency

Distributed systems continuously depend on:
- predictable runtime behavior.

Runtime consistency improves:
- deployment survivability
- communication stability
- and workload reliability.

---

# Why Runtime Consistency Matters

Deployment instability may create:
- inconsistent replicas
- unhealthy traffic routing
- and synchronization failures.

---

# Runtime Deployment Validation

Consistency commonly depends on:
- readiness validation
- traffic-aware deployment behavior
- and reconciliation stability.

---

# Operational Challenges

Runtime inconsistency may create:
- cascading failures
- retry amplification
- and deployment unpredictability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Runtime consistency strongly impacts:
- distributed-system survivability.

---

# Key Insight

Reliable deployments require predictable runtime behavior.
