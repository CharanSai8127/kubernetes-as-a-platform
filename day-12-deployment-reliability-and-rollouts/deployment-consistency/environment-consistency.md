# Environment Consistency

Reliable deployments continuously depend on:
- predictable runtime environments.

Environment inconsistency may create:
- deployment instability
- runtime drift
- and unhealthy application behavior.

---

# Why Consistency Matters

Applications may behave differently because of:
- configuration mismatches
- dependency differences
- or infrastructure drift.

---

# Runtime Deployment Stability

Consistent environments improve:
- rollout predictability
- runtime validation
- and deployment survivability.

---

# Operational Challenges

Inconsistent environments may create:
- hidden failures
- debugging complexity
- and deployment unpredictability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Environment consistency strongly impacts:
- deployment reliability engineering.

---

# Key Insight

Distributed deployments require predictable runtime environments.
