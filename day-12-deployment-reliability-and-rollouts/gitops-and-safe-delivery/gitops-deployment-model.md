# GitOps Deployment Model

GitOps manages deployments through:
- declarative version-controlled infrastructure.

Runtime state continuously reconciles against:
- Git-defined desired state.

---

# Why GitOps Matters

Manual deployments may introduce:
- runtime drift
- configuration inconsistency
- and operational unpredictability.

GitOps improves:
- deployment consistency
- auditability
- and survivability.

---

# Runtime Deployment Coordination

GitOps continuously manages:
- rollout synchronization
- configuration reconciliation
- and runtime correction.

---

# Operational Benefits

GitOps improves:
- rollback safety
- deployment reliability
- and runtime visibility.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

GitOps becomes:
- declarative runtime deployment orchestration.

---

# Key Insight

Reliable deployments continuously reconcile against declarative desired state.
