# Safe Runtime Delivery

Distributed systems continuously evolve during:
- runtime deployments.

Safe delivery focuses on:
- minimizing instability during change introduction.

---

# Why Safe Delivery Matters

Unsafe deployment behavior may create:
- cascading failures
- unhealthy traffic routing
- and runtime communication instability.

---

# Runtime Deployment Safety

Safe delivery commonly depends on:
- progressive rollout behavior
- readiness validation
- and traffic-aware orchestration.

---

# Operational Benefits

Safe runtime delivery improves:
- deployment survivability
- rollback reliability
- and runtime resilience.

# Platform Engineering Perspective

- runtime survivability engineering.
