# Runtime Traffic Protection

Distributed systems continuously serve:
- live user traffic.

Traffic protection ensures:
- unhealthy workloads do not impact users.

---

# Why Traffic Protection Matters

Unsafe deployments may expose:
- failing replicas
- unstable communication
- and unhealthy runtime behavior.

---

# Runtime Deployment Safety

Traffic should only reach:
- healthy
- synchronized
- and validated workloads.

---

# Operational Challenges

Unprotected traffic routing may create:
- cascading failures
- retry amplification
- and degraded platform stability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Traffic protection directly impacts:
- deployment survivability.

---

# Key Insight

Healthy traffic routing is critical during runtime deployments.
