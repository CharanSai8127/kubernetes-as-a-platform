# Healthy Traffic Routing

Distributed systems continuously route:
- live runtime traffic.

Routing behavior directly impacts:
- deployment safety
- and runtime stability.

---

# Why Healthy Routing Matters

Unhealthy workloads may create:
- failed requests
- retry amplification
- and cascading failures.

---

# Runtime Traffic Coordination

Kubernetes continuously routes traffic toward:
- healthy
- ready
- and synchronized replicas.

---

# Operational Challenges

Improper routing may destabilize:
- healthy workloads
- dependencies
- and communication paths.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Traffic routing becomes:
- runtime communication survivability engineering.

---

# Key Insight

Reliable deployments depend on healthy traffic routing behavior.
