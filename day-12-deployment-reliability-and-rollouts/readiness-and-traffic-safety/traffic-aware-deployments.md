# Traffic-Aware Deployments

Deployments should remain aware of:
- runtime traffic behavior.

Applications must not receive:
- production traffic
until:
- runtime readiness succeeds.

---

# Why Traffic Awareness Matters

Deployment instability may create:
- unhealthy request routing
- traffic spikes
- and communication failures.

---

# Runtime Rollout Coordination

Traffic-aware deployments continuously coordinate:
- readiness validation
- rollout progression
- and traffic exposure safely.

---

# Operational Benefits

Traffic awareness improves:
- deployment reliability
- runtime stability
- and user experience.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Traffic-aware delivery becomes:
- runtime survivability engineering.

---

# Key Insight

Traffic should only reach workloads that are truly operational.
