# Rollback Survivability

Rollback survivability focuses on:
- safely returning toward a previously healthy runtime state.

Rollback itself becomes:
- another distributed-system event.

---

# Why Rollback Matters

Failed deployments may destabilize:
- traffic flow
- dependencies
- synchronization
- and runtime communication.

Rollback improves:
- runtime recovery safety.

---

# Runtime Recovery Behavior

Rollback commonly includes:
- traffic shifting
- replica replacement
- connection draining
- and runtime reconciliation.

---

# Operational Challenges

Unsafe rollback behavior may create:
- traffic amplification
- unhealthy routing
- and cascading instability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Rollback safety strongly impacts:
- deployment survivability.

---

# Key Insight

Rollback is runtime recovery orchestration for distributed systems.
