# Runtime Recovery Behavior

Distributed systems continuously recover from:
- runtime instability.

Recovery behavior directly impacts:
- deployment survivability
- and workload availability.

---

# Why Recovery Matters

Deployment failures may introduce:
- unhealthy replicas
- failed traffic routing
- and communication instability.

Recovery operations must remain:
- controlled
- observable
- and safe.

---

# Runtime Recovery Coordination

Recovery commonly includes:
- rollback sequencing
- traffic redistribution
- and workload replacement.

---

# Operational Challenges

Unsafe recovery behavior may destabilize:
- healthy runtime environments.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Recovery coordination becomes:
- survivability engineering for deployments.

---

# Key Insight

Reliable systems recover safely during runtime instability.
