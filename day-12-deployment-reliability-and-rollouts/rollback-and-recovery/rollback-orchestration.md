# Rollback Orchestration

Rollback orchestration coordinates:
- safe runtime recovery after deployment failure.

Rollback behavior must remain:
- gradual
- observable
- and traffic-aware.

---

# Why Orchestration Matters

Rollback itself may introduce:
- synchronization instability
- unhealthy traffic routing
- and runtime disruption.

---

# Runtime Recovery Coordination

Rollback orchestration continuously manages:
- traffic shifting
- workload replacement
- readiness validation
- and runtime reconciliation.

---

# Operational Benefits

Coordinated rollback improves:
- deployment survivability
- failure containment
- and runtime consistency.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Rollback orchestration becomes:
- controlled runtime recovery engineering.

---

# Key Insight

Reliable rollback behavior requires coordinated runtime recovery.
