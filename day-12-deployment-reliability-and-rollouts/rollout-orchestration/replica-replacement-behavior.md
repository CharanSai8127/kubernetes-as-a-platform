# Replica Replacement Behavior

Deployments continuously replace:
- outdated replicas
with:
- updated replicas.

Replacement behavior directly impacts:
- runtime stability.

---

# Why Replacement Matters

Unsafe replacement may create:
- traffic instability
- synchronization delays
- and unhealthy runtime states.

---

# Runtime Deployment Operations

Kubernetes gradually performs:
- replica termination
- startup sequencing
- and readiness validation.

---

# Operational Benefits

Controlled replacement improves:
- deployment reliability
- workload availability
- and runtime consistency.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Replica replacement becomes:
- deployment survivability engineering.

---

# Key Insight

Replica replacement should remain gradual and observable during deployments.
