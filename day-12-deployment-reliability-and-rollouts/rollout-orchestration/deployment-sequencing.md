# Deployment Sequencing

Distributed deployments require:
- predictable rollout sequencing.

Sequencing controls:
- startup order
- replacement order
- and traffic exposure timing.

---

# Why Sequencing Matters

Improper sequencing may create:
- unhealthy traffic routing
- synchronization instability
- and deployment failures.

---

# Runtime Rollout Coordination

Kubernetes continuously coordinates:
- workload startup
- readiness checks
- and rollout progression.

---

# Operational Challenges

Unsafe sequencing may impact:
- runtime availability
- communication stability
- and dependency health.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Deployment sequencing strongly impacts:
- runtime rollout safety.

---

# Key Insight

Safe deployments require predictable rollout sequencing behavior.
