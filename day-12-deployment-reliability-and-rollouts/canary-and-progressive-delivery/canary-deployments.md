# Canary Deployments

Canary deployments expose:
- small portions of traffic
to:
- new application versions first.

This allows:
- runtime validation under real traffic conditions.

---

# Why Canary Deployments Matter

Unsafe deployments may create:
- widespread outages
- traffic instability
- and dependency failures.

Canary delivery limits:
- failure exposure.

---

# Runtime Deployment Behavior

Traffic gradually shifts toward:
- validated healthy replicas.

If instability appears:
- rollout progression stops
or:
- rollback begins automatically.

---

# Operational Benefits

Canary deployments improve:
- deployment safety
- observability
- and runtime survivability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Canary rollouts become:
- runtime failure-containment mechanisms.

---

# Key Insight

Small deployment exposure reduces operational risk significantly.
