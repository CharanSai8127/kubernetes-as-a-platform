# Progressive Delivery Overview

Progressive delivery gradually exposes:
- new application versions
to:
- production traffic.

Instead of replacing all replicas immediately,
traffic shifts:
- incrementally
- observably
- and safely.

---

# Why Progressive Delivery Matters

Large deployments introduce:
- significant runtime risk.

Gradual rollout behavior reduces:
- deployment blast radius
- runtime instability
- and rollback complexity.

---

# Runtime Traffic Coordination

Traffic commonly shifts through:
- staged rollout percentages
such as:
- 5%
- 20%
- 50%
- and 100%.

---

# Operational Benefits

Progressive delivery improves:
- deployment survivability
- runtime observability
- and failure containment.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Progressive delivery becomes:
- controlled runtime risk management.

---

# Key Insight

Do not expose all users to deployment risk simultaneously.
