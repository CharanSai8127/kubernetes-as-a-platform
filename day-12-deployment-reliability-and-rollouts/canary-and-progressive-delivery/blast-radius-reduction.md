# Blast Radius Reduction

Blast radius represents:
- how much of the system becomes affected during failure.

Deployment safety focuses on:
- minimizing failure impact scope.

---

# Why Blast Radius Matters

Unsafe deployments may destabilize:
- healthy workloads
- dependencies
- and communication paths.

Smaller blast radius improves:
- survivability.

---

# Runtime Failure Isolation

Canary deployments and traffic isolation help:
- contain deployment instability locally.

---

# Operational Benefits

Blast-radius reduction improves:
- deployment recovery
- rollback safety
- and runtime resilience.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Distributed systems survive better when failures remain isolated.

---

# Key Insight

A bad deployment should not destabilize the entire platform.
