# Zero Downtime Deployments

Reliable systems should continue serving:
- healthy traffic during deployments.

Zero downtime deployment behavior focuses on:
- uninterrupted runtime availability.

---

# Why Downtime Happens

Applications may become unavailable because of:
- unsafe rollout behavior
- unhealthy replicas
- or traffic instability.

---

# Runtime Deployment Safety

Traffic should only reach:
- healthy
- synchronized
- and ready workloads.

---

# Operational Challenges

Deployment instability may create:
- connection failures
- request drops
- and degraded user experience.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Deployment safety strongly depends on:
- traffic-aware runtime coordination.

---

# Key Insight

Runtime deployments should evolve without disrupting healthy traffic.
