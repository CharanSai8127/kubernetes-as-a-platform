# Controlled Replacement Strategies

Distributed deployments continuously replace:
- unhealthy
or:
- outdated replicas.

Controlled replacement improves:
- runtime safety.

---

# Why Controlled Replacement Matters

Unsafe replacement behavior may create:
- traffic instability
- synchronization failures
- and unhealthy runtime states.

---

# Runtime Deployment Coordination

Kubernetes gradually coordinates:
- replica termination
- replacement
- and readiness validation.

---

# Operational Benefits

Controlled replacement reduces:
- deployment blast radius
- runtime instability
- and traffic disruption.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Replica replacement becomes:
- coordinated deployment survivability engineering.

---

# Key Insight

Safe replacement behavior protects runtime stability during deployments.
