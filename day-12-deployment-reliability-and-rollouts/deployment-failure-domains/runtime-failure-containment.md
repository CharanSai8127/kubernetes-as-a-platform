# Runtime Failure Containment

Distributed systems continuously experience:
- deployment instability.

Containment ensures:
- failures remain isolated from healthy workloads.

---

# Why Containment Matters

Unsafe deployments may create:
- cascading failures
- dependency overload
- and unhealthy traffic amplification.

---

# Runtime Protection Mechanisms

Containment commonly uses:
- progressive delivery
- rollback automation
- traffic-aware routing
- and deployment isolation.

---

# Operational Benefits

Containment improves:
- deployment survivability
- runtime stability
- and operational resilience.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Failure containment becomes:
- runtime survivability engineering.

---

# Key Insight

Healthy systems should remain protected from unstable deployments.
