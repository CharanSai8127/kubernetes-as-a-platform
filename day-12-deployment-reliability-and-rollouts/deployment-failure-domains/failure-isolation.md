# Failure Isolation

Failure isolation prevents:
- deployment instability
from spreading across:
- healthy runtime systems.

Isolation improves:
- deployment survivability.

---

# Why Isolation Matters

Without isolation:
- retries amplify
- dependencies overload
- and runtime instability spreads rapidly.

---

# Runtime Deployment Protection

Isolation mechanisms commonly include:
- canary rollouts
- traffic segmentation
- deployment boundaries
- and workload separation.

---

# Operational Benefits

Failure isolation improves:
- runtime recovery
- traffic stability
- and rollback safety.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Deployment safety strongly depends on:
- localized runtime failure behavior.

---

# Key Insight

Failures should remain locally contained during deployments.
