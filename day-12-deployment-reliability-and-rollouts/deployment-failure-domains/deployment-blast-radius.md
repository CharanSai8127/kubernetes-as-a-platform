# Deployment Blast Radius

Blast radius represents:
- how much of the platform becomes affected during deployment failure.

Smaller blast radius improves:
- survivability.

---

# Why Blast Radius Matters

Large deployment exposure may destabilize:
- healthy workloads
- dependencies
- and communication paths simultaneously.

---

# Runtime Deployment Risks

Unsafe rollout behavior may create:
- widespread outages
- cascading retries
- and runtime communication instability.

---

# Operational Benefits

Blast-radius reduction improves:
- deployment recovery
- rollback flexibility
- and runtime reliability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Distributed systems require:
- localized failure impact during runtime change.

---

# Key Insight

A bad deployment should not destabilize the entire platform.
