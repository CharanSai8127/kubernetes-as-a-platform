# Runtime Scaling Behavior

Distributed systems continuously scale during:
- deployments
- traffic spikes
- and runtime instability.

Scaling behavior directly impacts:
- deployment survivability.

---

# Why Scaling Matters

Unsafe scaling may create:
- unhealthy replicas
- resource exhaustion
- and traffic instability.

---

# Runtime Deployment Coordination

Kubernetes continuously manages:
- replica scaling
- rollout progression
- and workload availability.

---

# Operational Benefits

Controlled scaling improves:
- deployment reliability
- traffic stability
- and runtime resilience.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Scaling behavior becomes:
- runtime survivability engineering.

---

# Key Insight

Scaling during deployments should remain gradual and observable.
