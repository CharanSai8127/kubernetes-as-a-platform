# Deployment Capacity Management

Deployments continuously require:
- sufficient runtime capacity.

Capacity planning directly impacts:
- rollout safety
- workload availability
- and runtime stability.

---

# Why Capacity Matters

Insufficient runtime resources may create:
- pending workloads
- degraded application behavior
- and deployment failures.

---

# Runtime Deployment Coordination

Capacity management continuously considers:
- replica scaling
- resource consumption
- and rollout progression.

---

# Operational Benefits

Proper capacity management improves:
- deployment reliability
- runtime resilience
- and workload survivability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

- runtime deployment engineering.
