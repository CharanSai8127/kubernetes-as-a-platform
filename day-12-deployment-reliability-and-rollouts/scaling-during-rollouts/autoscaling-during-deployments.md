# Autoscaling During Deployments

Applications may continuously scale while:
- deployments remain active.

Autoscaling introduces:
- additional runtime coordination complexity.

---

# Why Autoscaling Matters

Simultaneous rollout and scaling behavior may create:
- replica instability
- traffic imbalance
- and deployment unpredictability.

---

# Runtime Deployment Coordination

Autoscaling continuously interacts with:
- rollout orchestration
- readiness validation
- and traffic routing behavior.

---

# Operational Challenges

Improper scaling coordination may create:
- unstable traffic exposure
- cascading failures
- and unhealthy deployments.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Autoscaling strongly impacts:
- deployment survivability engineering.

---

# Key Insight

Scaling and deployments continuously influence each other during runtime.
