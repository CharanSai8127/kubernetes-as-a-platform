# Traffic Cutover Behavior

Traffic cutover controls:
- when production traffic moves toward new deployments.

Cutover timing directly impacts:
- deployment safety.

---

# Why Cutover Matters

Premature traffic switching may create:
- failed requests
- unhealthy routing
- and cascading instability.

---

# Runtime Deployment Coordination

Traffic cutover should occur only after:
- readiness validation
- dependency verification
- and runtime health checks succeed.

---

# Operational Challenges

Unsafe cutover behavior may destabilize:
- healthy workloads
- dependencies
- and runtime communication paths.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Traffic cutover becomes:
- deployment survivability coordination.

---

# Key Insight

Traffic movement should remain controlled and observable during deployments.
