# Runtime Rollout Visibility

Distributed systems continuously evolve during:
- deployments.

Visibility improves:
- rollout awareness
- traffic understanding
- and runtime safety.

---

# Why Visibility Matters

Unsafe deployments may create:
- unhealthy replicas
- traffic instability
- and cascading failures silently.

---

# Runtime Deployment Monitoring

Visibility commonly includes:
- rollout events
- deployment progress
- readiness behavior
- and runtime synchronization.

---

# Operational Challenges

Limited visibility may delay:
- rollback decisions
- failure containment
- and runtime recovery.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Runtime visibility strongly impacts:
- deployment survivability engineering.

---

# Key Insight

Observable deployments recover faster during runtime instability.
