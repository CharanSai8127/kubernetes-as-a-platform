# Drift Detection And Reconciliation

Operational drift occurs when:
- runtime environments diverge
from:
- the desired deployment state.

GitOps continuously detects and reconciles:
- this drift automatically.

---

# Why Drift Matters

Manual infrastructure changes may create:
- inconsistent deployments
- operational instability
- and debugging complexity.

---

# Reconciliation Behavior

GitOps controllers continuously compare:
- desired state in Git
with:
- actual cluster state.

Differences trigger:
- reconciliation behavior.

---

# Operational Benefits

Drift detection improves:
- deployment consistency
- operational visibility
- and infrastructure reliability.

---

# Production Reliability

Continuous reconciliation reduces:
- configuration drift
- and uncontrolled cluster mutation.

---

# Key Insight

Declarative reconciliation improves long-term operational consistency significantly.
