# Environment Promotion

CD systems manage:
- controlled promotion across environments.

Applications commonly move through:
- development
- staging
- and production environments.

---

# Why Promotion Matters

Deployments should not move directly into:
- production environments

without:
- validation
- testing
- and operational verification.

---

# Controlled Rollout Flow

Validated artifact created
→ Staging deployment
→ Verification and observability
→ Production promotion
→ Gradual rollout

---

# Operational Benefits

Environment promotion improves:
- deployment confidence
- rollback safety
- and production stability.

---

# Deployment Risk Reduction

Gradual promotion reduces:
- blast radius during failures.

This improves:
- operational survivability.

---

# Key Insight

Controlled environment progression reduces production deployment risk significantly.
