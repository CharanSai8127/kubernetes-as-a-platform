# Deployment Control And Governance

Production deployments require:
- operational governance
- and controlled rollout behavior.

Deployment systems should never operate:
- without visibility
- ownership
- or auditability.

---

# Why Governance Matters

Every deployment may impact:
- availability
- performance
- security
- and operational stability.

Controlled deployments reduce:
- production risk.

---

# Common Governance Mechanisms

Examples include:
- approval workflows
- deployment reviews
- progressive rollouts
- environment promotion
- and deployment auditing.

---

# Operational Separation

Deployment systems operate at:
- production trust boundaries.

This requires:
- stricter access control
- and safer deployment ownership.

---

# Platform Reliability

Governed deployments improve:
- rollback safety
- operational visibility
- and production consistency.

---

# Key Insight

Deployment control is a production responsibility,
not only a pipeline activity.
