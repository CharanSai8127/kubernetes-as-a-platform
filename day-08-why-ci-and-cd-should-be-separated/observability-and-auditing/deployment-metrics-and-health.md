# Deployment Metrics And Health

Deployment systems require:
- operational health monitoring.

Metrics help evaluate:
- rollout stability
- application health
- and production impact.

---

# Common Deployment Metrics

Examples include:
- deployment duration
- pod readiness
- restart counts
- latency
- throughput
- and error rates.

---

# Why Metrics Matter

Successful rollout completion does not always mean:
- stable runtime behavior.

Metrics reveal:
- operational degradation during deployment.

---

# Runtime Validation

Production systems continuously validate:
- workload health
- dependency stability
- and infrastructure behavior.

---

# Operational Benefits

Deployment metrics improve:
- incident response
- rollback timing
- and operational confidence.

---

# Key Insight

Deployment success should be measured operationally, not only through pipeline completion.
