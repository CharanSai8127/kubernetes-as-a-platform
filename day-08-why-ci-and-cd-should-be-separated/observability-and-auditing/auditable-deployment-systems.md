# Auditable Deployment Systems

Production deployment systems should remain:
- traceable
- reviewable
- and auditable.

Operational visibility improves:
- governance
- accountability
- and recovery reliability.

---

# Why Auditing Matters

Without deployment auditing:
- identifying operational changes becomes difficult.

This increases:
- rollback complexity
- debugging difficulty
- and operational uncertainty.

---

# GitOps Advantage

GitOps naturally improves:
- deployment traceability.

Every deployment change becomes:
- version-controlled
- reviewable
- and historically visible.

---

# Operational Benefits

Auditable deployment systems improve:
- rollback precision
- deployment governance
- and infrastructure accountability.

---

# Security Importance

Auditing helps detect:
- unauthorized production mutation
- and infrastructure drift.

---

# Key Insight

Operational visibility is critical for reliable deployment governance.
