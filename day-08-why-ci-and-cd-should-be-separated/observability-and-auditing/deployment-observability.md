# Deployment Observability

Production deployments must remain:
- observable
- measurable
- and traceable.

Without observability:
- deployment instability becomes difficult to detect safely.

---

# Why Observability Matters

Deployments may introduce:
- latency spikes
- dependency saturation
- restart loops
- or failed rollout behavior.

Operational visibility helps identify:
- instability early.

---

# Common Deployment Signals

Examples include:
- pod restart counts
- deployment progress
- error rates
- latency metrics
- and dependency health.

---

# Rollout Validation

Deployment systems should continuously monitor:
- application behavior during rollout.

This improves:
- deployment confidence
- and rollback timing.

---

# Production Importance

Observability reduces:
- deployment uncertainty
- debugging complexity
- and outage duration.

---

# Key Insight

Safe deployments require continuous operational visibility during rollout behavior.
