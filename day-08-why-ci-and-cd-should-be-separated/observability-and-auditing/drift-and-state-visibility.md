# Drift And State Visibility

Operational environments continuously evolve through:
- deployments
- scaling
- infrastructure updates
- and manual changes.

Visibility into deployment state is critical for:
- operational consistency.

---

# What Is Drift

Drift occurs when:
- runtime environments diverge
from:
- intended deployment state.

---

# Why Drift Matters

Configuration drift may create:
- inconsistent environments
- failed rollouts
- debugging difficulty
- and rollback instability.

---

# GitOps Advantage

GitOps continuously compares:
- desired Git state
with:
- actual cluster state.

Differences trigger:
- reconciliation behavior.

---

# Operational Benefits

State visibility improves:
- deployment consistency
- governance
- and infrastructure reliability.

---

# Key Insight

Operational consistency depends heavily on visibility into desired and runtime infrastructure state.
