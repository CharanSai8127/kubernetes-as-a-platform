# Uncontrolled Rollouts

Uncontrolled deployments may introduce:
- large-scale operational instability.

Production environments should never receive:
- ungoverned deployment changes.

---

# Why This Matters

Large deployments may create:
- startup storms
- dependency overload
- retry amplification
- and cascading failures.

---

# Common Operational Risks

Examples include:
- deploying all replicas simultaneously
- broken configuration promotion
- and failed dependency migrations.

---

# Production Consequences

Uncontrolled rollouts may create:
- downtime
- latency spikes
- and failed recovery behavior.

---

# Safer Deployment Strategies

Production systems commonly use:
- canary deployments
- phased rollouts
- deployment pausing
- and observability validation.

---

# Key Insight

Controlled rollout behavior reduces operational blast radius during deployments.
