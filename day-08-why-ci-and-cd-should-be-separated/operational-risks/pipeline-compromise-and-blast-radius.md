# Pipeline Compromise And Blast Radius

CI pipelines frequently interact with:
- external dependencies
- repositories
- plugins
- and user-controlled code.

Compromised pipelines may therefore create:
- significant operational risk.

---

# Why Blast Radius Matters

If CI systems own:
- unrestricted production access,

then pipeline compromise may impact:
- entire environments.

---

# Common Risk Areas

Examples include:
- credential exposure
- malicious dependency injection
- unauthorized deployments
- and infrastructure mutation.

---

# Operational Consequences

Pipeline compromise may create:
- production instability
- security breaches
- or infrastructure drift.

---

# Mitigation Strategies

Platforms reduce blast radius using:
- least privilege access
- GitOps separation
- RBAC
- and isolated deployment ownership.

---

# Key Insight

Operational separation reduces the impact of compromised validation systems significantly.
