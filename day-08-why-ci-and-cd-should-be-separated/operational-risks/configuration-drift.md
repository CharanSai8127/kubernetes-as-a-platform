# Configuration Drift

Configuration drift occurs when:
- runtime infrastructure diverges
from:
- the intended deployment state.

This creates:
- inconsistent operational behavior.

---

# Why Drift Happens

Manual production changes frequently introduce:
- undocumented infrastructure mutations.

Over time this creates:
- environment inconsistency
- debugging difficulty
- and deployment unpredictability.

---

# Operational Risks

Configuration drift may create:
- failed rollouts
- inconsistent environments
- and rollback instability.

---

# GitOps Advantage

GitOps continuously reconciles:
- runtime environments
toward:
- declarative desired state.

This reduces:
- uncontrolled operational drift.

---

# Operational Benefits

Drift reduction improves:
- consistency
- auditability
- and deployment reliability.

---

# Key Insight

Declarative infrastructure management improves long-term operational consistency significantly.
