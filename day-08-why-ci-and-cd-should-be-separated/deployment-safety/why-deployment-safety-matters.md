# Why Deployment Safety Matters

Even validated artifacts may still create:
- unstable production deployments.

Deployment safety focuses on:
- reducing operational risk during rollout.

---

# Why Deployments Fail

Production failures may occur due to:
- configuration errors
- dependency instability
- startup amplification
- failed migrations
- or resource exhaustion.

---

# Deployment Risk

Every deployment introduces:
- operational uncertainty.

Even small changes may trigger:
- cascading failures
- latency spikes
- or dependency overload.

---

# Controlled Rollouts

Safe deployment systems use:
- gradual rollout behavior
- staged promotion
- rollback mechanisms
- and observability validation.

---

# Operational Importance

Deployment safety improves:
- availability
- recovery speed
- and production stability.

---

# Key Insight

Trusted artifacts still require safe rollout behavior before production mutation occurs.
