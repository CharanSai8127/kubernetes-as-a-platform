# Deployment Observability

Deployments must remain:
- observable
- measurable
- and auditable.

Without visibility:
- deployment failures become difficult to detect safely.

---

# Why Observability Matters

Deployments may create:
- latency spikes
- restart loops
- dependency saturation
- or failed rollouts.

Operational visibility helps identify:
- instability early.

---

# Common Deployment Signals

Examples include:
- error rates
- restart counts
- latency metrics
- rollout progress
- and dependency health.

---

# Rollout Validation

Deployment systems should continuously observe:
- application behavior during rollout.

This improves:
- deployment confidence
- and rollback timing.

---

# Production Importance

Observability reduces:
- deployment uncertainty
- and operational debugging complexity.

---

# Key Insight

Safe deployments require operational visibility throughout rollout behavior.
