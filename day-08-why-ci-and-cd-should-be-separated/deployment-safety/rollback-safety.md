# Rollback Safety

Rollback capability is one of the most important:
- deployment safety mechanisms.

Production systems must recover quickly from:
- unstable deployments.

---

# Why Rollbacks Matter

Even validated deployments may still introduce:
- operational failures.

Examples include:
- dependency saturation
- startup instability
- latency amplification
- or broken configurations.

---

# Operational Recovery

Rollback mechanisms help restore:
- previously stable deployment states.

This reduces:
- outage duration
- and operational pressure.

---

# GitOps Advantage

GitOps improves rollback behavior because:
- deployment state remains version-controlled in Git.

Recovery becomes:
- reverting desired state safely.

---

# Deployment Reliability

Reliable rollback behavior improves:
- production confidence
- deployment governance
- and operational survivability.

---

# Key Insight

Deployment recovery speed strongly impacts production reliability.
