# Progressive Rollouts

Progressive rollouts reduce:
- deployment blast radius.

Applications are deployed gradually instead of:
- replacing the entire workload immediately.

---

# Why Progressive Rollouts Matter

Large-scale deployments may create:
- dependency overload
- startup storms
- latency spikes
- and cascading failures.

Gradual rollout reduces:
- operational pressure.

---

# Common Rollout Strategies

Examples include:
- rolling updates
- canary deployments
- blue-green deployments
- and phased traffic shifting.

---

# Operational Benefits

Progressive rollout improves:
- observability
- rollback safety
- and production survivability.

---

# Failure Detection

Gradual deployments help identify:
- operational instability early

before:
- entire environments become impacted.

---

# Key Insight

Controlled rollout speed reduces deployment blast radius significantly.
