# Blast Radius Reduction

Safe deployment systems minimize:
- the scope of deployment failures.

This reduces:
- operational impact during rollout instability.

---

# Why Blast Radius Matters

Uncontrolled deployments may affect:
- entire environments simultaneously.

This increases:
- outage severity
- and recovery difficulty.

---

# Controlled Deployment Scope

Blast radius may be reduced using:
- phased rollouts
- canary deployments
- isolated environments
- and traffic segmentation.

---

# Operational Benefits

Blast-radius control improves:
- rollback speed
- observability
- and deployment survivability.

---

# Kubernetes Importance

Distributed systems require:
- controlled rollout behavior

because:
- deployments may trigger cascading operational pressure.

---

# Key Insight

Deployment safety improves when failures remain operationally isolated instead of globally distributed.
