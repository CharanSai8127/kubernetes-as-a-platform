# Why Rollbacks Matter

Even validated deployments may still create:
- operational instability in production.

Rollback mechanisms help restore:
- previously stable deployment states safely.

---

# Common Deployment Failures

Examples include:
- broken configurations
- dependency overload
- startup instability
- failed migrations
- and runtime crashes.

---

# Why Recovery Speed Matters

Long recovery duration increases:
- outage impact
- operational pressure
- and cascading failures.

Fast rollback improves:
- production survivability.

---

# Operational Safety

Reliable deployment systems must support:
- predictable rollback behavior.

Rollback should never require:
- rebuilding infrastructure manually during outages.

---

# GitOps Advantage

Git-based deployment state improves:
- rollback consistency
because:
- previous desired states remain version-controlled.

---

# Key Insight

Deployment recovery speed directly impacts operational reliability.
