# Operational Ownership

Production deployments require:
- clear operational ownership.

This improves:
- accountability
- governance
- and deployment safety.

---

# Why Ownership Matters

Without controlled ownership:
- deployments may become:
  - inconsistent
  - unaudited
  - or operationally unsafe.

---

# CI Ownership

CI owns:
- validation
- testing
- scanning
- and artifact generation.

---

# CD Ownership

CD owns:
- deployment reconciliation
- rollout control
- rollback handling
- and production mutation.

---

# GitOps Perspective

GitOps improves ownership by making:
- Git repositories
the:
- operational source of truth.

This creates:
- visibility
- auditability
- and deployment consistency.

---

# Key Insight

Operational ownership improves deployment reliability and reduces uncontrolled production mutation.
