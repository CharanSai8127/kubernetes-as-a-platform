# RBAC And Operational Isolation

Role-Based Access Control improves:
- operational separation
- and infrastructure security.

RBAC ensures:
- systems only receive authorized permissions.

---

# Why RBAC Matters

Without controlled access boundaries:
- systems may gain:
  - excessive infrastructure permissions.

This increases:
- blast radius during compromise.

---

# Kubernetes Importance

Kubernetes environments rely heavily on:
- RBAC
to control:
- workload permissions
- deployment access
- and cluster operations.

---

# Operational Isolation

CI systems
CD systems
operators
and workloads

should each receive:
- isolated operational roles.

---

# Security Benefits

RBAC improves:
- auditability
- governance
- deployment safety
- and operational accountability.

---

# Key Insight

Operational isolation improves production security by minimizing unnecessary infrastructure permissions.
