# Secrets And Sensitive Access

CI/CD systems frequently interact with:
- credentials
- tokens
- registries
- and deployment secrets.

Protecting sensitive access is critical for:
- production security.

---

# Why Secrets Matter

Exposed credentials may allow:
- unauthorized deployments
- infrastructure compromise
- or data exposure.

---

# Common Sensitive Assets

Examples include:
- Kubernetes credentials
- cloud access keys
- registry tokens
- and GitOps authentication secrets.

---

# Operational Risks

Improper secret management may create:
- privilege escalation
- operational drift
- and uncontrolled production mutation.

---

# Secure Access Practices

Production systems commonly use:
- secret managers
- short-lived credentials
- role-based access control
- and workload identity separation.

---

# Key Insight

Sensitive deployment access should remain tightly controlled and operationally isolated.
