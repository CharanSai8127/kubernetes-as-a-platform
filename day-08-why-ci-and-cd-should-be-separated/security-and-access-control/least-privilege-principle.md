# Least Privilege Principle

Least privilege is one of the most important:
- security principles in platform engineering.

Every system should only receive:
- the minimum required permissions.

---

# Why Least Privilege Matters

Excessive permissions increase:
- operational blast radius.

Compromised systems with unrestricted access may:
- mutate infrastructure
- expose secrets
- or impact production environments.

---

# CI/CD Importance

CI and CD operate at:
- different trust boundaries.

They should therefore receive:
- different operational permissions.

---

# Operational Benefits

Least privilege improves:
- deployment safety
- operational isolation
- and infrastructure security.

---

# Kubernetes Perspective

Kubernetes environments should minimize:
- unrestricted cluster access.

This improves:
- auditability
- governance
- and production survivability.

---

# Key Insight

Operational security improves when systems only receive the permissions absolutely required for their responsibilities.
