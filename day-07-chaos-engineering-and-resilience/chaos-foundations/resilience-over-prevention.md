# Resilience Over Prevention

Distributed systems cannot eliminate all failures.

Instead,
modern platforms focus on:
- resilience
- survivability
- and graceful recovery.

---

# Prevention Has Limits

Even well-designed systems experience:
- cloud outages
- network instability
- software bugs
- infrastructure degradation
- and dependency failures.

Preventing every failure is impossible.

---

# Resilience Thinking

Resilient systems:
- isolate failures
- reduce blast radius
- recover automatically
- and degrade gracefully.

The goal becomes:
- operational survivability.

---

# Graceful Degradation

Good systems continue serving:
- partial functionality

instead of:
- collapsing completely.

Examples:
- serving cached data
- queueing requests
- or temporarily rejecting low-priority traffic.

---

# Key Insight

The strongest systems are not systems that never fail.

They are systems that recover predictably under pressure.
