# Blast Radius Control

Chaos experiments should begin with:
- limited operational scope.

This reduces:
- production risk
during:
- resilience validation.

---

# Why Blast Radius Matters

Uncontrolled failure injection may create:
- unintended outages
- cascading failures
- or infrastructure instability.

Chaos engineering must remain:
- controlled
- and observable.

---

# Operational Isolation

Blast radius may be reduced using:
- namespaces
- isolated workloads
- traffic segmentation
- node isolation
- or staged experiments.

---

# Progressive Validation

Chaos testing should evolve gradually:

small failures
→ isolated dependencies
→ partial production validation
→ larger resilience testing

---

# Observability Importance

Experiments require:
- metrics
- tracing
- logging
- and operational visibility

to safely evaluate:
- recovery behavior.

---

# Key Insight

Resilience validation should minimize operational risk while maximizing failure visibility.
