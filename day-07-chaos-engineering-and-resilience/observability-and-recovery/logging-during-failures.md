# Logging During Failures

Logs help explain:
- operational behavior
during:
- instability
- recovery
- and cascading failures.

---

# Why Logs Matter

Metrics may show:
- symptoms.

Logs frequently reveal:
- root operational causes.

Examples:
- dependency failures
- timeout events
- retry amplification
- or leader-election instability.

---

# Failure Challenges

During large outages:
- log volume increases dramatically.

This may create:
- storage pressure
- noisy operational signals
- and debugging difficulty.

---

# Distributed-System Complexity

Failures propagate across:
- multiple services
- infrastructure layers
- and dependencies.

Centralized logging improves:
- operational correlation.

---

# Operational Benefits

Logging supports:
- incident debugging
- recovery analysis
- and resilience validation.

---

# Key Insight

Operational recovery depends heavily on understanding failure sequences across distributed systems.
