# Failure Observability

Distributed-system failures must be:
- observable
- measurable
- and debuggable.

Without observability:
- failures become operationally invisible.

---

# Why Observability Matters

Modern systems fail through:
- latency
- retries
- dependency instability
- and partial outages.

These failures are difficult to detect without:
- operational telemetry.

---

# Core Signals

Failure observability relies on:
- metrics
- logs
- traces
- and events.

Together these provide:
- distributed-system visibility.

---

# Operational Visibility

Observability helps identify:
- failure propagation
- bottleneck migration
- retry amplification
- and dependency saturation.

---

# Distributed-System Challenge

Failures often appear:
- partially
- intermittently
- and asynchronously.

This makes:
- end-to-end visibility critical.

---

# Key Insight

Distributed systems cannot be stabilized reliably without operational visibility into failure behavior.
