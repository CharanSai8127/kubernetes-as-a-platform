# Graceful Degradation

Graceful degradation allows systems to:
- continue operating partially
during:
- dependency failures
- overload
- or infrastructure instability.

---

# Why This Matters

Distributed systems cannot guarantee:
- full functionality during every failure.

Instead resilient systems prioritize:
- core operational behavior.

---

# Examples

Examples include:
- serving cached responses
- disabling non-critical features
- queueing requests
- or rejecting low-priority traffic.

---

# Operational Benefits

Graceful degradation helps:
- reduce cascading failures
- preserve availability
- and maintain partial functionality.

---

# Failure Isolation

Systems degrade:
- selectively
instead of:
- collapsing completely.

---

# Key Insight

Reliable distributed systems prioritize survivability during failures instead of perfect functionality.
