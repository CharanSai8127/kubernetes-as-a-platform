# Blast Radius Reduction

Blast radius defines:
- how far failures propagate across systems.

Reducing blast radius improves:
- operational containment
- and resilience.

---

# Why Blast Radius Matters

Without isolation:
- small failures may amplify into:
  - large-scale outages.

Distributed systems require:
- failure boundaries.

---

# Common Isolation Strategies

Examples include:
- workload isolation
- namespaces
- node pools
- topology spread
- queues
- and dependency segmentation.

---

# Kubernetes Support

Kubernetes helps reduce blast radius through:
- anti-affinity
- topology constraints
- resource quotas
- and workload separation.

---

# Operational Benefits

Blast-radius control improves:
- survivability
- debugging
- and recovery speed.

---

# Key Insight

Resilient systems isolate operational failures before instability spreads globally.
