# StatefulSets Overview

StatefulSets manage:
- stateful workloads inside Kubernetes.

Unlike Deployments,
StatefulSets provide:
- stable identity
- ordered deployment
- and persistent storage association.

---

# Why StatefulSets Matter

Distributed databases continuously depend on:
- predictable identity behavior.

Examples include:
- PostgreSQL
- Kafka
- Elasticsearch
- and Cassandra.

---

# Runtime Identity Stability

StatefulSets preserve:
- stable hostnames
- stable storage
- and predictable network identity.

---

# Operational Benefits

Stable identity improves:
- replication consistency
- failover coordination
- and runtime predictability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

StatefulSets become:
- identity orchestration for distributed systems.

---

# Key Insight

Distributed stateful systems require predictable identity consistency.
