# Database Replica Identity

Distributed databases continuously depend on:
- replica identity consistency.

Each database replica commonly requires:
- stable hostname
- stable storage
- and predictable communication behavior.

---

# Why Replica Identity Matters

Replica identity instability may create:
- replication failures
- synchronization delays
- and quorum instability.

---

# Runtime Replica Coordination

Database replicas continuously exchange:
- replication traffic
- synchronization updates
- and consistency coordination.

---

# Operational Challenges

Replica instability may create:
- stale replicas
- recovery failures
- and distributed inconsistency.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Stable replica identity strongly impacts:
- distributed database survivability.

---

# Key Insight

Distributed databases require predictable replica identity behavior.
