# Stable Network Identity

Stateful systems continuously depend on:
- predictable communication endpoints.

Distributed databases commonly require:
- stable hostnames
- and stable peer discovery.

---

# Why Stable Identity Matters

Changing network identity may create:
- replication instability
- synchronization failures
- and cluster coordination issues.

---

# Runtime Communication

Distributed replicas continuously exchange:
- synchronization traffic
- quorum communication
- and consistency updates.

---

# Operational Benefits

Stable network identity improves:
- peer discovery
- replication reliability
- and failover consistency.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Identity stability strongly impacts:
- distributed-system survivability.

---

# Key Insight

Stateful systems require predictable runtime communication identity.
