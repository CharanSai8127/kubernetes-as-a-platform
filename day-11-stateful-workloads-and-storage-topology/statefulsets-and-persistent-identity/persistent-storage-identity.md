# Persistent Storage Identity

Stateful workloads continuously depend on:
- persistent storage association.

Each replica commonly requires:
- dedicated durable storage.

---

# Why Storage Identity Matters

Changing storage identity may create:
- inconsistent state
- replica instability
- and runtime corruption risks.

---

# Runtime Storage Association

StatefulSets preserve:
- predictable volume ownership
across:
- restarts
- rescheduling
- and failover operations.

---

# Operational Benefits

Persistent storage identity improves:
- recovery consistency
- data survivability
- and replica predictability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Storage identity stability directly impacts:
- distributed-system consistency.

---

# Key Insight

Stateful replicas require stable storage ownership.
