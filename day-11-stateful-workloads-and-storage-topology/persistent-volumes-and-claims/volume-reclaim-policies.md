# Volume Reclaim Policies

Persistent storage may behave differently after:
- workload deletion.

Reclaim policies define:
- what happens to storage after release.

---

# Common Reclaim Behaviors

Storage may:
- remain preserved
- become reusable
- or get deleted automatically.

---

# Why Reclaim Policies Matter

Improper reclaim behavior may create:
- orphaned storage
- accidental data loss
- or infrastructure waste.

---

# Runtime Survivability

Correct reclaim policies improve:
- recovery consistency
- storage durability
- and operational safety.

---

# Operational Challenges

Storage lifecycle cleanup must remain:
- predictable
- observable
- and recoverable.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Storage reclaim behavior directly impacts:
- long-term state survivability.

---

# Key Insight

Persistent storage lifecycle management requires controlled reclaim behavior.
