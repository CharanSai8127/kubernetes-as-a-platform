# Topology-Aware Provisioning

Storage provisioning must remain aware of:
- infrastructure topology.

Persistent storage commonly exists within:
- specific nodes
- availability zones
- or storage domains.

---

# Why Topology Awareness Matters

Improper storage placement may create:
- attachment failures
- increased latency
- and runtime instability.

---

# Runtime Storage Constraints

Provisioning must consider:
- zone availability
- attachment accessibility
- and scheduling compatibility.

---

# Operational Benefits

Topology-aware provisioning improves:
- workload survivability
- failover consistency
- and storage locality.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Storage provisioning becomes:
- topology-aware infrastructure orchestration.

---

# Key Insight

Storage topology directly impacts stateful workload reliability.
