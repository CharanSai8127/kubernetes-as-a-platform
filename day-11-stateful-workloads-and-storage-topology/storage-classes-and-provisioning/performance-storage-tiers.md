# Performance Storage Tiers

Different workloads require different:
- storage performance characteristics.

Storage tiers commonly vary by:
- latency
- throughput
- durability
- and cost.

---

# Why Performance Matters

Databases and stateful systems continuously depend on:
- predictable storage performance.

Poor storage performance may create:
- slow queries
- replication lag
- and runtime instability.

---

# Runtime Storage Behavior

Performance-sensitive workloads require:
- low latency
- stable throughput
- and reliable storage responsiveness.

---

# Operational Challenges

Improper storage selection may create:
- operational bottlenecks
- scaling instability
- and degraded application behavior.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Storage performance directly impacts:
- distributed-system runtime behavior.

---

# Key Insight

Stateful workloads are highly sensitive to storage performance behavior.
