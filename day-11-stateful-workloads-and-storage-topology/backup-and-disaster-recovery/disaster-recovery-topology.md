# Disaster Recovery Topology

Disaster recovery requires:
- distributed recovery architecture.

Stateful systems continuously depend on:
- recoverable infrastructure topology.

---

# Why Recovery Topology Matters

If workloads and backups exist within:
- the same failure domain,
both may fail together.

This creates:
- survivability risks.

---

# Runtime Recovery Design

Disaster recovery commonly includes:
- cross-zone redundancy
- cross-region backups
- and distributed replication.

---

# Operational Challenges

Recovery topology instability may create:
- delayed failover
- synchronization complexity
- and recovery unpredictability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Disaster recovery strongly depends on:
- topology-aware survivability engineering.

---

# Key Insight

Reliable recovery requires distributed failure-domain isolation.
