# Storage Backup Strategies

Stateful systems continuously depend on:
- recoverable persistent state.

Backups protect workloads from:
- data loss
- corruption
- and infrastructure failures.

---

# Why Backups Matter

Stateful workloads cannot recover safely if:
- persistent state disappears permanently.

Backups improve:
- survivability
- recoverability
- and operational resilience.

---

# Runtime Recovery Requirements

Backup strategies commonly include:
- snapshots
- replication
- scheduled backups
- and offsite recovery copies.

---

# Operational Challenges

Improper backup design may create:
- unrecoverable state
- inconsistent recovery
- and prolonged downtime.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Backups become:
- survivability engineering for distributed systems.

---

# Key Insight

Reliable backups are critical for stateful workload survivability.
