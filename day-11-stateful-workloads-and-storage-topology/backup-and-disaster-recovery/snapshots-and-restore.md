# Snapshots And Restore

Snapshots capture:
- point-in-time storage state.

They improve:
- recovery speed
- rollback safety
- and disaster recovery operations.

---

# Why Snapshots Matter

Distributed systems continuously modify:
- runtime state.

Snapshots help preserve:
- recoverable storage consistency.

---

# Runtime Recovery Operations

Restore workflows commonly include:
- volume recreation
- storage attachment
- and state synchronization recovery.

---

# Operational Challenges

Improper restore operations may create:
- stale state
- inconsistent replicas
- and synchronization instability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Snapshot recovery strongly impacts:
- runtime storage survivability.

---

# Key Insight

Snapshots improve recoverability during storage instability and failures.
