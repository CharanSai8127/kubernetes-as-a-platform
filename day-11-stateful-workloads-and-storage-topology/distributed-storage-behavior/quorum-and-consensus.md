# Quorum And Consensus

Distributed storage systems continuously coordinate:
- state agreement between replicas.

Quorum mechanisms help ensure:
- consistent distributed decision making.

---

# Why Quorum Matters

Distributed systems continuously experience:
- node failures
- communication delays
- and network instability.

Without quorum coordination:
- inconsistent state may emerge.

---

# Runtime Coordination

Distributed replicas continuously exchange:
- acknowledgements
- synchronization updates
- and consistency validation traffic.

---

# Operational Challenges

Quorum instability may create:
- split-brain behavior
- stale replicas
- and inconsistent writes.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Distributed consistency strongly depends on:
- reliable communication coordination.

---

# Key Insight

Consensus reliability directly impacts distributed storage survivability.
