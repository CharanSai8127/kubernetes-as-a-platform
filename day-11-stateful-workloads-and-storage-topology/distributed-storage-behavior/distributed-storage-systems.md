# Distributed Storage Systems

Modern storage platforms are themselves:
- distributed systems.

Storage continuously performs:
- replication
- synchronization
- failover coordination
- and consistency management.

---

# Why This Matters

Storage systems also fail through:
- communication instability.

This introduces:
- replication lag
- stale replicas
- and synchronization delays.

---

# Runtime Communication

Distributed storage continuously exchanges:
- replication traffic
- synchronization traffic
- and recovery operations.

---

# Operational Challenges

Storage instability may create:
- quorum failures
- inconsistent state
- and failover unpredictability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Storage reliability strongly depends on:
- communication consistency.

---

# Key Insight

Storage itself is a distributed system.
