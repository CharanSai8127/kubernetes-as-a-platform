# Storage Network Partitions

Distributed storage systems may experience:
- communication separation between replicas.

This is known as:
- network partitioning.

---

# Why Partitions Matter

Replicas may remain operational while:
- communication becomes unstable.

This creates:
- synchronization delays
- stale replicas
- and consistency risks.

---

# Runtime Communication Risks

Partitioned storage systems may struggle with:
- write coordination
- replication consistency
- and quorum stability.

---

# Operational Challenges

Network partitions may create:
- split-brain scenarios
- delayed failover
- and runtime unpredictability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Distributed storage reliability strongly depends on:
- stable communication paths.

---

# Key Insight

Communication instability directly impacts distributed storage consistency.
