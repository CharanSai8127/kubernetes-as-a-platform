# Topology-Aware Volume Allocation

Storage allocation must remain aware of:
- infrastructure topology.

Persistent storage commonly exists within:
- specific availability zones
- nodes
- or storage domains.

---

# Why Topology Awareness Matters

Improper allocation may create:
- attachment failures
- cross-zone latency
- and runtime instability.

---

# Runtime Allocation Constraints

Volume allocation continuously considers:
- topology compatibility
- storage accessibility
- and workload placement.

---

# Operational Benefits

Topology-aware allocation improves:
- storage locality
- workload survivability
- and failover predictability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Storage provisioning becomes:
- topology-aware infrastructure engineering.

---

# Key Insight

Storage topology directly impacts runtime allocation reliability.
