# Storage Lifecycle Automation

Persistent storage continuously moves through:
- provisioning
- attachment
- resizing
- recovery
- and reclamation phases.

Automation simplifies:
- runtime storage management.

---

# Why Automation Matters

Stateful systems continuously depend on:
- predictable storage operations.

Manual lifecycle handling may create:
- operational inconsistency
- delayed recovery
- and infrastructure drift.

---

# Runtime Storage Operations

Automated storage lifecycle management improves:
- provisioning consistency
- attachment reliability
- and failover recovery.

---

# Operational Challenges

Storage lifecycle failures may create:
- orphaned volumes
- attachment instability
- and recovery unpredictability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Storage automation directly impacts:
- operational survivability.

---

# Key Insight

Reliable storage lifecycle automation improves distributed-system stability.
