# Availability Zone Failures

Cloud infrastructure commonly distributes:
- workloads
- networking
- and storage
across:
- availability zones.

---

# Why Zone Failures Matter

If storage and workloads remain concentrated within:
- a single availability zone,
both may fail together.

This creates:
- survivability risks.

---

# Runtime Infrastructure Risks

Availability-zone failures may impact:
- storage accessibility
- workload recovery
- and replication stability.

---

# Operational Challenges

Cross-zone recovery may introduce:
- attachment delays
- synchronization lag
- and failover instability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Stateful systems require:
- topology-aware redundancy across failure domains.

---

# Key Insight

Availability-zone isolation improves storage survivability significantly.
