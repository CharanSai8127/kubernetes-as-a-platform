# Regional Storage Risks

Regional failures represent:
- large-scale infrastructure disruptions.

Examples include:
- regional outages
- cloud-control-plane instability
- and large-scale communication failures.

---

# Why Regional Risks Matter

Stateful systems continuously depend on:
- recoverable distributed state.

Regional failures may impact:
- storage accessibility
- replication behavior
- and disaster recovery operations.

---

# Runtime Recovery Complexity

Cross-region recovery commonly requires:
- replication coordination
- backup restoration
- and synchronization recovery.

---

# Operational Challenges

Regional recovery may create:
- increased latency
- delayed failover
- and operational complexity.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Regional survivability requires:
- distributed redundancy engineering.

---

# Key Insight

Distributed state survivability must extend beyond a single region.
