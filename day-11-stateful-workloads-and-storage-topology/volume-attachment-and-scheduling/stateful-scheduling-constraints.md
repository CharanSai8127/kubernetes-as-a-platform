# Stateful Scheduling Constraints

Stateful workloads cannot schedule freely across:
- infrastructure boundaries.

Scheduling must consider:
- storage locality
- attachment compatibility
- and topology constraints.

---

# Why Constraints Exist

Persistent storage commonly remains:
- physically constrained to infrastructure topology.

Improper scheduling may create:
- attachment failures
- increased latency
- and runtime instability.

---

# Runtime Scheduling Behavior

Scheduler continuously evaluates:
- node compatibility
- zone awareness
- storage accessibility
- and attachment rules.

---

# Operational Challenges

Stateful scheduling complexity may create:
- delayed failover
- pending workloads
- and operational unpredictability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Stateful scheduling becomes:
- topology-aware infrastructure orchestration.

---

# Key Insight

Stateful systems require constrained scheduling behavior for reliable operation.
