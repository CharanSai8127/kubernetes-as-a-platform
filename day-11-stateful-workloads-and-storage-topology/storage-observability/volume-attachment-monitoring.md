# Volume Attachment Monitoring

Persistent volumes continuously depend on:
- successful attachment behavior.

Monitoring attachment operations improves:
- runtime recovery visibility
- and operational stability.

---

# Why Attachment Visibility Matters

Attachment instability may create:
- delayed workload startup
- failover unpredictability
- and runtime failures.

---

# Runtime Storage Operations

Volume lifecycle monitoring commonly includes:
- attachment status
- detach operations
- mount recovery
- and scheduling behavior.

---

# Operational Challenges

Attachment failures may create:
- pending workloads
- storage recovery delays
- and infrastructure instability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Storage attachment visibility directly impacts:
- workload survivability.

---

# Key Insight

Reliable stateful recovery depends on observable storage attachment behavior.
