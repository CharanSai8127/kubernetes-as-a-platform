# Storage Replication Lag

Distributed storage continuously synchronizes:
- state across replicas.

Replication lag occurs when:
- synchronization becomes delayed.

---

# Why Replication Lag Matters

Delayed replication may create:
- stale reads
- inconsistent replicas
- and runtime unpredictability.

---

# Runtime Synchronization Risks

Replication depends heavily on:
- communication stability
- synchronization throughput
- and storage performance.

---

# Operational Challenges

Replication instability may create:
- delayed failover
- synchronization inconsistency
- and stale state visibility.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Replication reliability strongly depends on:
- communication coordination behavior.

---

# Key Insight

Distributed replication is highly sensitive to runtime communication delays.
