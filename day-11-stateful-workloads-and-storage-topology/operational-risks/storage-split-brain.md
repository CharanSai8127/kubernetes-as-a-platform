# Storage Split Brain

Split-brain occurs when:
- distributed storage replicas diverge into inconsistent states.

This commonly happens during:
- communication instability
- network partitions
- or quorum failures.

---

# Why Split Brain Matters

Multiple replicas may believe:
- they are authoritative simultaneously.

This creates:
- inconsistent writes
- stale replicas
- and data corruption risks.

---

# Runtime Coordination Risks

Distributed systems continuously depend on:
- reliable communication coordination.

Communication instability may disrupt:
- synchronization consistency.

---

# Operational Challenges

Split-brain recovery may require:
- manual reconciliation
- replica recovery
- and state correction.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Distributed storage reliability strongly depends on:
- communication consistency.

---

# Key Insight

Communication instability may create inconsistent distributed state behavior.
