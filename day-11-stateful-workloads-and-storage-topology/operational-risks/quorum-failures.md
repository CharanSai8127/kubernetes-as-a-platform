# Quorum Failures

Distributed storage systems continuously require:
- replica agreement.

Quorum failures happen when:
- replicas cannot coordinate consistently.

---

# Why Quorum Matters

Without quorum,
distributed systems may struggle with:
- write coordination
- synchronization
- and consistency validation.

---

# Runtime Communication Risks

Quorum reliability strongly depends on:
- stable communication between replicas.

Communication instability may create:
- delayed coordination
- inconsistent writes
- and stale replicas.

---

# Operational Challenges

Quorum instability may create:
- unavailable storage
- failover unpredictability
- and recovery complexity.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Distributed consistency strongly depends on:
- reliable quorum coordination.

---

# Key Insight

Quorum stability directly impacts distributed storage survivability.
