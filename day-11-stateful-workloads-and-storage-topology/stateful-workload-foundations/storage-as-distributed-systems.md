# Storage As Distributed Systems

Modern storage systems are themselves:
- distributed systems.

Storage continuously performs:
- replication
- synchronization
- failover coordination
- and consistency operations.

---

# Why This Matters

Storage systems also fail through:
- communication instability.

This introduces:
- replication lag
- split-brain scenarios
- stale replicas
- and quorum instability.

---

# Runtime Communication

Storage systems continuously exchange:
- synchronization traffic
- replication traffic
- and recovery operations.

---

# Operational Risks

Distributed storage introduces:
- additional operational complexity
compared to:
- stateless compute systems.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Storage reliability depends heavily on:
- communication consistency.

---

# Key Insight

Storage itself is a distributed system.
