# Persistent Storage Lifecycle

Persistent storage follows:
- a continuous lifecycle.

Volumes are:
- provisioned
- attached
- mounted
- detached
- reclaimed
- and recovered.

---

# Why Lifecycle Matters

Storage behavior continues even when:
- workloads restart
- nodes fail
- or scheduling changes.

---

# Runtime Storage Operations

Storage lifecycle operations commonly include:
- provisioning
- resizing
- failover recovery
- and cleanup.

---

# Operational Challenges

Storage lifecycle instability may create:
- attachment failures
- delayed recovery
- and runtime inconsistency.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Storage lifecycle management directly impacts:
- state survivability.

---

# Key Insight

Persistent storage remains operational beyond workload lifecycle changes.
