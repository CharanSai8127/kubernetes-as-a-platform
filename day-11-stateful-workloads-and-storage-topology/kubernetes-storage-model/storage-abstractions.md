# Storage Abstractions

Kubernetes uses:
- storage abstractions
to separate:
- workloads
from:
- physical storage infrastructure.

---

# Why Abstractions Matter

Applications should not directly manage:
- physical disks
- storage APIs
- or infrastructure dependencies.

---

# Core Abstractions

Kubernetes commonly abstracts storage through:
- Persistent Volumes
- Persistent Volume Claims
- and Storage Classes.

---

# Operational Benefits

Storage abstractions improve:
- portability
- automation
- and infrastructure consistency.

---

# Runtime Survivability

Storage abstraction simplifies:
- storage lifecycle management
during:
- scaling
- failover
- and recovery operations.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Storage abstractions improve:
- operational consistency across distributed environments.

---

# Key Insight

Kubernetes abstracts storage to simplify persistent infrastructure management.
