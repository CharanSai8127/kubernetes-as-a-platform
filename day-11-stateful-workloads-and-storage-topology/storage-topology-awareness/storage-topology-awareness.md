# Storage Topology Awareness

Storage topology awareness means:
- Kubernetes understands where storage physically exists.

Persistent storage commonly remains constrained to:
- nodes
- availability zones
- or storage domains.

---

# Why Topology Awareness Matters

Improper workload placement may create:
- attachment failures
- increased latency
- and runtime instability.

---

# Runtime Storage Constraints

Stateful workloads continuously depend on:
- topology-compatible storage access.

Scheduling must remain aware of:
- storage locality
- attachment accessibility
- and infrastructure topology.

---

# Operational Benefits

Topology awareness improves:
- workload survivability
- failover predictability
- and storage locality.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Storage topology directly impacts:
- runtime reliability for distributed systems.

---

# Key Insight

Storage-aware scheduling improves stateful workload survivability significantly.
