# Topology-Aware Scheduling

Stateful scheduling must remain aware of:
- storage topology constraints.

Unlike stateless workloads,
stateful systems cannot freely move across:
- infrastructure boundaries.

---

# Why Scheduling Complexity Exists

Storage may remain constrained by:
- zones
- nodes
- attachment behavior
- and storage accessibility.

---

# Runtime Scheduling Constraints

Scheduler must continuously consider:
- storage attachment compatibility
- topology locality
- and infrastructure availability.

---

# Operational Benefits

Topology-aware scheduling improves:
- storage accessibility
- failover consistency
- and workload survivability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Stateful scheduling becomes:
- constrained topology-aware orchestration.

---

# Key Insight

Stateful scheduling is constrained scheduling.
