# Shared Platform Challenges

As Kubernetes adoption grows,
multiple teams begin sharing:
- infrastructure
- services
- and operational tooling.

This creates:
- multi-tenant operational complexity.

---

# Why Shared Platforms Become Difficult

Different teams commonly require:
- different workloads
- different permissions
- different infrastructure needs
- and different operational requirements.

Without governance,
these requirements may conflict.

---

# Common Platform Risks

Examples include:
- resource starvation
- security violations
- operational inconsistency
- and excessive infrastructure consumption.

---

# Runtime Platform Impact

One team's actions may impact:
- cluster stability
- infrastructure economics
- and workload survivability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Shared platforms require:
- controlled operational boundaries.

---

# Key Insight

The larger the platform becomes, the more important governance becomes.
