# Platform Control Boundaries

Platform control boundaries define:
- what is allowed
- who can perform actions
- and where actions can occur.

These boundaries protect:
- platform stability
- security
- and operational consistency.

---

# Why Control Boundaries Matter

Without boundaries,
users may:
- deploy insecure workloads
- consume excessive resources
- bypass operational standards
- or impact other tenants.

---

# Common Control Areas

Platform boundaries commonly govern:
- access
- networking
- storage
- workload behavior
- and infrastructure consumption.

---

# Runtime Platform Protection

Control boundaries reduce:
- operational risk
- security risk
- and infrastructure instability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Control boundaries create:
- predictable platform behavior.

---

# Key Insight

Platform reliability depends on controlled operational boundaries.
