# Platform Guardrails Overview

Kubernetes is not simply:
- a container orchestration platform.

As organizations scale,
Kubernetes becomes:
- a shared platform.

Shared platforms require:
- governance
- isolation
- security controls
- and operational boundaries.

These controls are commonly referred to as:
- platform guardrails.

---

# Why Guardrails Matter

Multiple teams commonly share:
- clusters
- infrastructure
- networking
- storage
- and operational services.

Without guardrails,
shared platforms may experience:
- security violations
- resource abuse
- operational drift
- and platform instability.

---

# Shared Platform Challenges

Examples include:
- unrestricted deployments
- excessive infrastructure consumption
- privilege escalation
- cross-team impact
- and inconsistent operational practices.

---

# What Guardrails Provide

Platform guardrails establish:
- security boundaries
- operational standards
- resource controls
- compliance requirements
- and platform consistency.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Guardrails allow:
- developer self-service

while maintaining:
- platform safety.

---

# Key Insight

Shared platforms require controlled freedom rather than unrestricted access.
