# Golden Path Platforms

A golden path provides:
- approved
- supported
- and secure deployment patterns.

Developers receive:
- self-service capabilities

without needing to design:
- platform architecture.

---

# Why Golden Paths Matter

Without standards,
teams may deploy:
- inconsistent architectures
- insecure workloads
- and unsupported configurations.

---

# Common Golden Path Components

Examples include:
- deployment templates
- approved base images
- CI/CD standards
- security policies
- and observability integrations.

---

# Runtime Platform Benefits

Golden paths improve:
- developer productivity
- platform consistency
- and operational reliability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Golden paths provide:
- freedom within boundaries.
