# Platform Standardization

Standardization ensures:
- workloads are deployed consistently across the platform.

Consistency improves:
- operations
- governance
- security
- and supportability.

---

# Why Standardization Matters

Without standards,
platforms may experience:
- configuration drift
- inconsistent practices
- and operational complexity.

---

# Common Standardization Areas

Examples include:
- labels
- resource requests
- security controls
- deployment patterns
- and observability requirements.

---

# Runtime Platform Benefits

Standardization improves:
- troubleshooting
- automation
- auditing
- and governance.

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Standardization enables platform scale.
