# Platform Team Responsibilities

Platform teams provide:
- the Kubernetes platform as a service.

Their responsibility is:
- platform reliability
- platform security
- platform governance
- and platform operations.

---

# Common Platform Responsibilities

Examples include:
- cluster management
- networking
- observability
- policy enforcement
- security controls
- and infrastructure governance.

---

# What Platform Teams Do Not Own

Platform teams typically do not own:
- business logic
- application code
- or product functionality.

---

# Runtime Platform Benefits

Clear ownership reduces:
- operational confusion
- governance gaps
- and responsibility overlap.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Platform teams provide:
- paved roads rather than unrestricted infrastructure.

---

# Key Insight

Platform teams own the platform, not the applications.
