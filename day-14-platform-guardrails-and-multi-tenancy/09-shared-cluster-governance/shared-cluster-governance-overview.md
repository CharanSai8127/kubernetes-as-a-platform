# Shared Cluster Governance Overview

Shared Kubernetes clusters commonly host:
- multiple teams
- multiple applications
- multiple environments
- and multiple operational requirements.

Governance ensures:
- safe and predictable platform behavior.

---

# Why Shared Governance Matters

Without governance,
shared platforms may experience:
- security violations
- operational drift
- resource abuse
- and platform instability.

---

# Governance Goals

Shared cluster governance aims to provide:
- consistency
- fairness
- accountability
- and operational control.

---

# Runtime Platform Protection

Governance protects:
- infrastructure
- workloads
- platform services
- and tenant boundaries.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Governance allows many teams to safely share one platform.

---

# Key Insight

Shared infrastructure requires shared rules.
