# Blast Radius Reduction Overview

Blast radius reduction focuses on:
- limiting the impact of failures.

Failures should remain:
- localized
- predictable
- and recoverable.

---

# Why Blast Radius Matters

Distributed systems continuously experience:
- failures
- misconfigurations
- security incidents
- and operational mistakes.

Without boundaries,
small failures may become:
- platform-wide failures.

---

# Protection Goals

Blast radius reduction aims to provide:
- containment
- isolation
- resilience
- and survivability.

---

# Runtime Platform Benefits

Controlled failures improve:
- platform stability
- operational reliability
- and tenant protection.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Platforms should assume failures will occur and design boundaries around them.

---

# Key Insight

Failures are inevitable. Platform-wide failures are optional.
