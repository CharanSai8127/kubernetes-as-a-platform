# Failure Containment Strategies

Failure containment limits:
- the impact of unexpected events.

Platforms should be designed to:
- absorb failures
- isolate failures
- and recover from failures.

---

# Why Containment Matters

Distributed systems are composed of:
- multiple services
- multiple teams
- and multiple dependencies.

Failures naturally occur.

---

# Common Containment Techniques

Examples include:
- tenant isolation
- network segmentation
- least privilege access
- resource governance
- and workload boundaries.

---

# Runtime Platform Benefits

Containment improves:
- resilience
- survivability
- and operational stability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Platform design should prioritize containment over prevention alone.

---

# Key Insight

The goal is not to eliminate failures but to control their impact.
