# Operational Blast Radius

Operational blast radius measures:
- how far operational failures can spread.

Failures should remain:
- isolated to the affected workload or tenant.

---

# Why Operational Blast Radius Matters

Operational failures commonly include:
- bad deployments
- configuration errors
- dependency failures
- and runaway workloads.

Without isolation,
these failures may impact:
- unrelated applications
- shared services
- and platform stability.

---

# Common Protection Controls

Examples include:
- namespaces
- quotas
- deployment guardrails
- workload policies
- and tenant boundaries.

---

# Runtime Platform Benefits

Operational boundaries improve:
- platform reliability
- tenant independence
- and failure containment.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Operational failures should remain localized.

---

# Key Insight

One team's mistake should not become everyone's outage.
