# Tenant Boundaries

Tenant boundaries define:
- the limits of tenant access
- visibility
- and resource consumption.

These boundaries ensure:
- tenant independence within shared platforms.

---

# Why Tenant Boundaries Matter

Without boundaries,
tenants may:
- impact each other
- consume shared resources excessively
- or bypass security controls.

---

# Runtime Isolation Controls

Tenant boundaries commonly include:
- namespaces
- RBAC
- network policies
- quotas
- and policy enforcement.

---

# Operational Challenges

Weak boundaries may create:
- security risks
- operational instability
- and resource contention.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Tenant boundaries are the foundation of:
- secure multi-tenant platforms.

---

# Key Insight

Tenant isolation depends on multiple layers of platform controls.
