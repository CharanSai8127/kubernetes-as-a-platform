# Soft Multi-Tenancy

Soft multi-tenancy allows:
- multiple tenants
to share:
- the same Kubernetes cluster.

Isolation is implemented through:
- namespaces
- RBAC
- network policies
- quotas
- and governance controls.

---

# Why Soft Multi-Tenancy Matters

Soft multi-tenancy improves:
- infrastructure efficiency
- platform utilization
- and operational simplicity.

---

# Runtime Isolation

Tenants share:
- worker nodes
- control plane resources
- and platform services.

Isolation is primarily:
- logical rather than physical.

---

# Operational Challenges

Improper isolation may create:
- cross-tenant impact
- security exposure
- and resource contention.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Soft multi-tenancy balances:
- operational efficiency
- and tenant isolation.

---

# Key Insight

Soft multi-tenancy depends on governance rather than physical separation.
