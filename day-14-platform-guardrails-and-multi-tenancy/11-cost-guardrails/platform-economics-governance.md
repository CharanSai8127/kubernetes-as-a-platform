# Platform Economics Governance

Infrastructure economics are part of:
- platform governance.

Economic governance controls:
- how infrastructure resources are allocated and consumed.

---

# Why Economics Governance Matters

As platforms scale,
infrastructure consumption scales with:
- workloads
- storage
- networking
- and operational services.

Without governance,
costs may grow faster than platform value.

---

# Common Governance Areas

Examples include:
- workload sizing
- autoscaling controls
- infrastructure utilization
- and workload placement policies.

---

# Runtime Platform Benefits

Economic governance improves:
- sustainability
- predictability
- and infrastructure efficiency.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Reliable platforms must remain economically sustainable over time.

---

# Key Insight

Infrastructure economics should be managed with the same discipline as security and operations.
