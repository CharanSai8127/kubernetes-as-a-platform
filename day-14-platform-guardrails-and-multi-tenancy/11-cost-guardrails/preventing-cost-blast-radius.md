# Preventing Cost Blast Radius

Failures can create:
- operational impact
and:
- financial impact.

Cost blast radius measures:
- how much infrastructure economics can expand during failures.

---

# Why Cost Blast Radius Matters

Runtime instability may trigger:
- runaway autoscaling
- retry storms
- infrastructure expansion
- and excessive resource consumption.

---

# Common Cost Risks

Examples include:
- infinite scaling loops
- oversized workloads
- excessive replicas
- and uncontrolled infrastructure growth.

---

# Runtime Platform Protection

Cost guardrails limit:
- financial exposure
- runaway infrastructure usage
- and platform waste.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Failures should remain financially contained.

---

# Key Insight

Operational instability should not become financial instability.
