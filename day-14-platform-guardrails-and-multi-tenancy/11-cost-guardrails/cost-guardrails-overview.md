# Cost Guardrails Overview

Cost guardrails protect:
- infrastructure economics
- platform sustainability
- and resource efficiency.

Shared Kubernetes platforms require:
- financial governance in addition to technical governance.

---

# Why Cost Guardrails Matter

Without controls,
workloads may create:
- excessive infrastructure consumption
- runaway scaling
- idle resources
- and uncontrolled cloud spend.

---

# Governance Goals

Cost guardrails aim to provide:
- controlled consumption
- efficient infrastructure usage
- and economic sustainability.

---

# Runtime Platform Protection

Cost guardrails reduce:
- infrastructure waste
- cost amplification
- and financial instability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Platform reliability must also be economically sustainable.

---

# Key Insight

Operationally successful platforms can still fail financially without cost governance.
