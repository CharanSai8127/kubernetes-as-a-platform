# Policies Over Human Memory

Operational standards should not depend on:
- individual knowledge
- personal experience
- or tribal knowledge.

Platforms should enforce:
- documented policies.

---

# Why Human Memory Is Insufficient

People:
- change roles
- leave organizations
- make mistakes
- and interpret requirements differently.

Policies provide:
- consistent enforcement.

---

# Governance Benefits

Policies ensure:
- repeatable outcomes
- operational consistency
- and platform reliability.

---

# Runtime Platform Protection

Policy enforcement reduces:
- operational drift
- governance violations
- and inconsistent practices.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Platform knowledge should be encoded into the platform itself.

---

# Key Insight

Reliable platforms depend on enforceable rules rather than remembered rules.
