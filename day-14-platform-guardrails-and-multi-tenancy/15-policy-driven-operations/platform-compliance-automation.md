# Platform Compliance Automation

Compliance requirements should be:
- continuously enforced
through:
- automated controls.

Compliance becomes:
- an operational capability.

---

# Why Compliance Automation Matters

Manual compliance reviews are:
- expensive
- slow
- and difficult to scale.

Automation improves:
- consistency
- visibility
- and enforcement.

---

# Common Compliance Areas

Examples include:
- security requirements
- workload standards
- audit requirements
- and governance controls.

---

# Runtime Platform Benefits

Automation improves:
- policy adherence
- audit readiness
- and governance reliability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Compliance should be built into platform operations rather than added later.

---

# Key Insight

Continuous compliance is more effective than periodic compliance reviews.
