# Automated Operational Enforcement

Operational requirements should be:
- automatically enforced
rather than:
- manually verified.

Automation ensures:
- consistency
- reliability
- and scalability.

---

# Why Automated Enforcement Matters

Manual enforcement may create:
- configuration drift
- inconsistent behavior
- and governance gaps.

As platforms grow,
manual validation becomes:
- unsustainable.

---

# Common Enforcement Areas

Examples include:
- security requirements
- resource requirements
- deployment standards
- and compliance controls.

---

# Runtime Platform Benefits

Automation reduces:
- human error
- operational overhead
- and governance violations.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Automation should continuously protect platform standards.

---

# Key Insight

Every manual operational process eventually becomes a scaling bottleneck.
