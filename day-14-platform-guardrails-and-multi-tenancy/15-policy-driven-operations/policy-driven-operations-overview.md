# Policy Driven Operations Overview

Policy-driven operations use:
- policies

rather than:
- manual intervention

to manage platform behavior.

Policies become:
- operational decision makers.

---

# Why Policy Driven Operations Matter

Human processes are:
- inconsistent
- difficult to scale
- and error prone.

Policies provide:
- predictable
- repeatable
- and automated behavior.

---

# Operational Goals

Policy-driven operations aim to provide:
- consistency
- automation
- governance
- and platform reliability.

---

# Runtime Platform Benefits

Policies continuously enforce:
- platform standards
- security controls
- and operational requirements.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Operational behavior should be governed by policies rather than tribal knowledge.

---

# Key Insight

Platforms scale through automation, not through manual enforcement.
