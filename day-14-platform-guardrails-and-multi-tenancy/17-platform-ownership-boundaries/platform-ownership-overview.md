# Platform Ownership Overview

Platform ownership defines:
- who is responsible for the platform
- who is responsible for applications
- and where operational responsibilities begin and end.

Clear ownership improves:
- accountability
- governance
- and operational efficiency.

---

# Why Ownership Matters

Shared platforms involve:
- multiple teams
- multiple responsibilities
- and multiple operational domains.

Without ownership boundaries,
organizations may experience:
- confusion
- duplicated effort
- and operational gaps.

---

# Ownership Goals

Platform ownership aims to provide:
- accountability
- operational clarity
- and governance consistency.

---

# Runtime Platform Benefits

Clear ownership improves:
- supportability
- reliability
- and operational coordination.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Every platform capability should have a clearly defined owner.

---

# Key Insight

Undefined ownership creates undefined responsibility.
