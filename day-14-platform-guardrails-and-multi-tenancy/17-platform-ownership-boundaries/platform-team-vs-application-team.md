# Platform Team Vs Application Team

Platform engineering introduces:
- separation of responsibilities.

Platform teams and application teams solve:
- different problems.

---

# Platform Team Responsibilities

Platform teams commonly own:
- Kubernetes clusters
- networking
- storage
- observability
- security controls
- governance
- and platform services.

Their responsibility is:
- platform reliability.

---

# Application Team Responsibilities

Application teams commonly own:
- application code
- deployments
- APIs
- business logic
- and application functionality.

Their responsibility is:
- product delivery.

---

# Runtime Platform Benefits

Clear separation improves:
- scalability
- operational efficiency
- and governance.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Platforms should provide capabilities while applications consume capabilities.

---

# Key Insight

Platform teams build the roads. Application teams drive on them.
