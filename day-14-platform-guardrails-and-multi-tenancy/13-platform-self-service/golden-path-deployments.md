# Golden Path Deployments

Golden paths provide:
- approved deployment patterns.

Developers follow:
- supported
- secure
- and operationally validated approaches.

---

# Why Golden Paths Matter

Without guidance,
teams may create:
- inconsistent deployments
- insecure configurations
- and unsupported architectures.

---

# Common Golden Path Components

Examples include:
- deployment templates
- CI/CD standards
- observability integrations
- security defaults
- and networking standards.

---

# Runtime Platform Benefits

Golden paths improve:
- consistency
- security
- maintainability
- and developer experience.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Golden paths accelerate delivery while reducing risk.

---

# Key Insight

The preferred deployment path should also be the safest deployment path.
