# Platform Self Service Overview

Platform self-service allows:
- application teams
- developers
- and tenants

to consume platform capabilities independently.

Self-service reduces:
- operational bottlenecks
- platform dependency
- and deployment delays.

---

# Why Self Service Matters

Platform teams cannot manually manage:
- every deployment
- every application
- and every operational request.

Developers require:
- fast access to platform capabilities.

---

# Self Service Goals

Self-service aims to provide:
- developer autonomy
- operational efficiency
- and platform scalability.

---

# Runtime Platform Benefits

Self-service improves:
- deployment velocity
- platform adoption
- and operational consistency.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Modern platforms operate as:
- internal developer platforms.

---

# Key Insight

Self-service enables developer freedom while maintaining platform control.
