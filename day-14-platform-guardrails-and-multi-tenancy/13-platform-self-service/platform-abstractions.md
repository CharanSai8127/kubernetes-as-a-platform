# Platform Abstractions

Platform abstractions simplify:
- infrastructure consumption.

Developers should focus on:
- applications

rather than:
- platform internals.

---

# Why Abstractions Matter

Modern Kubernetes platforms contain:
- networking
- storage
- security
- observability
- and governance controls.

Application teams should not manage:
- every underlying implementation detail.

---

# Common Abstractions

Examples include:
- deployment templates
- service catalogs
- application blueprints
- and platform APIs.

---

# Runtime Platform Benefits

Abstractions improve:
- usability
- consistency
- and operational scalability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Platforms should simplify complexity rather than expose complexity.

---

# Key Insight

Good platforms hide complexity while exposing capability.
