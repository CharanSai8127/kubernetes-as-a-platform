# Least Privilege Principle

The principle of least privilege means:

Grant only the permissions required
to perform a task.

Nothing more.

---

# Why Least Privilege Matters

Excessive permissions increase:
- security risk
- operational risk
- and blast radius.

Compromised identities should have:
- limited capabilities.

---

# Runtime Security Benefits

Least privilege reduces:
- privilege escalation
- accidental modifications
- and unauthorized access.

---

# Common Examples

Developers may require:
- view workloads
- view logs
- create deployments

Developers may not require:
- cluster-admin access
- secret management
- or node administration.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Permissions should be aligned with:
- responsibilities.

---

# Key Insight

Every permission increases potential blast radius.
