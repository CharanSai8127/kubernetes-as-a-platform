# RBAC Overview

RBAC stands for:
- Role Based Access Control.

RBAC controls:
- who can access resources
- what actions can be performed
- and where those actions are allowed.

---

# Why RBAC Matters

Kubernetes is a shared platform.

Not every user should have access to:
- all workloads
- all namespaces
- all secrets
- or all cluster resources.

RBAC limits access to:
- only what is required.

---

# Access Control Model

RBAC answers:

Who are you?

What are you allowed to do?

Where can you perform those actions?

---

# Runtime Platform Protection

RBAC reduces:
- unauthorized access
- accidental modifications
- and privilege abuse.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

RBAC establishes:
- controlled access boundaries.

---

# Key Insight

Access should be granted based on responsibility rather than convenience.
