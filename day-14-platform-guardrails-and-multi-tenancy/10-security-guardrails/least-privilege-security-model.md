# Least Privilege Security Model

Least privilege means:

Grant the minimum access required
to perform a task.

Nothing more.

---

# Why Least Privilege Matters

Every permission increases:
- risk
- blast radius
- and attack surface.

Compromised identities should have:
- limited capabilities.

---

# Common Applications

Least privilege applies to:
- users
- service accounts
- workloads
- and platform administrators.

---

# Runtime Platform Protection

Least privilege reduces:
- unauthorized access
- privilege escalation
- and platform compromise.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Access should be based on responsibility rather than convenience.

---

# Key Insight

Permissions should be intentionally granted rather than broadly assigned.
