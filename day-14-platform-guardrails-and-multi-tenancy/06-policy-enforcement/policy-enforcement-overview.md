# Policy Enforcement Overview

Policy enforcement ensures:
- workloads comply with platform standards before entering the cluster.

Policies allow platform teams to define:
- acceptable platform behavior.

---

# Why Policy Enforcement Matters

Shared platforms require:
- consistency
- security
- compliance
- and operational governance.

Without policy enforcement,
users may deploy:
- insecure workloads
- misconfigured resources
- or non-compliant infrastructure.

---

# Common Enforcement Areas

Policies commonly govern:
- container security
- resource requirements
- networking standards
- image sources
- and deployment practices.

---

# Runtime Platform Protection

Policy enforcement prevents:
- platform drift
- security violations
- and operational inconsistency.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Policies transform best practices into:
- enforceable platform rules.

---

# Key Insight

Standards are only effective when they are enforced.
