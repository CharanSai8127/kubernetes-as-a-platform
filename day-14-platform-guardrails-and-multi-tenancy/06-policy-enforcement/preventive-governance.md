# Preventive Governance

Preventive governance focuses on:
- stopping problems before they enter the platform.

Rather than correcting issues later,
the platform blocks:
- invalid
- insecure
- or non-compliant resources.

---

# Why Preventive Governance Matters

Fixing problems after deployment is:
- expensive
- risky
- and operationally disruptive.

Preventing them early improves:
- platform safety.

---

# Runtime Governance Flow

Governance should occur:
- before workloads are admitted
- before resources are created
- and before risk reaches production.

---

# Common Examples

Examples include:
- blocking privileged containers
- enforcing resource limits
- restricting registries
- requiring security settings

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Prevention is more effective than remediation.

---

# Key Insight

The safest workload issue is the one that never reaches the cluster.
