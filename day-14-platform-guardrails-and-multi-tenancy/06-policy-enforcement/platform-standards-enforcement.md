# Platform Standards Enforcement

Platform standards define:
- how workloads should be deployed and operated.

Enforcement ensures:
- consistency across tenants.

---

# Why Standards Matter

Without standards,
platforms may experience:
- configuration drift
- security gaps
- operational inconsistency
- and governance failures.

---

# Common Standards

Examples include:
- mandatory labels
- approved images
- resource requirements
- ownership metadata
- and security controls.

---

# Runtime Platform Benefits

Standards improve:
- operational visibility
- troubleshooting
- auditing
- and platform maintainability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Standards create:
- predictable platform behavior.

---

# Key Insight

Consistency improves both security and operations.
