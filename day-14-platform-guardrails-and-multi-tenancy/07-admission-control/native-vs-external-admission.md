# Native Vs External Admission

Modern Kubernetes provides:
- native admission capabilities.

Additional governance can be implemented through:
- external policy engines.

---

# Native Admission Controls

Examples include:
- Pod Security Admission
- ValidatingAdmissionPolicy
- CEL-based validation

These capabilities are built directly into:
- Kubernetes.

---

# External Admission Controls

Examples include:
- Kyverno
- OPA Gatekeeper
- Kubewarden

These systems provide:
- advanced governance
- reusable policies
- auditing
- and compliance workflows.

---

# Why Both Approaches Matter

Native controls reduce:
- platform complexity.

External controls provide:
- richer governance capabilities.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Modern platforms often combine:
- native controls
- and policy engines.

---

# Key Insight

Governance is increasingly becoming a first-class Kubernetes capability.
