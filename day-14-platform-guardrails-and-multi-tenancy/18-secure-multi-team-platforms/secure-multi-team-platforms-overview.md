# Secure Multi Team Platforms Overview

Modern Kubernetes platforms commonly support:
- multiple teams
- multiple applications
- multiple environments
- and multiple operational requirements.

Secure multi-team platforms combine:
- governance
- security
- isolation
- and platform engineering.

---

# Why Secure Multi Team Platforms Matter

As platforms grow,
the risk of:
- security incidents
- operational mistakes
- and cross-team impact

also grows.

The platform must protect:
- tenants
- infrastructure
- and shared services.

---

# Platform Goals

Secure platforms aim to provide:
- self-service
- security
- governance
- and operational reliability.

---

# Runtime Platform Benefits

Secure multi-team platforms improve:
- scalability
- consistency
- and tenant independence.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Security should be built into the platform rather than added later.

---

# Key Insight

A secure platform allows many teams to operate independently without impacting one another.
