# Auditable Platform Governance

Auditing provides visibility into:
- governance decisions
- policy enforcement
- and platform compliance.

Governance should be:
- observable
- traceable
- and explainable.

---

# Why Auditing Matters

Organizations commonly require:
- compliance validation
- security reviews
- operational accountability
- and incident investigation.

---

# Governance Visibility

Auditable governance provides answers to:

Who changed a policy?

When was it changed?

Why was it changed?

What impact did it have?

---

# Runtime Platform Benefits

Auditing improves:
- compliance
- accountability
- and governance transparency.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Platform decisions should be observable and traceable.

---

# Key Insight

Governance that cannot be audited cannot be trusted.
