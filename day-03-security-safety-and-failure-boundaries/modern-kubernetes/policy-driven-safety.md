# Policy Driven Safety

Modern Kubernetes platforms enforce operational safety using policy engines.

Policies standardize:
- security requirements
- workload constraints
- deployment rules
- resource governance

Examples:
- Kyverno
- OPA Gatekeeper
- CEL admission policies

Policies reduce:
- unsafe deployments
- inconsistent configurations
- privilege escalation
- operational drift

Safety becomes enforceable at platform level instead of relying entirely on human review.
