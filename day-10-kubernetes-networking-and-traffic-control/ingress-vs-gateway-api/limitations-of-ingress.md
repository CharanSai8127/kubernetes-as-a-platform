# Limitations Of Ingress

Traditional ingress architectures commonly focus on:
- external HTTP exposure.

As distributed systems scale:
- communication requirements become more complex.

---

# Why Limitations Matter

Modern workloads require:
- advanced traffic control
- policy attachment
- and communication segmentation.

Traditional ingress models may struggle with:
- operational flexibility.

---

# Operational Challenges

Large-scale environments may require:
- traffic isolation
- multi-team ownership
- and runtime routing control.

---

# Runtime Traffic Complexity

Distributed communication continuously evolves during:
- scaling
- failover
- and dependency instability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Modern traffic architectures require:
- declarative traffic engineering.

---

# Key Insight

Distributed traffic control increasingly exceeds traditional ingress capabilities.
