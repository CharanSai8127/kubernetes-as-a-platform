# Namespace Isolation

Namespaces help create:
- workload separation boundaries inside Kubernetes.

Isolation improves:
- operational control
- communication segmentation
- and dependency protection.

---

# Why Namespace Isolation Matters

Distributed workloads frequently communicate across:
- multiple services
- teams
- and environments.

Without isolation:
- communication exposure increases significantly.

---

# Runtime Communication Control

Namespace isolation improves:
- communication predictability
- traffic governance
- and operational visibility.

---

# Operational Benefits

Isolation reduces:
- dependency amplification
- runtime instability
- and communication sprawl.
