- intentional communication paths.

Communication boundaries improve:
- operational predictability
- and runtime survivability.

Unrestricted communication creates:
- dependency sprawl
- noisy-neighbor behavior
- and runtime instability.

- workload protection

# Operational Benefits

- cascading failures
- runtime congestion
- and operational unpredictability.
