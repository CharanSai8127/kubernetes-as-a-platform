# Runtime Isolation Engineering

Modern Kubernetes networking increasingly focuses on:
- runtime communication isolation.

Isolation engineering improves:
- workload survivability
- dependency protection
- and operational resilience.

---

# Why Runtime Isolation Matters

Distributed systems continuously exchange:
- internal runtime traffic.

Uncontrolled communication paths may create:
- cascading instability
- dependency overload
- and runtime unpredictability.

---

# Communication Survivability

Runtime isolation improves:
- failure containment
- traffic segmentation
- and dependency stability.

---

# Operational Benefits

Isolation engineering improves:
- communication visibility
- operational control
- and distributed-system stability.
