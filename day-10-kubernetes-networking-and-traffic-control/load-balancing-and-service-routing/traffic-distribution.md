# Traffic Distribution

Distributed systems continuously distribute:
- runtime traffic across workloads.

Traffic distribution behavior directly impacts:
- communication stability
- workload survivability
- and operational resilience.

---

# Why Traffic Distribution Matters

Improper distribution may create:
- overloaded endpoints
- retry amplification
- and runtime instability.

---

# Runtime Communication Patterns

Traffic behavior changes continuously during:
- scaling
- dependency failures
- and runtime pressure.

---

# Operational Challenges

Traffic imbalance may create:
- congestion
- latency spikes
- and cascading failures.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Traffic distribution strongly influences:
- runtime communication survivability.

---

# Key Insight

Reliable traffic distribution improves distributed-system stability significantly.
