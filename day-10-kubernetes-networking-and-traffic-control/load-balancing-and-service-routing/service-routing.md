# Service Routing

Distributed systems continuously route:
- runtime traffic across workloads.

Kubernetes services help distribute:
- traffic across healthy endpoints.

---

# Why Load Balancing Matters

Uneven traffic distribution may create:
- overload
- latency spikes
- and runtime instability.

---

# Runtime Communication

Service routing influences:
- dependency behavior
- failover
- and traffic survivability.

---

# Operational Risks

Improper routing may create:
- connection exhaustion
- retry amplification
- and communication bottlenecks.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Traffic routing directly impacts:
- workload survivability under runtime pressure.

---

# Key Insight

Reliable service routing improves communication stability significantly.
