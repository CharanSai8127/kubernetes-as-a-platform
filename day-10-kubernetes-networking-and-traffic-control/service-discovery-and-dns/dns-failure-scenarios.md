# DNS Failure Scenarios

Distributed systems depend heavily on:
- stable DNS resolution.

DNS instability may propagate rapidly across:
- workloads
- services
- and dependencies.

---

# Common DNS Failures

Examples include:
- failed service resolution
- delayed lookups
- DNS timeouts
- and intermittent resolution instability.

---

# Operational Impact

DNS failures may create:
- retry storms
- dependency amplification
- and communication instability.

---

# Runtime Survivability

Traffic visibility improves:
- DNS debugging
- dependency tracing
- and operational recovery.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

DNS instability may become:
- a distributed-system failure amplifier.

Runtime communication reliability depends heavily on DNS stability.
