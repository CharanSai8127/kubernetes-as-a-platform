# CoreDNS Overview

CoreDNS provides:
- internal DNS resolution inside Kubernetes.

Applications use DNS continuously for:
- service discovery
- dependency communication
- and runtime resolution.

---

# Why CoreDNS Matters

Distributed systems rely heavily on:
- runtime dependency lookup.

DNS instability directly impacts:
- workload communication reliability.

---

# Operational Benefits

CoreDNS improves:
- service reachability
- communication consistency
- and runtime dependency discovery.

---

# Operational Risks

DNS degradation may create:
- timeout amplification
- dependency instability
- and cascading communication failures.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

DNS reliability becomes critical for:
- distributed-system survivability.

---

# Key Insight

Stable DNS resolution enables reliable distributed communication.
