# Runtime Communication Survivability

Distributed systems survive through:
- stable runtime communication behavior.

Applications may remain operational while:
- communication paths degrade internally.

---

# Why Survivability Matters

Communication instability may create:
- retries
- dependency overload
- and cascading failures.

---

# Runtime Communication Risks

Distributed workloads continuously depend on:
- stable dependency communication.

Communication degradation may rapidly impact:
- runtime reliability.

---

# Operational Benefits

Traffic visibility improves:
- runtime diagnosis
- communication tracing
- and operational recovery.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Networking reliability increasingly becomes:
- runtime survivability engineering.

Distributed systems survive or fail through communication stability.
