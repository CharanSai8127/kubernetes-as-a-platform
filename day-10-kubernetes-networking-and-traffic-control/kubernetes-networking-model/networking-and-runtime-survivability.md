# Networking And Runtime Survivability

Networking reliability strongly influences:
- runtime survivability.

Applications may continue running while:
- communication paths fail internally.

---

# Runtime Failure Patterns

Communication instability may create:
- retries
- dependency overload
- and cascading failures.

---

# Why Survivability Matters

Distributed systems survive through:
- stable communication behavior.

Networking therefore becomes:
- runtime survivability infrastructure.

---

# Operational Importance

Traffic visibility and communication control improve:
- recovery
- debugging
- and operational resilience.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Networking is not only:
- packet delivery.

It is:
- communication survivability engineering.

---

# Key Insight

Distributed systems survive or fail through communication stability.
