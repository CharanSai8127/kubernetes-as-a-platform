# Distributed-System Survivability

Distributed systems continuously experience:
- runtime instability
- dependency failures
- and communication pressure.

Survivability focuses on:
- maintaining operational reliability during instability.

---

# Why Survivability Matters

Applications may remain operational while:
- internal communication paths degrade.

This creates:
- hidden runtime instability.

---

# Runtime Communication Risks

Distributed failures may propagate through:
- traffic behavior
- dependency instability
- and communication amplification.

---

# Operational Benefits

Traffic visibility improves:
- runtime diagnosis
- dependency tracing
- and operational recovery.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Distributed-system survivability depends heavily on:
- communication stability.

---

# Key Insight

Reliable communication behavior strongly improves distributed-system survivability.
