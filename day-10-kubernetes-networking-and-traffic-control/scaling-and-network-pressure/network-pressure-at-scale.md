# Network Pressure At Scale

Scaling distributed systems increases:
- runtime communication pressure.

Applications continuously generate:
- service requests
- retries
- and dependency traffic.

---

# Why Network Pressure Matters

Scaling increases:
- communication complexity
not only:
- compute usage.

Runtime traffic amplification may create:
- congestion
- dependency overload
- and communication instability.

---

# Operational Challenges

High traffic pressure may create:
- packet drops
- latency spikes
- and runtime unpredictability.

---

# Runtime Survivability

Traffic visibility improves:
- pressure detection
- communication tracing
- and operational recovery.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Scaling distributed systems requires:
- communication survivability engineering.

---

# Key Insight

Scaling increases runtime communication complexity significantly.
