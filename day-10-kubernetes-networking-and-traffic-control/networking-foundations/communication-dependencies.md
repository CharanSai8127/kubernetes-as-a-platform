# Communication Dependencies

Distributed systems continuously depend on:
- runtime communication.

Applications exchange:
- service requests
- database traffic
- and dependency communication.

---

# Why Dependencies Matter

Applications may appear healthy while:
- communication paths remain unstable.

This creates:
- hidden runtime instability.

---

# Operational Risks

Dependency instability may create:
- retry amplification
- connection exhaustion
- and cascading failures.

---

# Runtime Survivability

Communication dependency visibility improves:
- debugging
- recovery
- and operational survivability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Reliable systems require:
- reliable communication dependencies.

---

# Key Insight

Communication dependency stability directly impacts workload survivability.
