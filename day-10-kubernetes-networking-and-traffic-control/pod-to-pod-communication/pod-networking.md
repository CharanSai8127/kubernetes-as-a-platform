# Pod Networking

Pods continuously communicate across:
- nodes
- services
- and workloads.

Reliable pod networking is critical for:
- distributed-system communication.

---

# Cross-Node Communication

Pod communication frequently spans:
- multiple worker nodes.

Networking layers therefore manage:
- routing
- overlays
- and traffic forwarding.

---

# Operational Challenges

Communication instability may create:
- latency spikes
- dropped packets
- and runtime failures.

---

# Runtime Pressure

Scaling increases:
- communication complexity
- dependency traffic
- and network pressure.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Pod communication reliability directly impacts:
- workload survivability.

---

# Key Insight

Distributed workloads depend heavily on reliable pod communication paths.
