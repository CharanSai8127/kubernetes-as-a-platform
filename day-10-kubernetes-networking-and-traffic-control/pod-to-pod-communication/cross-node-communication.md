# Cross-Node Communication

Distributed workloads frequently communicate across:
- multiple Kubernetes nodes.

Traffic therefore traverses:
- networking overlays
- routing layers
- and runtime forwarding paths.

---

# Why Cross-Node Traffic Matters

Applications rarely remain isolated to:
- a single node.

Communication stability across nodes directly impacts:
- runtime reliability.

---

# Operational Challenges

Cross-node communication may experience:
- latency variation
- packet drops
- and routing instability.

---

# Runtime Survivability

Communication visibility improves:
- troubleshooting
- dependency tracing
- and runtime recovery.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Cross-node communication reliability strongly influences:
- distributed-system stability.

---

# Key Insight

Distributed workloads depend heavily on stable cross-node communication.
