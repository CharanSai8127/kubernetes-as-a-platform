# Runtime Communication Pressure

Scaling distributed systems increases:
- runtime communication pressure.

Applications continuously generate:
- retries
- service requests
- and dependency traffic.

---

# Why Pressure Matters

Communication amplification may create:
- congestion
- dependency overload
- and runtime instability.

---

# Operational Challenges

High communication pressure may create:
- connection exhaustion
- packet drops
- and retry storms.

---

# Runtime Survivability

Traffic visibility improves:
- runtime debugging
- pressure detection
- and operational recovery.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Scaling increases communication complexity,
not only compute usage.

---

# Key Insight

Runtime communication pressure strongly impacts distributed-system survivability.
