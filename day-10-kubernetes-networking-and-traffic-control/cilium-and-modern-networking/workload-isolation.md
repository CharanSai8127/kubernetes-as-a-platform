# Workload Isolation

Distributed workloads should not communicate:
- without controlled communication boundaries.

Workload isolation improves:
- operational stability
- security
- and runtime predictability.

---

# Why Isolation Matters

Unrestricted communication paths may create:
- dependency sprawl
- noisy-neighbor behavior
- and runtime instability.
