# Traffic Behavior Control

Kubernetes networking is not only about:
- moving packets.

It also controls:
- traffic behavior
- routing decisions
- retries
- failover
- and runtime communication paths.

---

# Why Traffic Control Matters

Distributed systems continuously exchange:
- internal runtime traffic.

Traffic instability may create:
- cascading failures
- retry storms
- and communication overload.

---

# Runtime Traffic Engineering

Traffic routing determines:
- how requests move through distributed systems.

This impacts:
- latency
- reliability
- failover behavior
- and runtime survivability.

---

# Operational Importance

Traffic control improves:
- communication stability
- dependency isolation
- and runtime resilience.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Traffic behavior directly impacts:
- distributed-system survivability.

---

# Key Insight

Networking reliability depends heavily on traffic behavior engineering.
