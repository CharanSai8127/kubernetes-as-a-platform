# Traffic Engineering

Traffic engineering focuses on:
- controlling runtime communication behavior.

Distributed systems continuously exchange:
- internal service traffic
- dependency communication
- and failover requests.

---

# Why Traffic Engineering Matters

Traffic behavior directly impacts:
- latency
- reliability
- scaling stability
- and runtime survivability.

---

# Runtime Traffic Patterns

Traffic patterns change continuously during:
- scaling
- failures
- retries
- and dependency pressure.

---

# Operational Benefits

Traffic engineering improves:
- communication control
- dependency isolation
- and operational resilience.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Networking increasingly becomes:
- runtime communication engineering.

---

# Key Insight

Distributed-system survivability depends heavily on controlled traffic behavior.
