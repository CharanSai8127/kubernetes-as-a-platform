# East-West Communication

East-west traffic represents:
- internal workload communication inside distributed systems.

Applications continuously exchange:
- service requests
- retries
- and dependency traffic.

---

# Why East-West Traffic Matters

Most distributed-system communication occurs internally between:
- workloads
- APIs
- databases
- and runtime dependencies.

---

# Operational Challenges

East-west instability may create:
- retry storms
- dependency amplification
- and cascading failures.

---

# Runtime Survivability

Traffic visibility improves:
- communication tracing
- dependency debugging
- and runtime recovery.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Internal communication stability strongly impacts:
- distributed-system survivability.

---

# Key Insight

Distributed systems primarily fail through internal communication instability.
