# North-South Traffic Flow

North-south traffic represents:
- external communication entering or leaving the cluster.

This commonly includes:
- ingress traffic
- external APIs
- and internet-facing communication.

---

# Why North-South Traffic Matters

External traffic directly impacts:
- workload exposure
- traffic pressure
- and runtime availability.

---

# Operational Challenges

External traffic spikes may create:
- congestion
- latency instability
- and dependency overload.

---

# Runtime Communication Risks

North-south instability may propagate toward:
- internal runtime dependencies.

This increases:
- communication pressure
- and operational unpredictability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

External traffic behavior strongly influences:
- runtime survivability.

---

# Key Insight

External traffic instability may amplify internal distributed-system failures.
