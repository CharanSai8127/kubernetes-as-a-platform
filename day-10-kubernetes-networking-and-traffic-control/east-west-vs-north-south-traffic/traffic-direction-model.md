# Traffic Direction Model

Distributed systems process:
- different traffic directions.

Traffic behavior changes depending on:
- communication flow patterns.

---

# East-West Traffic

East-west traffic refers to:
- internal service-to-service communication.

Examples include:
- API communication
- database access
- and dependency interaction.

---

# North-South Traffic

North-south traffic refers to:
- external traffic entering or leaving the cluster.

Examples include:
- ingress traffic
- external API requests
- and internet communication.

---

# Why Traffic Direction Matters

Different traffic patterns create:
- different operational risks
- scaling behavior
- and failure characteristics.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Distributed systems fail differently depending on:
- traffic direction.

---

# Key Insight

Traffic direction strongly influences distributed-system survivability.
