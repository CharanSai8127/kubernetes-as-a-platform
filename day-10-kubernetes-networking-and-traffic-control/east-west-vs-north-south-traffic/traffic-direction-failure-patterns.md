# Traffic Direction Failure Patterns

Distributed systems fail differently depending on:
- traffic direction.

East-west and north-south traffic create:
- different communication risks
- and operational behaviors.

---

# East-West Failure Patterns

Internal communication instability may create:
- dependency failures
- retry storms
- and cascading outages.

---

# North-South Failure Patterns

External traffic instability may create:
- overload
- ingress congestion
- and communication amplification.

---

# Runtime Survivability

Traffic visibility improves:
- failure tracing
- dependency debugging
- and operational recovery.

---

# Operational Risks

Traffic instability may propagate rapidly across:
- workloads
- services
- and communication layers.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Traffic direction strongly influences distributed-system failure propagation.

---

# Key Insight

Communication instability behaves differently across internal and external traffic paths.
