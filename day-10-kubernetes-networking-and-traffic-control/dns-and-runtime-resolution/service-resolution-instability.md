# Service Resolution Instability

Distributed workloads continuously resolve:
- runtime service identities dynamically.

Service resolution instability may create:
- communication unpredictability
- dependency failures
- and runtime degradation.

---

# Why Instability Matters

Applications depend heavily on:
- reliable runtime dependency discovery.

Resolution instability may amplify:
- retries
- latency
- and communication pressure.

---

# Runtime Communication Risks

Unstable resolution behavior may create:
- cascading communication failures
- dependency overload
- and operational unpredictability.

---

# Operational Benefits

Resolution observability improves:
- debugging
- dependency tracing
- and runtime survivability.
