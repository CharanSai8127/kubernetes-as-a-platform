# Runtime Resolution Failures

Runtime dependency resolution may fail because of:
- DNS instability
- network congestion
- or communication degradation.

Distributed systems continuously depend on:
- successful runtime resolution.

---

# Why Resolution Failures Matter

Resolution instability may create:
- failed service communication
- increased latency
- and retry amplification.

---

# Operational Challenges

Resolution failures may propagate rapidly across:
- workloads
- namespaces
- and communication layers.

---

# Runtime Survivability

Traffic visibility improves:
- dependency tracing
- DNS debugging
- and operational recovery.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Resolution reliability directly impacts:
- distributed-system survivability.

---

# Key Insight

Runtime communication stability depends heavily on successful dependency resolution.
