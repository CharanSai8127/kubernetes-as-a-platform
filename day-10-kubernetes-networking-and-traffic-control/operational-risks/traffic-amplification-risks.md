# Traffic Amplification Risks

Distributed systems continuously generate:
- runtime communication traffic.

Retries
dependency calls
and failover behavior

may rapidly amplify:
- communication pressure.

---

# Why Amplification Matters

Traffic amplification may overload:
- APIs
- services
- and runtime dependencies.

---

# Operational Risks

Amplified communication may create:
- congestion
- retry storms
- and cascading instability.

---

# Runtime Survivability

Traffic observability improves:
- amplification tracing
- pressure analysis
- and operational recovery.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Communication amplification strongly impacts:
- distributed-system reliability.

---

# Key Insight

Uncontrolled traffic amplification may destabilize runtime communication rapidly.
