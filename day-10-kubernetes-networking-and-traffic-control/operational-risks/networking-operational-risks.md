# Networking Operational Risks

Distributed networking environments continuously face:
- runtime communication risks.

Networking instability may rapidly impact:
- workloads
- dependencies
- and operational reliability.

---

# Common Risks

Examples include:
- DNS instability
- packet drops
- retry storms
- routing asymmetry
- and connection exhaustion.

---

# Why Risks Matter

Communication instability may create:
- cascading failures
- dependency overload
- and runtime unpredictability.

---

# Operational Challenges

Networking failures may propagate across:
- services
- namespaces
- and distributed workloads.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Reliable networking requires:
- communication survivability engineering.

---

# Key Insight

Runtime communication instability strongly impacts distributed-system reliability.
