# Connection Exhaustion

Distributed systems continuously create:
- runtime network connections.

High communication pressure may eventually exhaust:
- available runtime connections.

---

# Why Connection Exhaustion Matters

Connection instability may create:
- failed communication
- dependency overload
- and runtime degradation.

---

# Runtime Communication Pressure

Scaling increases:
- traffic amplification
- dependency requests
- and communication concurrency.

---

# Operational Challenges

Connection exhaustion may create:
- latency spikes
- retries
- and cascading failures.

---

# Runtime Survivability

Traffic visibility improves:
- pressure detection
- connection tracing
- and operational recovery.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Scaling distributed systems increases:
- communication complexity significantly.

---

# Key Insight

Runtime connection stability strongly impacts distributed-system survivability.
