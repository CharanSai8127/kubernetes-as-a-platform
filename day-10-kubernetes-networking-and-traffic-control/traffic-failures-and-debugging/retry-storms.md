# Retry Storms

Distributed systems continuously perform:
- retries during communication instability.

Uncontrolled retries may create:
- retry storms.

---

# Why Retry Storms Matter

Retries intended for recovery may instead amplify:
- traffic pressure
- dependency overload
- and runtime instability.

---

# Runtime Amplification

Large-scale retry behavior may create:
- communication congestion
- overloaded dependencies
- and cascading failures.

---

# Operational Challenges

Retry amplification may overwhelm:
- APIs
- databases
- and internal services.

---

# Runtime Survivability

Controlled retry behavior improves:
- communication resilience
- and operational stability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Communication recovery behavior strongly influences:
- distributed-system survivability.

---

# Key Insight

Uncontrolled retries may amplify distributed-system failures significantly.
