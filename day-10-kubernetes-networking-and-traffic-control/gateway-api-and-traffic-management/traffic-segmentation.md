# Traffic Segmentation

Distributed systems continuously exchange:
- internal runtime traffic.

Traffic segmentation helps control:
- communication boundaries
- routing behavior
- and workload exposure.

---

# Why Segmentation Matters

Uncontrolled traffic paths may create:
- dependency sprawl
- operational instability
- and communication unpredictability.

---

# Runtime Communication Control

Traffic segmentation improves:
- workload isolation
- dependency protection
- and communication survivability.

---

# Operational Benefits

Segmentation reduces:
- cascading communication failures
- runtime congestion
- and dependency amplification.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Traffic segmentation improves:
- distributed-system stability at scale.

---

# Key Insight

Controlled communication paths improve runtime survivability significantly.
