# Scaling Cost Behavior

Distributed systems continuously scale through:
- replicas
- storage
- networking
- and runtime infrastructure expansion.

Scaling improves:
- survivability
- availability
- and performance.

But scaling also increases:
- infrastructure economics continuously.

---

# Why Scaling Economics Matter

Every scalability decision creates:
- operational cost impact.

Examples include:
- additional worker nodes
- more replicas
- more storage
- and increased network communication.

---

# Runtime Infrastructure Expansion

Scaling continuously amplifies:
- compute usage
- storage allocation
- and runtime communication behavior.

---

# Operational Challenges

Improper scaling behavior may create:
- runaway infrastructure growth
- unstable autoscaling
- and financially inefficient platforms.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Scaling economics becomes:
- distributed-system infrastructure sustainability engineering.

---

# Key Insight

Scalability continuously increases infrastructure consumption.
