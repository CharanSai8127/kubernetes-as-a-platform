# Redundancy Vs Cost

Distributed systems continuously depend on:
- redundancy for runtime survivability.

Redundancy improves:
- failover behavior
- workload continuity
- and infrastructure resilience.

---

# Why Redundancy Matters

Without redundancy,
distributed systems may experience:
- single points of failure
- runtime outages
- and survivability instability.

---

# Runtime Infrastructure Economics

Redundant systems commonly require:
- additional replicas
- backup infrastructure
- standby capacity
- and distributed synchronization.

---

# Operational Challenges

Excessive redundancy may create:
- idle infrastructure
- unnecessary cloud spend
- and inefficient runtime economics.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Redundancy becomes:
- survivability versus economics balancing engineering.

---

# Key Insight

Reliable distributed systems continuously balance redundancy and infrastructure efficiency together.
