# High Availability Economics

High availability improves:
- runtime survivability
- failover resilience
- and operational continuity.

But high availability also increases:
- infrastructure redundancy
- and cloud economics.

---

# Why Availability Economics Matter

Highly available systems commonly require:
- additional replicas
- multi-zone deployment
- storage replication
- and failover infrastructure.

---

# Runtime Survivability Costs

Availability engineering continuously increases:
- compute usage
- storage economics
- and distributed communication behavior.

---

# Operational Challenges

Improper availability planning may create:
- excessive redundancy
- idle failover capacity
- and inefficient infrastructure economics.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Availability engineering strongly impacts:
- economic survivability.

---

# Key Insight

Infrastructure survivability continuously increases operational economics.
