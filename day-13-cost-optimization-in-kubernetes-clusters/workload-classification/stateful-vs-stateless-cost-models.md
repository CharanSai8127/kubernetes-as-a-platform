# Stateful Vs Stateless Cost Models

Stateful and stateless workloads generate:
- different infrastructure economics.

Stateful workloads prioritize:
- consistency
- persistence
- and survivability.

Stateless workloads prioritize:
- elasticity
- restartability
- and runtime flexibility.

---

# Why Cost Models Matter

Stateful systems commonly require:
- persistent storage
- failover capacity
- and infrastructure stability.

Stateless systems commonly tolerate:
- interruption
- redistribution
- and autoscaling behavior.

---

# Runtime Infrastructure Economics

Stateful workloads continuously increase:
- storage economics
- replication cost
- and infrastructure survivability requirements.

Stateless workloads improve:
- infrastructure elasticity
- and cost optimization flexibility.

---

# Operational Challenges

Improper workload placement may create:
- runtime instability
- storage inconsistency
- and unnecessary infrastructure economics.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Workload architecture directly influences:
- infrastructure cost behavior.

---

# Key Insight

Stateful survivability continuously increases distributed-system infrastructure economics.
