# Interruption Tolerant Workloads

Some distributed workloads tolerate:
- restart
- eviction
- and runtime redistribution safely.

These workloads commonly prioritize:
- infrastructure efficiency over runtime continuity.

---

# Why Interruption Tolerance Matters

Interruption-tolerant workloads are strong candidates for:
- spot infrastructure
- autoscaling environments
- and dynamic workload placement.

---

# Runtime Workload Behavior

Examples commonly include:
- frontend workloads
- batch processing
- CI runners
- background workers
- and stateless APIs.

---

# Operational Challenges

Improper interruption handling may create:
- runtime instability
- traffic disruption
- and degraded workload survivability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Interruption tolerance improves:
- infrastructure economic optimization.

---

# Key Insight

Not all workloads require identical runtime survivability guarantees.
