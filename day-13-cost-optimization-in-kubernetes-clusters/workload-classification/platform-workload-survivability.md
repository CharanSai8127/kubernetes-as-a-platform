# Platform Workload Survivability

Core platform workloads continuously coordinate:
- cluster survivability
- networking
- observability
- and runtime orchestration.

These systems require:
- highly stable infrastructure.

---

# Why Platform Survivability Matters

Failure in core platform workloads may destabilize:
- the entire Kubernetes environment.

Examples include:
- DNS instability
- CNI failure
- observability outages
- and control-plane communication issues.

---

# Runtime Infrastructure Requirements

Platform workloads commonly require:
- on-demand infrastructure
- stable runtime continuity
- and predictable node availability.

---

# Operational Challenges

Improper platform workload placement may create:
- cascading cluster instability
- runtime outages
- and unhealthy failover behavior.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Platform survivability strongly impacts:
- distributed-system operational stability.

---

# Key Insight

Core platform systems should survive infrastructure volatility.
