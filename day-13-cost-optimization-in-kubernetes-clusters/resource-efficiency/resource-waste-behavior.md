# Resource Waste Behavior

Distributed systems may continuously generate:
- hidden infrastructure waste.

Waste commonly appears through:
- oversized requests
- idle workloads
- fragmented scheduling
- and unused infrastructure.

---

# Why Waste Matters

Cloud providers charge for:
- allocated infrastructure,
not:
- infrastructure usefulness.

---

# Runtime Waste Generation

Resource waste commonly includes:
- idle nodes
- unused storage
- abandoned services
- and overprovisioned workloads.

---

# Operational Challenges

Uncontrolled waste may create:
- financially inefficient platforms
- infrastructure sprawl
- and unstable scaling economics.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Resource waste directly impacts:
- infrastructure sustainability.

---

# Key Insight

Infrastructure waste continuously amplifies distributed-system economics.
