# Infrastructure Utilization

Infrastructure utilization measures:
- how effectively resources are consumed.

Efficient platforms maximize:
- workload density
- and runtime capacity usage.

---

# Why Utilization Matters

Partially unused infrastructure still generates:
- full infrastructure cost.

Examples:
- idle worker nodes
- oversized workloads
- and fragmented scheduling.

---

# Runtime Infrastructure Behavior

Efficient systems continuously optimize:
- compute usage
- memory utilization
- and workload placement.

---

# Operational Challenges

Poor utilization creates:
- unnecessary infrastructure expansion
- higher cloud spend
- and inefficient scaling behavior.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Infrastructure utilization directly impacts:
- economic survivability.

---

# Key Insight

Idle infrastructure remains financially active infrastructure waste.
