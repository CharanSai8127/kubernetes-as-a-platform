# Uncontrolled Infrastructure Growth

Distributed systems continuously evolve through:
- scaling
- replication
- redundancy
- and runtime expansion.

Without governance,
infrastructure growth may become:
- operationally and financially unstable.

---

# Why Infrastructure Growth Matters

As systems scale,
they continuously increase:
- compute usage
- storage allocation
- networking communication
- and observability infrastructure.

---

# Runtime Infrastructure Expansion

Improper scaling and optimization behavior may create:
- oversized clusters
- idle infrastructure
- and excessive redundancy.

---

# Operational Challenges

Uncontrolled growth may create:
- runaway cloud spend
- infrastructure sprawl
- and economically unsustainable platforms.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Infrastructure governance becomes:
- long-term economic survivability engineering.

---

# Key Insight

Scalable systems require controlled infrastructure growth behavior.
