# Inefficient Autoscaling

Autoscaling continuously modifies:
- infrastructure capacity during runtime operations.

Improper autoscaling behavior may create:
- unstable infrastructure economics.

---

# Why Autoscaling Efficiency Matters

Poor autoscaling behavior commonly creates:
- oversized clusters
- unstable node balancing
- and unnecessary infrastructure growth.

---

# Runtime Infrastructure Risks

Inefficient autoscaling commonly includes:
- repeated node scaling
- infrastructure thrashing
- and fragmented workload placement.

---

# Operational Challenges

Improper autoscaling governance may create:
- increased cloud spend
- unstable workloads
- and operational unpredictability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Autoscaling efficiency strongly impacts:
- economic survivability engineering.

---

# Key Insight

Infrastructure elasticity must remain operationally stable and economically efficient.
