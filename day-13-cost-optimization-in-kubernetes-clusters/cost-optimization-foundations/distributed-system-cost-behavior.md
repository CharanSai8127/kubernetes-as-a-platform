# Distributed System Cost Behavior

Distributed systems continuously consume:
- infrastructure resources.

Examples include:
- compute
- storage
- networking
- observability
- and runtime coordination.

---

# Why Cost Behavior Matters

As systems scale,
resource consumption also scales continuously.

Examples:
- more replicas
- more traffic
- more synchronization
- and more redundancy.

---

# Runtime Infrastructure Pressure

Distributed systems continuously generate:
- infrastructure economics through runtime operations.

---

# Operational Challenges

Improper scaling behavior may create:
- runaway cloud cost
- infrastructure fragmentation
- and inefficient resource utilization.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Distributed systems require:
- controlled infrastructure economics.

---

# Key Insight

Scalability continuously increases infrastructure consumption.
