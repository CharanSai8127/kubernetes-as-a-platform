# Economic Survivability

Reliable platforms must remain:
- operationally survivable
and:
- financially sustainable.

Economic survivability focuses on:
- balancing reliability and infrastructure efficiency.

---

# Why Economic Survivability Matters

Highly resilient systems commonly require:
- more replicas
- more storage
- more redundancy
- and more infrastructure capacity.

This continuously increases:
- infrastructure cost.

---

# Runtime Infrastructure Balance

Platforms continuously balance:
- survivability
- scalability
- performance
- and infrastructure economics.

---

# Operational Challenges

Uncontrolled infrastructure growth may create:
- financially unsustainable distributed systems.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Economic survivability becomes:
- runtime infrastructure efficiency engineering.

---

# Key Insight

Reliable systems must survive both failures and infrastructure economics.
