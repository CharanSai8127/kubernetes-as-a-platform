# Node Fragmentation

Node fragmentation occurs when:
- worker-node resources become partially unusable.

Fragmentation commonly appears because of:
- oversized workload requests
- inefficient scheduling
- and uneven resource allocation.

---

# Why Node Fragmentation Matters

Fragmented infrastructure may force:
- additional node provisioning
even when:
- unused capacity still exists.

---

# Runtime Scheduling Risks

Fragmented nodes reduce:
- scheduling flexibility
- workload density
- and autoscaling efficiency.

---

# Operational Challenges

Improper fragmentation handling may create:
- underutilized infrastructure
- unstable scaling behavior
- and increased cloud economics.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Node fragmentation directly impacts:
- infrastructure utilization optimization.

---

# Key Insight

Partially unusable infrastructure continuously amplifies operational economics.
