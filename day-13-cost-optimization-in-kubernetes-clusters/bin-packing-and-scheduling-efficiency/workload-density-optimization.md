# Workload Density Optimization

Workload density optimization focuses on:
- maximizing infrastructure utilization safely.

Higher workload density improves:
- runtime efficiency
- node utilization
- and cloud economics.

---

# Why Density Optimization Matters

Low-density clusters require:
- more worker nodes
- more infrastructure capacity
- and more operational cost.

---

# Runtime Infrastructure Behavior

Kubernetes continuously balances:
- workload placement
- runtime resource allocation
- and infrastructure capacity usage.

---

# Operational Challenges

Improper density optimization may create:
- resource contention
- workload instability
- and noisy-neighbor behavior.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Density optimization becomes:
- runtime infrastructure balancing engineering.

---

# Key Insight

Efficient workload density improves both scalability and infrastructure economics.
