# Scheduling Efficiency

Scheduling efficiency measures:
- how effectively workloads are distributed across infrastructure nodes.

Efficient scheduling improves:
- workload survivability
- runtime density
- and infrastructure economics.

---

# Why Scheduling Efficiency Matters

Inefficient scheduling may create:
- fragmented clusters
- poor node utilization
- and unnecessary infrastructure growth.

---

# Runtime Infrastructure Coordination

Kubernetes continuously evaluates:
- node capacity
- workload requests
- and scheduling constraints.

Scheduling decisions directly impact:
- cluster efficiency
- and runtime scalability.

---

# Operational Challenges

Improper scheduling behavior may create:
- pending workloads
- inefficient autoscaling
- and unstable runtime balancing.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Scheduling efficiency strongly impacts:
- infrastructure sustainability engineering.

---

# Key Insight

Efficient scheduling continuously improves workload density and infrastructure utilization.
