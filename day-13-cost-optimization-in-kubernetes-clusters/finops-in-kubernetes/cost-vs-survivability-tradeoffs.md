# Cost Vs Survivability Tradeoffs

Distributed systems continuously balance:
- survivability
- performance
- scalability
- and infrastructure economics.

Improving survivability commonly increases:
- infrastructure consumption.

---

# Why Tradeoffs Matter

Highly resilient systems commonly require:
- additional replicas
- storage replication
- failover capacity
- and distributed coordination.

This continuously increases:
- cloud economics.

---

# Runtime Infrastructure Decisions

Platforms continuously choose between:
- lower cost
and:
- higher survivability guarantees.

Examples include:
- spot versus on-demand infrastructure
- redundancy levels
- and cross-zone deployment strategies.

---

# Operational Challenges

Improper tradeoff decisions may create:
- runtime instability
- excessive cloud spend
- or economically unsustainable infrastructure.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Infrastructure tradeoffs become:
- survivability economics engineering.

---

# Key Insight

Infrastructure cost optimization continuously introduces survivability tradeoffs.
