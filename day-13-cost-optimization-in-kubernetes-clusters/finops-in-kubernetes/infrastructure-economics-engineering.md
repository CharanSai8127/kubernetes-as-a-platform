# Infrastructure Economics Engineering

Distributed systems continuously generate:
- infrastructure economics during runtime operations.

Infrastructure economics engineering focuses on:
- balancing survivability and resource efficiency.

---

# Why Economics Engineering Matters

Reliable systems commonly require:
- redundancy
- failover capacity
- observability
- and distributed communication.

These continuously increase:
- infrastructure consumption.

---

# Runtime Infrastructure Coordination

Platforms continuously optimize:
- workload density
- autoscaling behavior
- and infrastructure allocation.

---

# Operational Challenges

Improper optimization may create:
- uncontrolled cloud spend
- fragmented infrastructure
- and operational inefficiency.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Infrastructure economics becomes:
- distributed-system sustainability engineering.

---

# Key Insight

Efficient platforms continuously balance reliability, scalability, and infrastructure economics together.
