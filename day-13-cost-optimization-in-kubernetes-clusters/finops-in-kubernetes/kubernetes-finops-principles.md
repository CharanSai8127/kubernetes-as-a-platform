# Kubernetes FinOps Principles

Kubernetes continuously orchestrates:
- distributed infrastructure consumption.

FinOps principles improve:
- infrastructure efficiency
- workload survivability
- and economic sustainability.

---

# Why FinOps Principles Matter

Cloud infrastructure continuously charges for:
- allocated runtime resources.

Improper infrastructure behavior may create:
- hidden cloud spend
- inefficient scaling
- and infrastructure waste.

---

# Runtime Infrastructure Optimization

Kubernetes FinOps commonly focuses on:
- workload right sizing
- autoscaling efficiency
- workload classification
- and infrastructure utilization.

---

# Operational Challenges

Improper optimization may create:
- idle infrastructure
- oversized workloads
- and unstable runtime economics.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

FinOps principles become:
- runtime infrastructure governance engineering.

---

# Key Insight

Kubernetes makes infrastructure programmable, which also makes infrastructure waste programmable.
