# Runtime Resource Fragmentation

Fragmentation occurs when:
- infrastructure capacity becomes partially unusable.

Distributed systems continuously create fragmentation through:
- oversized workloads
- inefficient scheduling
- and uneven resource allocation.

---

# Why Fragmentation Matters

Fragmented infrastructure may force:
- unnecessary node provisioning
even when:
- unused capacity still exists.

---

# Runtime Scheduling Behavior

Kubernetes continuously schedules workloads based on:
- allocatable runtime resources.

Fragmentation reduces:
- workload density
- scheduling flexibility
- and infrastructure efficiency.

---

# Operational Challenges

Improper fragmentation management may create:
- underutilized nodes
- unstable autoscaling
- and increased cloud spend.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Fragmentation directly impacts:
- infrastructure utilization efficiency.

---

# Key Insight

Partially unusable infrastructure continuously increases operational economics.
