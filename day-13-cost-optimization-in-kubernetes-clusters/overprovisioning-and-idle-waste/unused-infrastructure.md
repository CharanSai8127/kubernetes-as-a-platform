# Unused Infrastructure

Distributed systems continuously generate:
- unused infrastructure components during runtime evolution.

Examples include:
- orphaned volumes
- stale snapshots
- unused clusters
- inactive services
- and abandoned networking resources.

---

# Why Unused Infrastructure Matters

Unused infrastructure silently generates:
- recurring cloud economics.

Many infrastructure components continue billing even when:
- workloads no longer use them.

---

# Runtime Infrastructure Lifecycle

Infrastructure continuously evolves through:
- scaling
- deployment changes
- workload removal
- and operational drift.

---

# Operational Challenges

Improper cleanup behavior may create:
- infrastructure sprawl
- hidden cloud spend
- and operational inefficiency.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Infrastructure lifecycle cleanup strongly impacts:
- economic survivability.

---

# Key Insight

Unused infrastructure silently amplifies distributed-system economics.
