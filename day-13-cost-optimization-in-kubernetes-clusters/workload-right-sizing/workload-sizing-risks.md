# Workload Sizing Risks

Incorrect workload sizing introduces:
- operational instability
- infrastructure waste
- and runtime inefficiency.

Distributed systems continuously depend on:
- predictable resource allocation.

---

# Why Sizing Risks Matter

Oversized workloads create:
- unused infrastructure consumption.

Undersized workloads create:
- runtime instability
- performance degradation
- and unhealthy scaling behavior.

---

# Runtime Infrastructure Risks

Improper sizing may impact:
- autoscaling
- scheduling efficiency
- and workload survivability.

---

# Operational Challenges

Sizing instability may create:
- runaway cloud cost
- resource contention
- and fragmented infrastructure usage.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Sizing accuracy strongly impacts:
- economic survivability.

---

# Key Insight

Improper workload sizing destabilizes both runtime behavior and infrastructure economics.
