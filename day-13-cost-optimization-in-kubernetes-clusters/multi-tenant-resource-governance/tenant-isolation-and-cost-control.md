# Tenant Isolation And Cost Control

Multi-tenant Kubernetes platforms continuously share:
- infrastructure economics across teams and workloads.

Tenant isolation improves:
- runtime safety
- infrastructure governance
- and economic control.

---

# Why Isolation Matters

Without isolation,
one tenant may create:
- excessive infrastructure consumption
- runaway scaling
- or runtime instability.

This may impact:
- all workloads sharing the cluster.

---

# Runtime Infrastructure Governance

Isolation commonly includes:
- namespaces
- quotas
- RBAC
- policy enforcement
- and workload segmentation.

---

# Operational Challenges

Improper isolation may create:
- uncontrolled cloud spend
- noisy neighbors
- and operational instability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Tenant isolation becomes:
- multi-tenant infrastructure survivability engineering.

---

# Key Insight

Shared infrastructure requires controlled economic and operational boundaries.
