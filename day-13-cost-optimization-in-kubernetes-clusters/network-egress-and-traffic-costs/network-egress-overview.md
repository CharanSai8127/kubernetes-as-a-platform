# Network Egress Overview

Distributed systems continuously generate:
- runtime network communication.

Cloud providers commonly charge for:
- outbound traffic
- cross-zone traffic
- and cross-region communication.

---

# Why Network Economics Matter

As distributed systems scale,
runtime communication also scales continuously.

Examples include:
- service-to-service traffic
- replication traffic
- observability traffic
- and backup synchronization.

---

# Runtime Communication Behavior

Modern platforms continuously depend on:
- east-west traffic
- north-south traffic
- and distributed synchronization behavior.

---

# Operational Challenges

Improper traffic design may create:
- excessive egress cost
- communication latency
- and inefficient infrastructure economics.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Network communication becomes:
- distributed-system economic behavior.

---

# Key Insight

Distributed communication continuously increases infrastructure economics.
