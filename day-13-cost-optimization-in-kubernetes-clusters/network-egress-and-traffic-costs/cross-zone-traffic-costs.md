# Cross Zone Traffic Costs

Cloud providers commonly charge for:
- traffic crossing availability-zone boundaries.

Distributed systems continuously generate:
- cross-zone runtime communication.

---

# Why Cross Zone Traffic Matters

High-availability architectures commonly spread workloads across:
- multiple zones.

This improves:
- survivability
- and failover resilience.

But it also increases:
- networking economics.

---

# Runtime Infrastructure Communication

Cross-zone communication commonly includes:
- database replication
- observability traffic
- service synchronization
- and distributed storage behavior.

---

# Operational Challenges

Improper workload placement may create:
- excessive cross-zone communication
- increased latency
- and amplified infrastructure economics.
