# Distributed Communication Costs

Distributed systems continuously depend on:
- runtime communication between workloads.

Communication economics commonly include:
- service traffic
- synchronization traffic
- observability traffic
- and replication traffic.

---

# Why Communication Economics Matter

As distributed systems scale,
runtime communication also scales continuously.

Examples include:
- retries
- failover synchronization
- service discovery
- and telemetry collection.

---

# Runtime Traffic Behavior

Distributed workloads continuously exchange:
- runtime operational data across infrastructure boundaries.

---

# Operational Challenges

Improper communication behavior may create:
- excessive network economics
- latency amplification
- and unstable infrastructure scaling.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Runtime communication strongly impacts:
- distributed-system infrastructure sustainability.

---

# Key Insight

Distributed-system coordination continuously increases infrastructure economics.
