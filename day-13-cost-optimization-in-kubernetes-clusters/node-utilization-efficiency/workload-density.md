# Workload Density

Workload density measures:
- how many workloads efficiently fit onto worker-node infrastructure.

Higher density improves:
- infrastructure utilization
- runtime efficiency
- and cloud economics.

---

# Why Workload Density Matters

Low-density clusters require:
- more worker nodes
- more infrastructure
- and more operational cost.

---

# Runtime Scheduling Behavior

Kubernetes continuously balances:
- workload placement
- resource allocation
- and runtime capacity usage.

Efficient workload density improves:
- node efficiency
- and scheduling optimization.

---

# Operational Challenges

Improper workload density may create:
- infrastructure fragmentation
- unstable scaling
- and inefficient resource usage.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Workload density becomes:
- infrastructure optimization engineering.

---

# Key Insight

Efficient workload density reduces unnecessary infrastructure expansion.
