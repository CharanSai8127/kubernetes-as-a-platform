# Autoscaling Instability

Autoscaling continuously modifies:
- runtime infrastructure capacity.

Improper scaling behavior may introduce:
- runtime instability
- infrastructure thrashing
- and workload disruption.

---

# Why Autoscaling Instability Matters

Rapid scaling events may create:
- unstable workload placement
- resource exhaustion
- and inefficient infrastructure behavior.

---

# Runtime Infrastructure Risks

Autoscaling instability commonly includes:
- repeated node creation
- repeated node removal
- and oscillating workload behavior.

---

# Operational Challenges

Unstable autoscaling may create:
- increased cloud cost
- scheduling delays
- and runtime unpredictability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Autoscaling stability strongly impacts:
- infrastructure survivability engineering.

---

# Key Insight

Infrastructure elasticity must remain controlled and predictable.
