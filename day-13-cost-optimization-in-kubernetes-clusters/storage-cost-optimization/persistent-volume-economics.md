# Persistent Volume Economics

Persistent volumes continuously provide:
- runtime storage survivability for stateful workloads.

Stateful systems require:
- durable storage allocation
- runtime persistence
- and infrastructure continuity.

---

# Why Persistent Volume Economics Matter

Persistent storage commonly remains allocated:
- even after workload scaling or runtime inactivity.

This continuously generates:
- recurring cloud cost.

---

# Runtime Storage Allocation

Stateful workloads commonly depend on:
- dedicated storage volumes
- replication
- and backup infrastructure.

Examples include:
- databases
- message queues
- and persistent application storage.

---

# Operational Challenges

Improper storage allocation may create:
- oversized volumes
- orphaned storage
- and inefficient storage utilization.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Persistent storage allocation strongly impacts:
- distributed-system infrastructure economics.

---

# Key Insight

Stateful survivability continuously increases persistent storage economics.
