# Storage Cost Behavior

Distributed systems continuously generate:
- storage infrastructure consumption.

Storage economics commonly include:
- persistent volumes
- snapshots
- backups
- replication
- and cross-region storage.

---

# Why Storage Cost Matters

Stateful systems continuously increase:
- infrastructure survivability requirements.

More survivability commonly requires:
- more storage redundancy
- more replication
- and more backup infrastructure.

---

# Runtime Storage Economics

Storage infrastructure continuously generates:
- recurring operational cost.

Unlike compute,
storage often remains allocated even when:
- workloads become inactive.

---

# Operational Challenges

Improper storage lifecycle management may create:
- unused persistent volumes
- stale snapshots
- and hidden cloud spend.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Storage economics strongly impact:
- infrastructure sustainability engineering.

---

# Key Insight

Distributed storage survivability continuously increases infrastructure economics.
