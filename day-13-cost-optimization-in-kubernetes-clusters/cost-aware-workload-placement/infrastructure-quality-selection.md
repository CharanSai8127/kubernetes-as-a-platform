# Infrastructure Quality Selection

Different workloads require:
- different infrastructure survivability guarantees.

Infrastructure quality continuously balances:
- stability
- availability
- and cloud economics.

---

# Why Infrastructure Selection Matters

Critical workloads require:
- stable runtime continuity.

Interruption-tolerant workloads may prioritize:
- infrastructure efficiency.

---

# Runtime Infrastructure Classification

On-demand infrastructure commonly supports:
- stateful systems
- platform workloads
- and survivability-critical services.

Spot infrastructure commonly supports:
- stateless workloads
- batch jobs
- and interruption-tolerant systems.

---

# Operational Challenges

Improper infrastructure selection may create:
- runtime instability
- workload eviction
- and operational disruption.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Infrastructure classification strongly impacts:
- economic survivability engineering.

---

# Key Insight

Workload survivability requirements should continuously influence infrastructure quality decisions.
