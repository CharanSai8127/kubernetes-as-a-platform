# Workload Placement Strategies

Distributed workloads require:
- infrastructure placement decisions based on survivability and economics.

Not all workloads require:
- identical infrastructure quality.

---

# Why Placement Matters

Improper placement may create:
- runtime instability
- unnecessary cloud spend
- and inefficient infrastructure usage.

---

# Runtime Infrastructure Coordination

Kubernetes continuously schedules workloads based on:
- resource availability
- scheduling policies
- and workload constraints.

Placement strategies improve:
- survivability
- workload density
- and infrastructure economics.

---

# Operational Challenges

Improper placement behavior may create:
- unstable failover
- fragmented clusters
- and infrastructure inefficiency.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Workload placement becomes:
- runtime infrastructure optimization engineering.

---

# Key Insight

Infrastructure quality should align with workload survivability requirements.
