# Reliability Vs Cost

Reliable distributed systems continuously require:
- redundancy
- failover capacity
- observability
- and runtime coordination.

These survivability improvements continuously increase:
- infrastructure economics.

# Why Reliability Economics Matter

Higher reliability commonly requires:
- additional replicas
- multi-zone deployment
- storage replication
- and backup infrastructure.

This creates:
- operational tradeoffs between survivability and economics.

# Runtime Infrastructure Balance

- runtime continuity

- and infrastructure efficiency.
