# Economic Sustainability

Reliable systems must remain:
- operationally survivable
and:
- financially sustainable.

Economic sustainability focuses on:
- long-term infrastructure efficiency.

---

# Why Sustainability Matters

Distributed systems continuously grow through:
- scaling
- replication
- observability
- and runtime coordination.

Without economic governance,
infrastructure growth may become:
- financially unsustainable.

---

# Runtime Infrastructure Governance

Sustainable platforms continuously optimize:
- workload density
- infrastructure utilization
- autoscaling behavior
- and runtime efficiency.

---

# Operational Challenges

Improper optimization may create:
- runaway infrastructure growth
- hidden cloud spend
- and operational instability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Economic sustainability becomes:
- distributed-system survivability engineering.

---

# Key Insight

Reliable distributed systems must survive both runtime instability and infrastructure economics.
