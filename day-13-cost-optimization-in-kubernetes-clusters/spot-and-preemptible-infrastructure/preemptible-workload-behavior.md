# Preemptible Workload Behavior

Preemptible infrastructure continuously operates through:
- interruption-based runtime availability.

Workloads deployed on preemptible nodes must tolerate:
- restart
- eviction
- and runtime redistribution.

---

# Why Preemptible Workloads Matter

Not all workloads require:
- identical infrastructure survivability guarantees.

Some workloads prioritize:
- infrastructure economics over runtime continuity.

---

# Runtime Infrastructure Coordination

Preemptible workloads commonly include:
- batch processing
- CI workloads
- frontend services
- and interruption-tolerant APIs.

---

# Operational Challenges

Improper workload classification may create:
- runtime instability
- unhealthy failover behavior
- and operational disruption.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Workload interruption tolerance strongly impacts:
- infrastructure economics engineering.

---

# Key Insight

Infrastructure survivability requirements should influence workload placement decisions.
