# Infrastructure Interruption Risk

Spot infrastructure continuously carries:
- interruption probability.

Cloud providers may reclaim:
- temporary infrastructure capacity
during:
- changing market demand.

---

# Why Interruption Risk Matters

Infrastructure interruption may create:
- workload eviction
- pod rescheduling
- and runtime instability.

Critical workloads therefore require:
- stable infrastructure survivability guarantees.

---

# Runtime Infrastructure Risks

Interruption events commonly impact:
- node availability
- scheduling stability
- and workload continuity.

---

# Operational Challenges

Improper interruption handling may create:
- cascading workload failures
- unstable autoscaling
- and degraded runtime survivability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Infrastructure interruption becomes:
- runtime economic survivability tradeoff engineering.

---

# Key Insight

Lower infrastructure cost commonly increases interruption probability.
