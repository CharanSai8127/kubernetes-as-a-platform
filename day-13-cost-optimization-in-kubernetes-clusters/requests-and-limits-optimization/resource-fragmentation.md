# Resource Fragmentation

Resource fragmentation occurs when:
- infrastructure capacity becomes partially unusable.

Fragmentation commonly appears through:
- oversized requests
- inefficient workload placement
- and uneven resource allocation.

---

# Why Fragmentation Matters

Fragmented infrastructure may force:
- unnecessary node creation
even when:
- unused capacity still exists.

---

# Runtime Scheduling Behavior

Kubernetes scheduling continuously depends on:
- available allocatable resources.

Improper workload sizing reduces:
- scheduling efficiency.

---

# Operational Challenges

Fragmentation may create:
- poor node utilization
- infrastructure sprawl
- and unnecessary cloud spend.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Resource fragmentation strongly impacts:
- workload density optimization.

---

# Key Insight

Partially unusable infrastructure continuously increases distributed-system economics.
