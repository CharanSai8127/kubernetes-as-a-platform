# Reproducible Infrastructure

Infrastructure should remain:
- reproducible across environments.

Reproducibility improves:
- consistency
- predictability
- and operational reliability.

---

# Why Reproducibility Matters

Non-reproducible infrastructure creates:
- inconsistent runtime behavior
- deployment surprises
- and debugging complexity.

---

# Declarative Infrastructure

Terraform uses:
- declarative infrastructure definitions.

Infrastructure becomes:
- desired-state driven
instead of:
- manually configured.

---

# Platform Engineering Benefits

Reproducibility improves:
- recovery speed
- scaling consistency
- and environment parity.

---

# Operational Stability

Consistent infrastructure behavior reduces:
- operational surprises during deployments and scaling.

---

# Key Insight

Reproducible infrastructure improves operational reliability significantly.
