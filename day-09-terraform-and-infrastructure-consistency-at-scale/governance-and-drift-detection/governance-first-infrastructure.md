# Governance First Infrastructure

Governance infrastructure may be provisioned:
- before workload infrastructure.

This creates:
- preventive operational governance.

---

# Typical Governance Layer

Examples include:
- AWS Config
- CloudTrail
- IAM guardrails
- centralized logging
- and policy validation systems.

---

# Why Governance First Matters

Governance infrastructure helps detect:
- drift
- policy violations
- and operational inconsistency early.

---

# Operational Benefits

Preventive governance improves:
- infrastructure visibility
- compliance awareness
- and operational predictability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Infrastructure governance itself should remain:
- declarative
- reproducible
- and version-controlled.

---

# Key Insight

Preventive governance reduces operational unpredictability over time.
