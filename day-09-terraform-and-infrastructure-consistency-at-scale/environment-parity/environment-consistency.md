# Environment Consistency

Infrastructure should behave:
- predictably across environments.

Applications should not experience:
- different operational behavior
between:
- development
- staging
- and production.

---

# Why Environment Consistency Matters

Infrastructure inconsistency creates:
- deployment unpredictability
- runtime instability
- and debugging complexity.

---

# Common Inconsistencies

Examples include:
- different IAM permissions
- networking differences
- storage mismatches
- inconsistent node groups
- and observability gaps.

---

# Operational Impact

Applications may behave differently even when:
- deployment manifests remain unchanged.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Consistent infrastructure improves:
- deployment confidence
- rollback predictability
- and operational reliability.

---

# Key Insight

Environment inconsistency eventually becomes deployment unpredictability.
