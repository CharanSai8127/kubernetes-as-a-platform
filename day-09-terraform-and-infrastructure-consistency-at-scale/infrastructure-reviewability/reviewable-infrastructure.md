# Reviewable Infrastructure

Infrastructure should remain:
- reviewable
- auditable
- and version-controlled.

Operational visibility improves:
- infrastructure reliability
- deployment predictability
- and governance.

---

# Why Reviewability Matters

Infrastructure changes may impact:
- networking
- IAM
- storage
- scaling
- and workload behavior.

Reviewability helps detect:
- operational risks before deployment.

---

# Declarative Change Visibility

Terraform improves reviewability through:
- declarative infrastructure changes.

Infrastructure modifications become:
- visible
- traceable
- and reviewable before execution.

---

# Operational Benefits

Reviewable infrastructure improves:
- collaboration
- governance
- rollback confidence
- and operational accountability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Infrastructure should evolve through:
- observable operational workflows
instead of:
- uncontrolled runtime mutation.

---

# Key Insight

Infrastructure visibility improves operational consistency significantly.
