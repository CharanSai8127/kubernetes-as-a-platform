# Governance Cost Tradeoffs

Infrastructure governance improves:
- visibility
- auditability
- and operational consistency.

But governance itself also introduces:
- operational cost.

---

# Without Governance Infrastructure

Minimal governance environments commonly have:
- lower cost
- reduced complexity
- and simpler infrastructure management.

But they also have:
- weaker drift visibility
- limited auditability
- and increased operational unpredictability.

---

# With Governance Infrastructure

Governed environments improve:
- compliance awareness
- drift detection
- infrastructure visibility
- and operational accountability.

But governance may increase:
- logging cost
- rule evaluation cost
- and operational complexity.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Governance should scale according to:
- operational criticality
instead of:
- blindly across every environment.

---

# Key Insight

Infrastructure governance requires balancing operational visibility with cost efficiency.
