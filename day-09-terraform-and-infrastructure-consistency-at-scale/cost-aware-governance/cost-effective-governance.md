# Cost Effective Governance

Governance should remain:
- operationally valuable
without becoming:
- unnecessarily expensive.

---

# Cost Optimization Strategies

Examples include:
- tracking only critical resources
- reducing excessive logging
- limiting unnecessary compliance rules
- and environment-specific governance.

---

# Environment Tiering

Different environments may require:
- different governance intensity.

Examples:

Development
→ minimal governance

Staging
→ moderate governance

Production
→ strict governance

---

# Why Tiering Matters

Not all environments require:
- enterprise-level compliance visibility.

This reduces:
- unnecessary operational cost.

---

# Operational Benefits

Cost-aware governance improves:
- operational efficiency
- infrastructure visibility
- and platform scalability.

---

# Key Insight

Effective governance focuses on operational value instead of maximum visibility everywhere.
