# Immutable Infrastructure Thinking

Immutable infrastructure avoids:
- modifying infrastructure manually after deployment.

Instead:
- infrastructure gets replaced consistently through declarative workflows.

---

# Why Immutable Thinking Matters

Manual infrastructure modification introduces:
- drift
- inconsistency
- and operational unpredictability.

---

# Operational Benefits

Immutable infrastructure improves:
- reproducibility
- rollback consistency
- and deployment reliability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Infrastructure should evolve through:
- controlled declarative changes
instead of:
- uncontrolled runtime mutation.

---

# Kubernetes Connection

Kubernetes itself follows:
- declarative reconciliation thinking.

Infrastructure consistency benefits from:
- similar operational principles.

---

# Key Insight

Immutable infrastructure reduces configuration unpredictability over time.
