# Scaling Consistency

Scaling behavior should remain:
- predictable across environments.

Infrastructure inconsistency may create:
- unstable autoscaling behavior
- scheduling unpredictability
- and runtime instability.

---

# Common Scaling Issues

Examples include:
- inconsistent node groups
- uneven resource allocation
- insufficient isolation
- and workload contention.

---

# Kubernetes Connection

Kubernetes scaling depends heavily on:
- underlying infrastructure consistency.

Node availability
networking
storage
and workload isolation

all influence:
- scaling reliability.

---

# Operational Consequences

Inconsistent scaling infrastructure may create:
- startup storms
- resource exhaustion
- and dependency overload.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Scaling consistency improves:
- operational predictability
- and workload survivability.

---

# Key Insight

Infrastructure consistency strongly influences scaling reliability.
