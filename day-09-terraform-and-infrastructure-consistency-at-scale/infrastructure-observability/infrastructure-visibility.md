# Infrastructure Visibility

Operational reliability depends heavily on:
- infrastructure visibility.

Teams should continuously understand:
- infrastructure state
- resource ownership
- runtime changes
- and operational drift.

---

# Why Visibility Matters

Infrastructure environments continuously evolve through:
- deployments
- scaling
- manual changes
- and runtime activity.

Without visibility:
- operational debugging becomes difficult.

---

# Common Visibility Areas

Examples include:
- infrastructure state
- IAM changes
- networking updates
- scaling behavior
- and compliance drift.

---

# Operational Benefits

Infrastructure visibility improves:
- recovery speed
- auditability
- governance
- and operational confidence.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

- long-term platform reliability.
