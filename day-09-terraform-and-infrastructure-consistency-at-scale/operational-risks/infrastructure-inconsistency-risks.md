# Infrastructure Inconsistency Risks

Infrastructure inconsistency eventually creates:
- operational instability.

As environments drift over time:
- deployments become unpredictable.

---

# Common Risks

Examples include:
- inconsistent IAM behavior
- networking drift
- orphaned infrastructure
- workload scheduling instability
- and scaling unpredictability.

---

# Operational Consequences

Applications may behave differently across:
- staging
- production
- or disaster recovery environments.

---

# Recovery Complexity

Infrastructure inconsistency increases:
- debugging difficulty
- recovery time
- and operational uncertainty.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Reliable platforms require:
- predictable infrastructure foundations.

---

# Key Insight

Infrastructure inconsistency eventually becomes operational unpredictability.
