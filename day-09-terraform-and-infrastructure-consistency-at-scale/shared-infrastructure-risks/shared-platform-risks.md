# Shared Platform Risks

Shared infrastructure may introduce:
- operational bottlenecks
- and dependency amplification.

---

# Common Shared Infrastructure

Examples include:
- shared VPCs
- centralized ingress
- shared observability systems
- shared databases
- and shared networking layers.

---

# Why Shared Risks Matter

Failures in shared systems may propagate across:
- multiple environments
- teams
- or workloads.

---

# Operational Consequences

Shared infrastructure failures may create:
- cascading instability
- scaling bottlenecks
- and noisy-neighbor problems.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Infrastructure consistency also requires:
- dependency isolation where necessary.

---

# Key Insight

Shared infrastructure increases operational blast radius significantly.
