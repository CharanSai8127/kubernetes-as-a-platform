# Terraform And GitOps

Terraform and GitOps solve:
- different infrastructure problems.

But together they improve:
- operational consistency significantly.

---

# Terraform Responsibility

Terraform manages:
- infrastructure desired state.

Examples include:
- VPCs
- IAM
- EKS
- networking
- and cloud resources.

---

# GitOps Responsibility

GitOps manages:
- Kubernetes desired state.

Examples include:
- workloads
- deployments
- applications
- and cluster configuration.

---

# Operational Relationship

Terraform establishes:
- infrastructure foundations.

GitOps reconciles:
- workload state on top of that infrastructure.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Infrastructure reconciliation
and
workload reconciliation

become:
- connected operational systems.

---

# Key Insight

Terraform manages infrastructure consistency.
GitOps manages workload consistency.
