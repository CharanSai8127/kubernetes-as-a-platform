# Environment Tiering

Different environments may require:
- different operational controls.

But infrastructure behavior should still remain:
- predictable and consistent.

---

# Typical Environment Layers

Examples include:
- development
- staging
- and production.

---

# Why Tiering Matters

Production systems commonly require:
- stronger governance
- stricter observability
- and enhanced operational safeguards.

Development environments may prioritize:
- flexibility and cost efficiency.

---

# Operational Risks

Excessive differences between environments create:
- deployment unpredictability
- and inconsistent runtime behavior.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Environment tiering should balance:
- operational consistency
- governance
- and cost efficiency.

---

# Key Insight

Environment behavior should remain predictable even when governance levels differ.
