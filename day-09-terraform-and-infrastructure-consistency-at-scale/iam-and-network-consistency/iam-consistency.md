# IAM Consistency

IAM consistency is critical for:
- predictable infrastructure behavior.

Permission inconsistency may create:
- deployment failures
- runtime instability
- and operational unpredictability.

---

# Common IAM Drift

Examples include:
- manually modified IAM policies
- inconsistent role permissions
- missing trust relationships
- and unmanaged access changes.

---

# Operational Risks

Applications may fail due to:
- unexpected permission denial
- inconsistent role assumptions
- or missing infrastructure access.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

IAM should remain:
- declarative
- reviewable
- and consistently version-controlled.

---

# Environment Consistency

Infrastructure environments should maintain:
- predictable permission behavior across deployments.

---

# Key Insight

IAM inconsistency eventually becomes runtime unpredictability.
