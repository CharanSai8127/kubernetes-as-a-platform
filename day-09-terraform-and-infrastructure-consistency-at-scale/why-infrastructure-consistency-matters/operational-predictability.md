# Operational Predictability

Infrastructure consistency improves:
- operational predictability.

Production systems behave more reliably when:
- infrastructure remains consistent across environments.

---

# Why Predictability Matters

Inconsistent infrastructure creates:
- deployment instability
- debugging difficulty
- scaling unpredictability
- and runtime inconsistency.

---

# Common Infrastructure Differences

Examples include:
- different IAM permissions
- subnet differences
- inconsistent storage classes
- routing inconsistencies
- and varying node behavior.

---

# Operational Impact

Applications may behave differently even when:
- the deployment itself remains unchanged.

---

# Platform Reliability

Consistent infrastructure improves:
- deployment confidence
- recovery reliability
- and operational stability.

---

# Key Insight

Infrastructure predictability is critical for platform reliability.
