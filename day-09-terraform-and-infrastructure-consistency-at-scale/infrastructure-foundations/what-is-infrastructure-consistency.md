# What Is Infrastructure Consistency

Infrastructure consistency focuses on:
- predictable infrastructure behavior across environments.

As systems scale, infrastructure should remain:
- reproducible
- declarative
- reviewable
- and operationally stable.

---

# Why Consistency Matters

Infrastructure inconsistency eventually creates:
- operational unpredictability.

Examples include:
- networking differences
- IAM drift
- inconsistent node groups
- storage mismatch
- and deployment instability.

---

# Platform Engineering Perspective

Reliable platforms require:
- consistent infrastructure foundations.

Kubernetes reliability depends heavily on:
- the infrastructure underneath it.

---

# Declarative Infrastructure

Infrastructure should evolve through:
- declarative desired state
instead of:
- uncontrolled manual mutation.

---

# Key Insight

Infrastructure consistency reduces operational surprises at scale.
