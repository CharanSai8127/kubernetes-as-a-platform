# Metrics Pipeline

Observability systems depend on reliable telemetry collection pipelines.

In Kubernetes environments, metrics continuously flow from:
- workloads
- nodes
- Kubernetes objects
- and platform services

toward centralized monitoring systems.

This telemetry pipeline forms the foundation of operational visibility.

---

# How the Pipeline Works

Metrics are generated by:
- applications
- exporters
- Kubernetes components
- and infrastructure services

Prometheus scrapes these metrics continuously from configured endpoints.

The collected telemetry is then:
- stored
- queried
- visualized
- and used for alerting

during operational analysis.

---

# Why Metrics Pipelines Matter

Without reliable telemetry collection:
- dashboards lose accuracy
- alerts become unreliable
- and debugging visibility decreases significantly

Distributed systems require continuous operational visibility into:
- workload behavior
- infrastructure pressure
- latency
- saturation
- and system health

---

# Kubernetes Perspective

Kubernetes environments are highly dynamic.

Workloads continuously:
- scale
- restart
- move across nodes
- and change operational state

Metrics pipelines must adapt continuously to these changes.

Prometheus and exporters help maintain operational visibility despite workload dynamism.

---

# Reliability Engineering

Reliable observability systems depend on:
- consistent telemetry collection
- scalable metric ingestion
- and operational signal quality

Poor metrics pipelines may create:
- missing visibility
- delayed telemetry
- and unreliable monitoring behavior

during production failures.

---

# Key Insight

Metrics pipelines continuously transform distributed-system behavior into observable operational signals.
