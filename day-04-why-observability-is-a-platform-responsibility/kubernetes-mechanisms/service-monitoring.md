# Service Monitoring

Distributed systems communicate through services continuously.

Reliable platforms require visibility into:
- request behavior
- service health
- dependency communication
- and operational latency

Service monitoring helps expose these interactions operationally.

---

# Why Service Visibility Matters

Applications rarely operate independently.

Services continuously depend on:
- APIs
- databases
- queues
- and networking layers

Instability inside one dependency may propagate across the entire system.

Service monitoring helps engineers:
- identify latency
- detect failures
- and understand traffic behavior

during production conditions.

---

# Kubernetes Perspective

Kubernetes environments continuously:
- scale services
- replace workloads
- update endpoints
- and rebalance traffic

This creates changing request paths and operational conditions.

Monitoring service behavior becomes critical for understanding:
- distributed-system health
- dependency pressure
- and operational propagation

inside Kubernetes platforms.

---

# Operational Visibility

Service monitoring helps expose:
- request latency
- error rates
- traffic patterns
- and dependency instability

This improves:
- debugging
- failure correlation
- and incident response

during production failures.

---

# Reliability Engineering

Reliable systems depend heavily on:
- healthy service communication
- operational visibility
- and latency awareness

Observability systems help engineers reason about:
- request flow
- propagation behavior
- and distributed-system interactions

under changing operational conditions.

---

# Key Insight

Service monitoring exposes how distributed systems communicate, propagate failures, and behave under production pressure.
