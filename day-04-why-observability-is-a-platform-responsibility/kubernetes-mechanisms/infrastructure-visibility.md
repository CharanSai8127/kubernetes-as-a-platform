# Infrastructure Visibility

Applications ultimately depend on infrastructure behavior.

Even healthy workloads may experience instability when:
- nodes saturate
- networking degrades
- disks slow down
- or infrastructure resources become exhausted

Infrastructure visibility helps engineers understand these operational conditions clearly.

---

# Why Infrastructure Visibility Matters

Infrastructure problems often propagate toward:
- applications
- request latency
- scaling behavior
- and distributed-system reliability

Examples include:
- node pressure
- memory exhaustion
- filesystem saturation
- and networking bottlenecks

Without infrastructure visibility:
- debugging becomes incomplete
- and operational reasoning becomes unreliable

---

# Kubernetes Perspective

Kubernetes abstracts infrastructure aggressively.

Workloads continuously:
- move across nodes
- scale dynamically
- and share infrastructure resources

Infrastructure visibility becomes critical for understanding:
- workload placement
- saturation behavior
- and resource pressure

inside Kubernetes environments.

---

# Operational Visibility

Infrastructure telemetry helps engineers:
- detect saturation
- identify unstable nodes
- correlate infrastructure failures
- and predict degradation

before outages occur.

---

# Reliability Engineering

Reliable systems depend heavily on:
- infrastructure stability
- operational visibility
- and saturation awareness

Observability systems expose:
- node behavior
- infrastructure pressure
- and operational bottlenecks

during production conditions.

---

# Key Insight

Infrastructure visibility exposes the operational conditions that distributed systems ultimately depend on for reliability.
