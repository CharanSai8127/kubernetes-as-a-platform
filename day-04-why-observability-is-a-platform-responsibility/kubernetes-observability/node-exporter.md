# node-exporter

Distributed systems ultimately depend on infrastructure behavior.

Even when applications appear healthy, infrastructure instability may still introduce:
- latency
- saturation
- resource exhaustion
- and operational degradation

node-exporter exposes infrastructure-level telemetry from Kubernetes nodes.

---

# What node-exporter Provides

node-exporter exposes metrics related to:
- CPU usage
- memory pressure
- filesystem utilization
- network behavior
- and system load

This helps engineers understand infrastructure conditions affecting workloads.

---

# Why Infrastructure Visibility Matters

Infrastructure instability often propagates toward:
- applications
- networking
- scaling behavior
- and distributed-system reliability

Examples include:
- node pressure
- memory exhaustion
- disk saturation
- and networking bottlenecks

Without infrastructure visibility:
- debugging becomes incomplete
- and operational reasoning becomes unreliable

---

# Kubernetes Perspective

Kubernetes abstracts infrastructure aggressively.

Workloads continuously:
- move across nodes
- scale dynamically
- and share infrastructure resources

node-exporter helps expose:
- node health
- infrastructure pressure
- and saturation behavior

inside Kubernetes environments.

---

# Operational Visibility

Infrastructure telemetry helps engineers:
- identify saturation
- understand workload placement pressure
- correlate node instability
- and predict operational degradation

before outages occur.

---

# Reliability Engineering

Reliable systems depend heavily on:
- infrastructure stability
- operational visibility
- and saturation awareness

node-exporter improves:
- infrastructure debugging
- failure correlation
- and operational reasoning

during production failures.

---

# Key Insight

node-exporter exposes infrastructure behavior as operational telemetry for understanding distributed-system stability under production pressure.
