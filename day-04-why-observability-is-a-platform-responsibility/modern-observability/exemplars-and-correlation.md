# Exemplars and Correlation

Modern observability increasingly focuses on improving telemetry correlation across distributed systems.

Exemplars help connect:
- metrics
- traces
- and operational events

into unified debugging workflows.

---

# Why Correlation Matters

Distributed systems generate fragmented telemetry continuously.

Without correlation:
- symptoms remain disconnected
- debugging slows
- and operational reasoning becomes difficult

Exemplars improve visibility between:
- latency spikes
- request traces
- and distributed-system behavior

during production failures.

---

# Operational Visibility

Metrics help expose:
- system pressure
- saturation
- and latency

Traces help expose:
- request flow
- dependency interactions
- and propagation behavior

Exemplars improve correlation between these operational signals.

---

# Kubernetes Perspective

Kubernetes environments continuously change operational state through:
- autoscaling
- workload movement
- and infrastructure reconciliation

Correlation becomes increasingly important for understanding:
- distributed-system behavior
- operational transitions
- and instability propagation

inside dynamic environments.

---

# Reliability Engineering

Reliable observability systems prioritize:
- operational context
- telemetry correlation
- and debugging clarity

Correlation improves:
- incident response
- recovery coordination
- and operational understanding

during production failures.

---

# Key Insight

Modern observability increasingly focuses on connecting operational signals together instead of collecting telemetry independently.
